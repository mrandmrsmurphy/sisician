# иже (izhe)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "Who, which" — the Church Slavonic relative pronoun, surviving in Sisician **only as a frozen liturgical fossil**: **Отче наш, иже си на небесима** "Our Father, who art in heaven." See [[../Texts/Pater Noster|Pater Noster]] and [[../Sisician|Sisician]].

## Meaning

"Who, which" — introduces a relative clause. In the prayer it is indeclinable in practice (a frozen form, agreeing with nothing it governs), and it is **not** the productive Sisician relative. The modern relative-clause system — [[../Words/који|који]]'s full oblique paradigm, the relativization strategy, [[../Words/куи|куи]]'s distribution — is the vault's flagged next grammar thread and remains open; иже is a museum piece from the liturgical layer, not a down payment on that system. Its presence is one of the three things that mark the prayer as a fossil register (see [[../Texts/Pater Noster|the prayer page]] § The Prayer as a Fossil Register).

## Etymology

**Old Church Slavonic иже** — the prayer's own word (иже еси на небесѣхъ). Composed of \*jь (the old anaphoric pronoun — the same root that supplies [[../Words/он|он]]'s entire oblique stem, per that page's double-root etymology) + the emphatic particle же. Imported with the liturgy and never nativized: it keeps its Slavonic shape and its Slavonic job, in this one text.

## Register

**church-slavonic-liturgical**, [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]] Layer III. Outside the prayer register it has no life at all — a Sisician speaker forming a relative clause uses the (still-to-be-built) modern system, not иже.
