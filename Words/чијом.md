# чијом (čijom)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Feminine singular instrumental of [[чији]]. See [[../Grammar/Relative Clauses|Grammar/Relative Clauses]]. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"With/by whose (feminine singular)?" — **каза, чијом сијам** "the dwelling by whose [light] I shine" — the instrumental of the possessed noun phrase. With the built prepositions: **за чијом** "after whose (f.)," **пред чијом** "before whose (f.)."

## Etymology

**чиј- stem + -ом**, the feminine instrumental of the established family (the pronoun ending, same shape as the demonstratives' [[../Words/том|том]]). No sound change applies; real BCS *čijom* matches.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, both uses productive.

## Related

[[чији]], [[чија]], [[чијој]] (dative). [[којом]] — the same cell on који.
