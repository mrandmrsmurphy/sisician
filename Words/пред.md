# пред (pred)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Static "in front of" with instrumental, motion "to in front of" with accusative; whence is [[../Words/испред|испред]]. See [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] for the system and [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

**+ instrumental** = "in front of" (where); **+ accusative** = "to in front of" (whither). The whence cell is the compound [[../Words/испред|испред]] + genitive, completing a where/whither/whence set (see [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] § Location Expressions). This alternation was never locative-based, so the locative's loss did not touch it.

## Government

| Case | Sense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Instrumental | in front of | **пред домом** "in front of the house" |
| Accusative | to in front of | **пред дом** "to in front of the house" |

## Etymology

Common Slavic **\*perdъ**, by regular South Slavic liquid metathesis (\*TerT → TreT) and loss of the weak final jer ([[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]]), matching BCS *pred*.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary — outside [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]]' three-layer model, the same footing as [[../Words/одовде|одовде]].
