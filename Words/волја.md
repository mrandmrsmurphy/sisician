# волја (volja)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "Will, volition" — part of the [[../Texts/Pater Noster|Pater Noster]] set: **да буде волја твоја** "let thy will be done." See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Will, volition" — the faculty of choosing, and what is chosen. Feminine e-declension. In the prayer it takes the feminine possessive **твоја** (unlike [[../Words/име|име]] and [[../Words/царство|царство]], which are old neuters and take masculine твој).

## Declension (e-declension, feminine)

Same pattern as [[../Words/каза|каза]] ([[../Grammar/Declension Classes|Declension Classes]]):

| Case | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| Nominative | волја | волје |
| Genitive | волје | волја |
| Dative | волји | волјама |
| Accusative | волју | волје |
| Instrumental | волјом | волјама |

**Genitive plural волја is syncretic with the nominative singular** — the same shape [[../Words/каза|каза]] already shows in this cell, kept here as inherited texture (real BCS *volja* has the same genitive plural). Stress is root-anchored on во́-.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*voľà**, from Proto-Balto-Slavic \*wáljāˀ, PIE \*welh₁- "to wish, want" ([Wiktionary воља](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D0%B2%D0%BE%D1%99%D0%B0), fetched via the Serbian entry, which cites the reconstruction directly). The only process is the ordinary \*lj → љ development already behind [[../Words/земља|земља]] and [[../Words/сишчани|сишчани]]'s own palatal outcomes. Real BCS *volja* and Slovene *volja* are identical — no fork. The initial в is bilabial [β] in every position ([[../Phonology/Modern Inventory|Modern Inventory]] § в/ф): /ˈβoʎa/.

## Register

Native, unmarked, [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]] Layer I (ordinary abstract vocabulary). The prayer puts it to theological use without any register shift in the word itself.
