# васходити (vashoditi)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Coined for [[../Texts/Conlang Syntax Test Cases|Conlang Syntax Test Cases]] item 10 ("The sun is rising now"), on the user's proposal: OCS **въсходити**, compare Slovene noun *vzhod* "sunrise, east." See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"To rise, to ascend," above all of the sun, moon and stars coming up over the horizon. Imperfective, so **ел сунце васходи** is "the sun is rising" (ongoing), not "the sun rises (once, completely)". A perfective partner is not coined.

## Conjugation (i-type, present tense)

Same class as [[../Grammar/Verb Paradigm (Sketch)|радити]]. Stress is root-anchored on the root's own syllable, хо-, not on the prefix:

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | васхо́дим | васхо́димо |
| 2nd | васхо́диш | васхо́дите |
| 3rd | васхо́ди | васхо́де |

## Imperative

Consonant-final stem, so **-и / -имо / -ите** ([[../Grammar/Imperative|Imperative]]): **васхо́ди**, **васхо́димо**, **васхо́дите**. The jussive is **не́ка** + present, and the negative is **немо́ј да** + present, as for every verb.

## Verbal Noun: васхоџење

I-type rule ([[../Grammar/Verbal Categories|Verbal Categories]] § Concrete Formation): the thematic -i- jotates the root-final д before -ње, and д+ј goes to џ, as in раџење. Gives **васхоџење** "rising, ascent." The noun for the event "sunrise" is a separate matter, see Open Points.

## Past Tense (l-participle + the three-way evidential contrast)

L-participle, i-type, same as радио: **васходио / васходила / васходили / васходиле**. 3rd-person contrast ([[../Grammar/Past Tense Fusion and Evidentiality|Past Tense Fusion and Evidentiality]]):

| Form | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| Conservative | је васходио / је васходила | су васходили / су васходиле |
| Fused (witnessed) | вја̀сходио / вја̀сходила | сва̀сходили / сва̀сходиле |
| Dropped (hearsay) | васходио / васходила | васходили / васходиле |

The fused forms are built by direct analogy with рја̀дио and сра̀дили (је's ј metathesizes behind the initial consonant, су reduces to с- before it). They are derived, not independently checked.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*vъz- "up, back"** (prefix) + **\*xoditi "to go, walk"**, from \*xodъ "motion," itself from PIE \*sod- "road, course" ([Wiktionary \*xoditi](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Reconstruction:Proto-Slavic/xoditi), an i-verb; its entry lists \*jьzxoditi, \*naxoditi and others as prefixed derivatives). The prefix: [Wiktionary \*vъz-](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Reconstruction:Proto-Slavic/vъz-), which lists Serbo-Croatian уз-, plus OCS-derived **ваз-/вас-** alongside it. The noun \*vъzxodъ gives Slovene *vzhod* "rise (of a celestial body); east" ([Wiktionary vzhod](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/vzhod)). Before voiceless х, the prefix's з devoices to с: \*vъs-xoditi, the OCS form **въсходити**.

**Sisician outcome — a design choice, flagged.** Applying [[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]] mechanically, the prefix jer is the rightmost jer in the word, hence weak, and drops: \*vъsxoditi → **всходити**, with an initial всх- cluster (real Slovene *vzhod*, Russian *всходить*). I did not use that. Instead the prefix keeps its jer as **a**, the way real Serbo-Croatian Church Slavonicisms do (*vaskrs*, *vazduh*, *vaznesenje*, from the same \*vъz-). That follows the user's OCS starting point, avoids an onset cluster the vault's distancing thread dislikes, and fits the verb's literary, solar-and-liturgical register. It's an extension of the real ваз-/вас- pattern, not a fresh citation for this particular verb.

Then х surfaces as ç ([[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § 11) and the д between vowels as [ð] (§ 12), giving /vasˈçoðiti/.

## Register

Native but literary, with a Church Slavonic tint from the prefix (compare the plain Layer I verbs like [[../Words/сијати|сијати]]). It is the ordinary word for the sun rising; a homier \*izlaziti type is not coined.

## Open Points

- **The prefix fork:** васходити (chosen) vs. **всходити** (mechanical jer fall) vs. **усходити** (the plain Serbo-Croatian уз- reflex, with devoicing).
- **A perfective partner** (the "rise once" verb, Russian *взойти* type) is not coined.
- **The noun for "sunrise"** (Slovene *vzhod*, from \*vъzxodъ) is not coined; васхоџење is only the verbal noun.
- **Root stress vs. prefix stress** on prefixed verbs has no prior precedent in the vault. I used root-anchored stress.
