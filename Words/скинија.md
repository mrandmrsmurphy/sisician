# скинија (skinija)

**Status: exploratory/proposed, more tentative than its siblings — see note at bottom.** Part of the [[дом]] / [[каза]] / [[скинија]] trio illustrating the [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|three-layer lexical model]] for "home/house." See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

Not a generic word for "house" — there may or may not be a plain Greek-derived word for that (an "oikos word," from Greek οἶκος "house/household"). This is instead a specifically **theological** term: the dwelling of God with humanity. Register has no real English counterpart — "theological but intimate, mystical but personal" (the user's own framing): not a building, not an institution, but presence, nearness, indwelling.

## Etymology

Greek **σκηνή** (*skēnē*) "tent, tabernacle, dwelling-place" — the word behind two resonant biblical passages: **Revelation 21:3**, "the tabernacle (σκηνή) of God is with men," and **John 1:14**, "the Word became flesh and *skēnē*-d [ἐσκήνωσεν, *eskēnōsen*, "tabernacled/pitched-tent-among"] us." Real Church Slavonic already has this as a direct, attested loan: **скиния** (*skinija*) "tabernacle" — still the standard Church Slavonic/Orthodox liturgical term. Per [[../Sources/Old Church Slavonic|Sources/Old Church Slavonic]]'s established role, Sisician imports its Greek/theological vocabulary *through* Church Slavonic rather than directly from spoken Greek — so the proposed path is **Greek σκηνή → (real) Church Slavonic скиния → Sisician скинија**, entering close to unchanged, as befits the most foreign/learned register (see [[../Culture/Religion|Culture/Religion]] § The Church as a Stabilizer: "Slavonic/Greek for heaven").

## Register

**Greek/Church Slavonic, liturgical, the third register with no English parallel.** Belongs to [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Etymology/Semantic Domains]]'s "Greek/Church Slavonic" layer — theology, Scripture. Distinct from both [[дом]] (warm/native) and [[каза]] (formal/secular): this word carries neither of those flavors, only a devotional one.

## Related

- [[дом]] — the native, warm, everyday counterpart
- [[каза]] — the Dalmatian-derived formal, secular counterpart
- [[икос]] — the Greek-derived (direct, not via Church Slavonic) "household/community" counterpart — the two together show that even one semantic layer can split into distinct pathways (via OCS vs. direct Greek contact)

---

**Note on status — more open than [[дом]] or [[каза]]:** this is a *proposed direction*, not a settled derivation. Two things are unconfirmed: (1) whether Sisician takes the Church Slavonic form essentially unchanged (скинија) or adapts its ending to match native noun classes, and (2) whether this is even the right concept to reach for — the user explicitly floated that a plainer Greek "oikos"-type house-word might also exist alongside this one, rather than this being the only Greek-layer entry for "dwelling." Held loosely pending confirmation.
