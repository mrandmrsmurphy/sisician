# широк (širok)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "Wide, broad," filling [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Etymology/Swadesh List]] item 29, from the Proto-Slavic adjective \*širokъ at the user's direction. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Wide, broad": **широк пут** "a wide road," **широка река** "a broad river," **широки домови** "wide houses." The measure-of-breadth word, opposite in use to the (not yet coined) "narrow" of item 34.

**Adverbial use: the masculine singular, unchanged**, per [[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]] § Adjective to Adverb: **широк** is also "widely."

## Forms

Gender and number agreement only, like every adjective ([[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]] § The Modern System):

| | Masculine | Feminine |
|---|---|---|
| Singular | широк | широка |
| Plural | широки | широке |

No case and no definiteness. A noun that is old-neuter-plural takes the feminine plural, by the neuter merger. Stress is root-anchored on the first syllable, **ши-**, throughout.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*širokъ** "wide," a hard adjective, formed from \*širь "scope, vast" plus the suffix \*-okъ; related to \*širъ "vast, broad" ([Wiktionary \*širokъ](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Reconstruction:Proto-Slavic/širokъ)). The page gives no deeper PIE or Baltic source. Descendants listed there: OCS широкъ, Serbo-Croatian широк, Slovene širok, Macedonian and Bulgarian широк, Russian широкий, Polish szeroki, Czech široký.

**Sisician derivation, one step:**

1. **Fall of the Jers** ([[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]]): the only jer is the word-final ъ, which is weak and drops: \*širokъ → **широк**.

Nothing else applies. The initial *š* is already in the inventory and no change affects it; the к is never between vowels in a way § 12 covers (that rule is б, д, г only); there is no л and no cluster. The result is identical to Serbo-Croatian, Slovene, Macedonian and Bulgarian.

## Register

Native, unmarked, [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]] Layer I.

## Open Points

- **Dalmatian competitor.** The Swadesh row shows Dalmatian *luarg* "wide" (also the second Dalmatian word for "big," item 27). Not borrowed; широк stays native. "Big" and "wide" are separate words here ([[../Words/велик|велик]], широк), so that Dalmatian overlap isn't reproduced.
- **The comparative** isn't built.
- **The derived noun** \*širota "width" and the verb "to widen" (\*širiti) aren't coined, and Wiktionary's \*širokъ page does not list them.
