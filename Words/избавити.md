# избавити (izbaviti)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "To deliver, rescue, free" — part of the [[../Texts/Pater Noster|Pater Noster]] set: **но избави нас од зла** "but deliver us from evil." See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"To deliver, rescue, set free" — perfective: one bounded act of rescue, taking the rescued person in the accusative and the threat in the genitive after [[../Words/од|од]] (од зла, "from evil"). The prayer's final verb.

Stress on ба́-: /izˈbaβiti/ — the б is preceded by з (not intervocalic), so it stays a stop; the в is bilabial [β] as always.

## Etymology

**из- + \*baviti** — the prefix is the ordinary native из- "out of"; the root \*baviti is recalled general Slavic (real BCS *izbaviti* is the living comparandum, matching cell for cell), flagged as not freshly cited — the root's own deeper history ("to talk, to protect") is not re-derived here. No sound change applies beyond the prefix's ordinary shape.

## Register

Native formation; the "deliver from evil" use is the liturgical application of an ordinary rescue verb.
