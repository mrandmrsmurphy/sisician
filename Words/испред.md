# испред (ispred)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The whence member of the [[../Words/пред|пред]] set: "from in front of." See [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] for the system and [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"From in front of" — motion away from that position, **whence only**. Real BCS's cognate has bleached into a plain static "in front of"; Sisician keeps the older source meaning, as Russian's из- compounds do (see [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] § Location Expressions). Static "in front of" is [[../Words/пред|пред]] + instrumental.

## Government

| Case | Sense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Genitive | from in front of | **испред дома** "from in front of the house" |

## Etymology

[[../Words/из|из]] + [[../Words/пред|пред]], with regressive devoicing of з to с before п. The form matches real BCS; the meaning is deliberately the conservative, source-only one.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary — outside [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]]' three-layer model, the same footing as [[../Words/одовде|одовде]].
