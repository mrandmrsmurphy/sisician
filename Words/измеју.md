# измеју (izmeju)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The whence member of the [[../Words/меју|меју]] set: "from among, from between." See [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] for the system and [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"From among, from between" — motion away from that position, **whence only**. Real BCS's cognate has bleached into a plain static "among, from between"; Sisician keeps the older source meaning, as Russian's из- compounds do (see [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] § Location Expressions). Static "among, from between" is [[../Words/меју|меју]] + instrumental.

## Government

## Etymology

[[../Words/из|из]] + [[../Words/меју|меју]], unchanged. The form matches real BCS; the meaning is deliberately the conservative, source-only one.
