# чију (čiju)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Feminine singular accusative of [[чији]]. See [[../Grammar/Relative Clauses|Grammar/Relative Clauses]]. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Whose (feminine singular, object role)?" — accusative of the possessed noun with чију marking it: **даожник, чију казу сијам** "the debtor whose house I shine upon." The feminine accusative is always чију; the animate/inanimate split is masculine-only, as everywhere in the pronoun system.

## Etymology

**чиј- stem + -у**, the feminine accusative of the established ending family (same -у as [[../Words/коју|коју]], [[../Words/ту|ту]], [[../Words/њу|њу]]). No sound change applies; real BCS *čiju* matches.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, interrogative and relative uses both productive.
