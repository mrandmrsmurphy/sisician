# чији (čiji)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "Whose" — the possessive interrogative/relative, the ч-root twin of [[који]]'s к-root, built as part of the [[../Grammar/Relative Clauses|Relative Clauses]] thread. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Whose?" — interrogative (**Чији је дом?** "Whose house is it?") and relative (**даожник чији дом је велик** "the debtor whose house is big"). **чији agrees with the possessed noun** in gender, number, and case — the possessor relation to the antecedent is genitive in force — and its paradigm is [[који]]'s exactly, including the short/long register doublet:

| Case | M. sg. | F. sg. | M. pl. | F. pl. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nominative | чији | чија | чији | чије |
| Genitive | **чега** / **чијега** | чије | чијих | чијих |
| Dative | **чему** / **чијему** | чијој | чијим | чијим |
| Accusative | чега (anim.) / чији (inan.) | чију | чијих (anim.) / чије (inan.) | чије |
| Instrumental | чијим | чијом | чијим | чијим |

The short obliques **чега/чему** are the very forms [[кај]]'s paradigm borrowed from the *čьto family — same ч- root, same formation, one oblique family shared by "what" and "whose" (see the homograph notes on those pages).

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*čьjь** — stated directly on the [Wiktionary чији entry](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D1%87%D0%B8%D1%98%D0%B8) (fetched), which confirms real BCS *čiji* as interrogative "whose," relative "whose," and indefinite "anyone's" alike, and lists its derived indefinites (*nȅčiji*, *bilo čiji* "whosever"). The structure is [[који]]'s: bare root (\*čь-, the *čьto* family) plus the same "further suffixation" whose internal make-up [[који]]'s own page flags as not fully pinned down — the к/ч root pairing runs through the whole interrogative system (*kъto/*čьto, *kъjь/*čьjь). No sound change applies beyond the inherited shape.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary; the indefinite senses ("anyone's," "whosever") are real but not yet built for Sisician — same standing status as the "whatever/whoever" item on [[../Grammar/Interrogatives|Interrogatives]].
