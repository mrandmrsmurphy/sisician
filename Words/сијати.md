# сијати (sijati)

**Status: exploratory/tentative — the vault's first genuinely native *lexical* verb `Words/` page.** [[../Words/респонџати|респонџати]] was the first verb page overall but is a Dalmatian loanword, and [[../Words/бити|бити]] is a suppletive grammatical auxiliary rather than an ordinary content verb — радити itself, used throughout [[../Grammar/Verb Paradigm (Sketch)|Verb Paradigm (Sketch)]] as the i-type example, has never had its own page either. This is the first plain native verb naming an ordinary action/state. Directly answers [[../Words/сунце|сунце]]'s own opening line, which flagged [[../Texts/Conlang Syntax Test Cases|Texts/Conlang Syntax Test Cases]] item 1 ("The sun shines") as having a subject but no verb yet — see § Related below for the now-translated sentence. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"To shine, to glow, to give off light" — of the sun, stars, fire, or any light source; also figuratively (a shining face, a shining reputation), the same metaphorical extension real Croatian/Slovene *sjati*/*sijati* and Russian *сиять* all make. Pairs naturally with [[../Words/сунце|сунце]] as its most basic real-world subject.

## Conjugation (a-type, present tense)

Following [[../Grammar/Verb Paradigm (Sketch)|Verb Paradigm (Sketch)]]'s гледати model — the root **сија-** is vowel-final throughout (no thematic-vowel contraction, see § Etymology), so the paradigm is completely regular with no irregularities to flag:

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | си́јам | си́јамо |
| 2nd | си́јаш | си́јате |
| 3rd | си́ја | си́јају |

**Stress root-anchored on the first root syllable си-**, matching радити's own precedent (ра́дити, not †ради́ти) for a multisyllabic native root — not the Latin-inherited exception [[../Words/респонџати|респонџати]] shows (стрес on -спон-, matching that word's own Latin accentuation instead).

## Imperative (built the same day, per [[../Grammar/Imperative|Grammar/Imperative]])

Vowel-final a-type stem, same allomorph as гледати/[[../Words/респонџати|респонџати]]:

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| 2nd | си́јај | си́јајте |
| 1st | — | си́јајмо |

1sg/3sg/3pl are periphrastic (**не́ка си́јам**, **не́ка си́ја**, **не́ка си́јају**), and the negative imperative is the suppletive немој + да (**немо́ј да си́јаш**) — a slightly unusual pragmatic case (commanding the sun not to shine is folklore/poetic register, not everyday speech), but the grammar itself is a plain application of already-decided rules.

## Verbal Noun: сијање

Per [[../Grammar/Verbal Categories|Verbal Categories]] § "Concrete Formation and Worked Examples": vowel-final a-type stem takes **-ње** directly, no jotation — **сија + ње → сијање**, "shining, radiance, the act of shining." The general **-ње** allomorph, not [[../Words/бити|биti]]'s special **-че** (that one is reserved for roots ending in a vowel immediately before infinitive *-ти*, a narrower shape сијати doesn't share despite superficially resembling биti). Real Croatian more idiomatically zero-derives **sjaj** "shine, glow, radiance" for this concept (parallel to [[../Words/респонџ|респонџ]]'s own zero-derivation from респонџати) rather than favoring the *-nje* form — noted as a real alternative word-formation path, not pursued here since it wasn't asked for.

## Past Tense (l-participle + the three-way evidential contrast, built 2026-09-25)

**L-participle**, a-type, same mechanism as гледати/[[../Words/респонџати|респонџати]]:

| | Masculine (sg.) | Feminine (sg.) | Masculine (pl.) | Feminine (pl.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| l-participle | сијао | сијала | сијали | сијале |

| Form | Masculine | Feminine |
|---|---|---|
| Conservative, singular | је сијао | је сијала |
| Conservative, plural | су сијали | су сијале |
| Fused (witnessed), singular | сјѝјао | сјѝјала |
| Fused (witnessed), plural | сисѝјали | сисѝјале |
| Dropped (hearsay), singular | сијао | сијала |
| Dropped (hearsay), plural | сијали | сијале |

This word's own /s/-initial root turned out to matter twice over. The **singular** surfaced a real correction: је+сијао's /j/+/s/ is the same shape of Sonority Sequencing violation [[../Grammar/Past Tense Fusion and Evidentiality|радити's /j/+/r/]] already needed metathesis for — glides outrank obstruents even more steeply than they outrank liquids — so it repairs to **сјѝјао**, and working this out is what caught (and corrected) [[../Words/бити|бити]]'s own је+био, previously and wrongly treated as an exception. The **plural** hits a genuinely different problem no other verb has: су+сијали prefixes bare /s/ onto a root that *also* starts with /s/, a true geminate that metathesis can't fix (there's nothing to reorder). Sisician resolves it with a new, second repair — copy-vowel epenthesis, breaking the geminate with a copy of the root's own stressed vowel: **сисѝјали**. See [[../Grammar/Past Tense Fusion and Evidentiality|Past Tense Fusion and Evidentiality]] § "Third Worked Example" and [[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § "Geminate-Breaking Copy-Vowel Epenthesis" for the full account of both.

## Etymology

**Common Slavic \*sijati, inherited directly — the phonologically simplest derivation in the vault so far.** No [[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]] (the root has no yer at all), no yat reflex (the root vowel is a real inherited PIE high front vowel, not a *ě*), no liquid metathesis, no denasalization — real Wiktionary's own Proto-Slavic reconstruction gives the headword as **\*sijati** with a plain vowel, not a yer-containing *\*sьjati*, confirmed via search this session. Ultimately from **PIE \*(s)ḱeh₁y-** "to shine," the same root behind Proto-Germanic *\*skīnaną* — real English **shine** is a distant cognate.

**A genuine fork exists across the real daughter languages, and Sisician deliberately takes the Slovene/Russian side of it.** Real Croatian contracts the root to **sjati** (the stem vowel *i* is lost), while Slovene **sijati** and Russian **сиять** (transliterated *sijatʹ*) keep it. Sisician's **сијати** follows the fuller, uncontracted shape — consistent with [[../Words/штири|штири]]'s own already-established precedent of leaning Slovene where [[../Sources/Late Common Slavic|Late Common Slavic]]'s Kajkavian base gives a real choice, not a fresh departure invented for this word.

**A potential collision checked and found not to apply.** Real modern Standard Croatian's own **sijati** actually means "to sow" (a *different* verb, from *\*sěti*) — a real homograph with "to shine" that standard Croatian's own contraction to *sjati* conveniently sidesteps. Sisician doesn't need the same escape: [[../Sources/Late Common Slavic|Late Common Slavic]]'s already-decided **Ekavian-type yat reflex** (ě→e, not Ijekavian's je/ije) means a future Sisician "to sow" — if built from the same *\*sěti* root — would surface with an **e-**, not an **i-**, shape (something like **сети**), keeping it distinct from **сијати** automatically. Checked before finalizing this word, per this vault's own standing consistency-checking priority, not discovered by accident.

## Register

Native, unmarked, [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]] Layer I ("Slavic Earth") — ordinary vocabulary for a natural phenomenon, the same register tier as [[../Words/сунце|сунце]] and [[../Words/дом|дом]]. No Dalmatian or Greek/Church-Slavonic doublet exists or is proposed.

The Slovene-leaning choice to keep the full **сија-** stem (rather than contracting to a Croatian-style **сја-**) is a deliberate design pick with vault precedent ([[../Words/штири|штири]]), not a citation of a single "correct" outcome — real Slavic genuinely varies here.
