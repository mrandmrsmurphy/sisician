# и (i)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "And" — the ordinary coordinator, appearing three times in the [[../Texts/Pater Noster|Pater Noster]] (како на небом **и** на земљом; **и** отпусти нам...; **и** не веди нас...). See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"And" — coordinating conjunction, proclitic and unstressed. About as bedrock as vocabulary gets.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*i** "and" — from PIE \*h₁ey, an old locative-singular determiner ([Wiktionary \*i](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Reconstruction:Proto-Slavic/i), fetched; "and" across the entire family: OCS и, BCS и, Slovene *in/i*, Czech *i*, Polish *i*). No sound change applies; none could.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, outside the three-layer model.
