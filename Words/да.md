# да (da)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The complementizer **да** — "that," governing a finite clause — and, in the [[../Texts/Pater Noster|Pater Noster]], a **frozen liturgical jussive**: да се свети / да доје / да буде, "let it be hallowed / let it come / let it be done." See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

Two uses, one word:

1. **Complementizer** (productive): да + finite clause, the modern outcome of the infinitive's recession — the whole system is documented on [[../Grammar/Infinitive Loss and Da-Complementation|Infinitive Loss and Da-Complementation]], and the future tense already uses it (ел сунце че да сија).
2. **Jussive** (frozen, liturgical): да + present in the prayer's three "let" clauses. The **productive** vernacular jussive is **нека** ([[../Grammar/Imperative|Imperative]]), and the two particles are deliberately distinct there — да is the general complementizer, нека the jussive. The prayer's да-jussive is a fossil of the older system, kept because the Slavonic text itself uses да (да свѧтитъ, да придетъ, да будетъ); real liturgical texts preserve older grammar against the productive system exactly this way (the real BCS prayer's own *ne vodi nas* does the same for the negative imperative). See [[../Texts/Pater Noster|the prayer page]] § The Prayer as a Fossil Register.

## Etymology

**PIE \*do-**, a demonstrative root — paralleling English "that" — traced on [[../Grammar/Infinitive Loss and Da-Complementation|the Da-Complementation page]] rather than re-derived here. No sound change applies.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary; the jussive use is liturgical-fossil only.
