# с (s)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Two prepositions' worth of meaning in one word: comitative "with" and the whence partner of [[../Words/на|на]]. See [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] for the system and [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

**+ instrumental** = "with," accompaniment — and the comitative of the Plural Pronoun Construction: **ми с њим** is either "we [a group] with him" or "he and I," plural verb either way (see [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] § The Comitative Plural). **+ genitive** = "off of, down from" — motion away from a surface, the whence counterpart of на.

**Allomorph са** before с, з, ш, ж and before clusters that would otherwise be hard to pronounce (real BCS *sa*): **са сунцем** "with the sun" ([[../Words/сунце|сунце]]).

## Government

| Case | Sense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Instrumental | with (accompaniment) | **с њим** "with him"; **с њом** "with me" or "with her" |
| Genitive | off of, down from | **с казе** "off the building" |

## Etymology

Common Slavic **\*sъ(n)**, from PIE **\*ḱom** — the same root as Latin **cum**. One lexeme whose government varies by sense, not two merged homophones. The weak final jer drops ([[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]]). Its old final -n (\*sъn) is one source of the prothetic н- on њега/њему/њим — see [[../Words/он|он]] § Etymology.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary — outside [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]]' three-layer model, the same footing as [[../Words/одовде|одовде]].
