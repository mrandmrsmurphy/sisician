# њом (njom)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The instrumental cell of [[она]]'s paradigm — with this page, every cell of [[../Grammar/Personal Pronouns|Personal Pronouns]]'s full table has its own built page.

"With her" — no clitic form exists for this cell, matching every other instrumental built in this set.

## Etymology — Confirming, Not Reopening, њој's Own Hypothesis

**The prothetic-n mechanism is [[он]]'s own** (see that page), applied here the same way it was applied to every other feminine oblique cell.

**Fetched the *jь* feminine singular declension (already used for [[ње]]'s and [[њу]]'s builds) for its instrumental cell:** it gives **\*jejǫ** — built on the same *-e-* vowel color as dative *\*jeji*, not the *-o-* of [[тобом]]'s (and this word's own 1st-person sense's) masculine/neuter *-ojǫ*.

**This word is where [[њој]]'s own hypothesis actually gets tested, not where a fresh puzzle starts.** [[њој]]'s page already flagged, honestly and explicitly, that dative *\*jeji* → њој's real *o*-vowel has no documented sound-law explanation, and offered — as a plausible, uncited hypothesis rather than a finding — that dative and instrumental might have leveled together as a pair, since both cells end up *o*-colored in the modern outcome (њој, њом) while genitive [[ње]] and accusative [[њу]] don't. **This page's own fetch confirms the second half of that prediction directly**: instrumental *\*jejǫ*, like dative *\*jeji*, has an *e*-vowel in the reconstruction, yet real modern њом has *o* — the exact same unexplained shift, in the exact same direction, hitting exactly the two cells [[њој]]'s hypothesis singled out. This is real supporting evidence for analogical leveling between dative and instrumental specifically (not proof — the underlying *e→o* mechanism itself is still not documented by any citation reached in this session), but it's a meaningfully stronger position than [[њој]]'s page could take on its own with only one data point.

**The short story: this sense of њом did not get its own *-ojǫ*-style regular derivation the way [[тобом]] did.** тобом (and the 1st-person sense below) continue a real masculine/neuter *-ojǫ* ending via the ordinary sound law. Feminine *\*jejǫ* is a genuinely different historical ending, and њом's *o*-vowel is the same open question as њој's, not a coincidentally similar-looking but separately-explained development.

**Real Slavic comparanda:**

| Branch | Language | Form |
|---|---|---|
| South Slavic (liturgical) | Old Church Slavonic | ѥѭ (jejǫ) |
| South Slavic | Serbo-Croatian | njom |
| South Slavic | Slovene | njo (same vowel as its own accusative *njo*, per [[њу]]'s page — worth noting as a further Slovene-internal pattern, not investigated further here) |
| East Slavic | Russian | ей, ею (jej, jeju) — e-vowel, as reconstructed |
| West Slavic | Polish | nią |

**The same branch tension as [[њој]], now confirmed a second time in the same paradigm:** Russian keeps the expected *e*-vowel; Serbo-Croatian's *njom* is again the outlier. Sisician follows Serbo-Croatian, the same acknowledged (not resolved) branch choice as every other cell in [[она]]'s oblique paradigm.

## Second Sense: 1st Person Singular Instrumental, "With Me" (Formerly мном)

**њом also means "with me,"** the instrumental cell of [[јаз]]'s paradigm. It used to be a separate word, **мном**, until the word-initial /mn-/ cluster was removed (§ 14 of [[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]]), after which it came out identical to the 3sg feminine instrumental. Both senses are the same form, in the same case, and no instrumental clitic exists to tell them apart, so the person is read from context: the verb's own agreement, pro-drop, or an overt subject. This is the same kind of two-sense entry as [[ми]], and the user accepted the merger rather than repair it.

**Derivation of this sense**, from **\*mъnojǫ** (the instrumental row of the \*azъ declension table, as fetched for [[мене]]):

1. **Fall of the Jers:** the ъ is the rightmost jer, so weak: \*mъnojǫ → \*mnojǫ.
2. **\*-ojǫ → -om**, the regular masculine/neuter instrumental ending (the same one behind [[тобом]]): → *мном*. This is the real Serbo-Croatian outcome *mnom*, and the pre-merger Sisician form.
3. **Initial mn- → ɲ** (§ 14): *мном* → **њом**, /ɲom/.

Real comparanda for the pre-merger form: OCS мъною, Serbo-Croatian *mnom*, Slovene *mano*, Russian *мной*/*мною*, Polish *mną*. No citation explains the *-ojǫ→-om* change itself; it is bedrock general Slavic morphology (the productive ending of real *žena→ženom*), not a pronoun-specific irregularity.

**Worth noticing, but not claimed as an explanation:** this sense's *o*-vowel is regular, while the 3sg feminine sense's *o* (from \*jejǫ) is unexplained. Since they are now one word, the merger could have helped pull the feminine form toward *o*. That is a hypothesis in the same uncited tier as the dative/instrumental leveling above, and the dating of § 14 would have to precede it.

## Register

Same closed-class status as [[она]]. No clitic exists for this cell, so њом is used in every register and position.
