# који (koji)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Native "which" and the productive relative "who/that" — see [[../Grammar/Interrogatives|Grammar/Interrogatives]] § Which for why this doesn't collide with [[кај]] despite sharing its root, [[../Grammar/Relative Clauses|Grammar/Relative Clauses]] for the relativization strategy and this word's full paradigm, [[куи]] for the Latinate legal-register doublet, and [[иже]] for the liturgical fossil. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Which?" — interrogative and relative alike, adjective-shaped rather than a frozen pronoun like [[кто]]/[[кај]]. As a relative it agrees with its antecedent in gender and number (including the neuter merger: **ел сунце који сија** "the sun, which shines") while its case comes from its role inside the relative clause.

The short genitive/dative ([[кога]]/[[кому]]) are homographs of [[кто]]'s own obliques — one к- root, one form, two paradigms, the documented-homograph category already accepted for [[ти]]/[[те]]. The long forms ([[којега]]/[[којему]]) are the first lexical examples of the soft adjective endings -ега/-ему ([[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]]'s soft declension).

## Etymology

**Real Serbo-Croatian *koji* is confirmed via Wiktionary to descend from the identical Proto-Slavic root behind Kajkavian's own [[кај]], \*kъjь** (PIE \*kʷos + relative particle \*yos — [Wiktionary који](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D0%BA%D0%BE%D1%98%D0%B8), fetched), carrying further suffixation beyond the bare root that [[кај]]'s own frozen, invariant shape never took on. The exact internal make-up of that further material isn't pinned down here (flagged honestly rather than guessed at), but the outcome is clear and directly adopted: real modern *koji* → Sisician **који**, adjective-shaped rather than a frozen particle — the same "declining word" territory already occupied by [[тај]]/[[овај]]'s own paradigm. The oblique endings are the demonstrative family (-ога/-ому/-им/-их/-ој/-у/-ом), established via the \*tъ declension citation on [[../Words/тога|тога]]'s build.

## Register

Productive in every register — the ordinary interrogative "which" and the ordinary relative. The legal register uses [[куи]] instead; the liturgical fossil is [[иже]].

## Related

[[кај]] — the same ultimate root, frozen rather than further suffixed. [[куи]] — the Latinate doublet. [[иже]] — the liturgical fossil relative. [[тај]]/[[овај]] — the closest structural parallel (an adjective-shaped, jь-suffixed declining word). [[чији]] — the ч-root twin ("whose"), paradigm-identical.
