# који (koji)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Native "which" — see [[../Grammar/Interrogatives|Grammar/Interrogatives]] § Which for why this doesn't collide with [[кај]] despite sharing its root, and [[куи]] for the Latinate register doublet. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Which?" — an interrogative/relative determiner, adjective-shaped rather than a frozen pronoun like [[кто]]/[[кај]].

## Etymology

**Real Serbo-Croatian *koji* is confirmed via Wiktionary to descend from the identical Proto-Slavic root behind Kajkavian's own [[кај]], \*kъjь** — but *koji* carries further suffixation beyond the bare root that [[кај]]'s own frozen, invariant shape never took on. The exact internal make-up of that further material wasn't pinned down this session (flagged honestly rather than guessed at), but the outcome is clear and directly adopted: real modern *koji* → Sisician **који**, adjective-shaped rather than a frozen particle — the same "declining word" territory already occupied by [[тај]]/[[овај]]'s own paradigm.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, unmarked. Contrast [[куи]], the Dalmatian-Latin-flavored formal/legal-register synonym.

## Related

[[кај]] — the same ultimate root, frozen rather than further suffixed. [[куи]] — the Latinate doublet. [[тај]]/[[овај]] — the closest structural parallel (an adjective-shaped, jь-suffixed declining word).
