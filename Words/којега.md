# којега (kojega)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The **long-register** masculine singular genitive (and animate accusative) of [[који]] — one half of the short/long doublet the paradigm carries, copied from real BCS *koga~kojega*. See [[../Grammar/Relative Clauses|Grammar/Relative Clauses]]. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Of which / whom (masculine singular)?" — the careful, written-register counterpart of colloquial [[кога]]. Same cells: genitive proper, and animate accusative by the usual rule. The register split is real BCS texture (long forms dominate careful speech and writing; short forms dominate colloquial speech) — not a meaning difference.

## Etymology

**кој- stem + -ега** — the soft-stem adjective long-form genitive ([[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]]'s soft declension), making којега/[[којему]] the **first lexical examples of the -ега/-ему pair**, defined since that page was built but never yet instantiated by a word. The г lenites intervocalically ([[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § 12): /ˈkojeɣa/. Real BCS *kojega* matches in shape and register behavior.

## Register

The careful/written half of the doublet; colloquial registers use [[кога]] instead.

## Related

[[кога]] (short counterpart), [[којему]] (long dative), [[који]] (nominative). The -ега ending: [[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]]'s soft declension, of which this is the first example.
