# нон (non)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** See [[../Grammar/Negation|Grammar/Negation]] for the full [[не]]/нон split and why it's a genuine Sisician-specific innovation, not an inheritance from either source language. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

Standalone/adjectival negation — the bare "no!" reply, and negative-predicate-adjective use. Contrast [[не]], the ordinary verbal negator. Real Croatian and Slovene use *ne* for this too; нон filling this specific slot instead is Sisician's own innovation.

## Etymology

**Latin NON, escaping [[../Sources/Dalmatian|Dalmatian]]'s own diphthongization rule via its proclitic status.** Classical Latin *nōn* has a genuinely long stressed ō (a real, documented contraction of *ne oinom* "not one thing") — in a closed syllable, exactly the environment that would trigger the established stressed-ē/ō diphthongization (→ *uo* → *wa*, the same mechanism behind [[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]]'s мварт example), giving an expected outcome like **†nvan**, not нон at all. **But that rule is explicitly stated as conditioned on the vowel being *stressed*, and NON — a preverbal proclitic in ordinary speech — never bears independent phrasal stress**, the same well-documented cross-linguistic pattern that exempts function words from sound changes their stressed lexical cognates undergo. Unstressed, NON's ō simply merges to plain /o/, giving Dalmatian **non** → Sisician **нон**.

**Real Campidanese Sardinian *nono* ("no") is the comparandum that suggested reaching for this word at all** — the same typological-model role Campidanese *dexi*/*noi* played for [[../Words/дешет|дешет]]/[[../Words/ној|ној]], not a claim that нон is a literal reduced copy of *nono* (its own internal Sardinian derivation wasn't found this session — flagged honestly rather than guessed at). **Doesn't replace or contradict real attested Dalmatian's own cited reflexes of NON** ([[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Etymology/Swadesh List]] item 16 already lists Dalmatian *на, наун*) — нон is a separate, additional Sisician coinage for one specific pragmatic slot, an aesthetic pick in the same category as [[../Words/штири|штири]] over четири.

**Why this specific word, of all things, got borrowed:** interjections and discourse particles are cross-linguistically *more* prone to prestige borrowing than almost any other word class — more so even than the core numeral [[../Words/ној|ној]] displaced. A standalone, emphatic "no!" is exactly the expressive, socially-marked slot where a Dalmatian-prestige alternative plausibly took root.

## Register

Dalmatian-formal/expressive, per frontmatter — unlike [[../Words/ној|ној]], нон doesn't fully generalize to displace не; it stays in its own pragmatic lane.

## Related

[[не]] — the native verbal negator. [[../Words/ној|ној]], [[../Words/дешет|дешет]] — the other Campidanese-comparandum coinages.
