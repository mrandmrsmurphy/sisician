# код (kod)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The productive "at X's place" preposition; whither partner [[../Words/к|к]], whence partner [[../Words/од|од]]. See [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] for the system and [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"At," split by case into two senses. **+ genitive** = at, within: at X's place, in X's household or premises (real BCS *kod kuće* "at home," *kod mene* "at my place"). **+ instrumental** = at, beside: physically next to. **+ accusative** = to beside. Real BCS *kod* + genitive covers both "within" and "beside" (*kod mosta* "by the bridge"); Sisician's split comes from analogy with the instrumental/accusative spatial prepositions — marked but universal (see [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] § код: Within vs. Beside). The open-class, productive member of the "at" pair; [[../Words/при|при]] is its idiom-bound counterpart.

## Government

| Case | Sense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Genitive | at, within (X's place) | **код дома** "at home"; **код нас** "at our place"; **код тебе** "at your place" |
| Instrumental | at, beside | **код домом** "next to the house"; **код тобом** "beside you" |
| Accusative | to beside | **код дом** "to beside the house"; **код њу** "to beside her" |

Genitive and accusative coincide for most pronouns and animate masculine nouns, so **код тебе** is also "to beside you" after a motion verb.

## Etymology

A compound: [[../Words/к|к]] + [[../Words/од|од]] (real BCS *kod* = *k* + *od*, per Wiktionary). Genitive government comes from од; instrumental and accusative are Sisician additions by analogy.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary — outside [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]]' three-layer model, the same footing as [[../Words/одовде|одовде]].
