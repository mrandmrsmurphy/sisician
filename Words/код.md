# код (kod)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The productive "at X's place" preposition; whither partner [[../Words/к|к]], whence partner [[../Words/од|од]]. See [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] for the system and [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"At, by, at someone's place" — location determined by a person or a prominent object, *near* rather than exactly *in* (real BCS *kod kuće* "at home," *kod mene* "at my place"). The open-class, productive member of the "at" pair; [[../Words/при|при]] is its idiom-bound counterpart.

## Government

| Case | Sense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Genitive | at, by, at X's place | **код нас** "at our place"; **код тебе** "at your place" |

## Etymology

A compound: [[../Words/к|к]] + [[../Words/од|од]] (real BCS *kod* = *k* + *od*, per Wiktionary). Genitive government comes from од.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary — outside [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]]' three-layer model, the same footing as [[../Words/одовде|одовде]].
