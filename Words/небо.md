# небо (nebo)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "Heaven, sky" — part of the [[../Texts/Pater Noster|Pater Noster]] set, appearing twice in the prayer: plural **на небесима** ("in the heavens," v. 1, matching the Slavonic's own plural на небесѣхъ) and singular **на небом** ("in heaven," v. 4, matching the Slavonic's singular на небеси). Fills [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Etymology/Swadesh List]] item 162. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Heaven, sky" — both the physical sky and the divine realm, one word covering both as in the Slavic neighbors. An old neuter noun: per [[../Grammar/Nominal Morphology|Nominal Morphology]]'s neuter merger, it takes **masculine singular** agreement and **feminine plural** agreement.

## Declension (a-declension, hard variant)

| Case | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| Nominative | небо | небеса |
| Genitive | неба | небеса |
| Dative | небу | небесима |
| Accusative | небо | небеса |
| Instrumental | небом | небесима |

- **The plural stem extends with -ес-** (небо → небеса), the old neuter collective plural with its own extension — real BCS *nebo* → *nebesa* shows the identical shape.
- **Genitive plural небеса is syncretic with nominative plural** (real BCS *nebesa* has the same syncretism). A [[../Words/сунце|сунце]]-style filler vowel (небесаца) would be available under the functional-load principle, but сунце's three-way syncretism (gen. pl. = gen. sg. = nom. pl.) was the stronger case; here genitive singular неба is distinct, so the simpler real-BCS-matching shape is kept. Flagged as a choice, not an oversight.
- **Hard stem**: б is not in the soft trigger set, so instrumental singular is -ом (небом), not -ем — unlike [[../Words/сунце|сунце]]'s сунцем.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*nebo** — already gathered on [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Swadesh List]] item 162 (OCS небо, Serbo-Croatian небо, Slovene *nebo*). **No sound change applies at all**: no jer, no nasal vowel, no cluster — the word comes through the whole chain untouched, one of the cleanest derivations in the vault. The Dalmatian cell on the same row (*cil*, < Latin *caelum*) was not borrowed.

The intervocalic б lenites to [β] in pronunciation ([[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § 12): /ˈnɛβo/.

## Register

Native, unmarked for the physical sky ([[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]] Layer I); the prayer's plural "heavens" and the theological "Kingdom of Heaven" usage are the liturgical register built on top of the ordinary word, not a separate lexeme.
