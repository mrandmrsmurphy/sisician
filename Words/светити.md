# светити (svetiti)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "To sanctify, hallow" — part of the [[../Texts/Pater Noster|Pater Noster]] set: **да се свети име твој** "let thy name be hallowed." See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"To sanctify, hallow" — make holy. In the prayer it appears in the **reflexive passive** use: **да се свети** "let it be hallowed" (the Slavonic's own да свѧтитъ сѧ). The root adjective **свет** "holy" is not yet coined; note the near-miss with [[../Words/светао|светао]] "bright" — a *different* root (\*světlъ), despite the surface similarity.

**Imperative** (consonant-final stem свет-, per [[../Grammar/Imperative|Imperative]]): свети / светимо / светите. **L-participle** (via [[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § 10): светио / светила / светили / светиле. Root-anchored stress on све́- throughout; the в is bilabial [β] in every position: /ˈsβetiti/.

## Etymology

**Old Church Slavonic свѧтити** — the prayer's own word (да свѧтитъ), with real BCS *svetiti* as the living vernacular comparandum; the underlying Proto-Slavic \*svętiti (from \*svętъ "holy") is recalled general knowledge, flagged as such rather than freshly cited. **Nativization:** the OCS ѧ reduces via the same denasalization as native ę→e ([[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § 2): свѧтити → **светити** — a loan that lands exactly on the shape the native word would have had, the same convergence the vault already accepts for liturgical loans that nativize cleanly.

## Register

**church-slavonic-liturgical** in origin and primary use, [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]] Layer III — but nativized enough to serve as the ordinary "sanctify" if the vernacular ever needs one.
