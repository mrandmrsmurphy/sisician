# одпустити (odpustiti)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "To forgive, remit" — part of the [[../Texts/Pater Noster|Pater Noster]] set: **и отпусти нам даоге наши** "and forgive us our debts." See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"To forgive, remit" — a debt, a sin, an offense. Perfective: one bounded act of forgiveness. The imperfective partner [[../Words/одпуштати|одпуштати]] is built alongside it (the prayer uses both: perfective imperative **одпусти** in v. 6a, imperfective **одпуштамо** in v. 6b). Built on \*pustiti "to let go" (unbuilt) with the prefix од-.

## Etymology

**\*otъ- + \*pustiti** — the root is [Wiktionary \*pustiti](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Reconstruction:Proto-Slavic/pustiti) (fetched: "to let go, let, send," imperfective \*puščati; OCS поустити, BCS *pùstiti*, Slovene *pustíti* all listed). "Forgive" = "let go [a debt]."

**The prefix is од-, not от-.** Real BCS *otpustiti* keeps the t; real Slovene *odpustiti* has the d. Sisician goes with the d, on two converging grounds: (1) the vault's own preposition [[../Words/од|од]] already generalized -d from \*otъ (by analogy with над/под/пред, per that page), and the verbal prefix is the same morpheme; (2) the Kajkavian/Slovene side of the fork is the vault's standing lean. The BCS *otpustiti* shape is flagged as the road not taken.

## Register

Native formation; the "forgive debts/sins" sense is the liturgical application of an ordinary verb.
