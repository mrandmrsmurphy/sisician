# седам (sedam)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** See [[../Grammar/Numerals|Grammar/Numerals]] for the full paradigm. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Seven." No gender distinction.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*sedmь → real Croatian sedam / real Slovene sedem — a genuine a/e vowel-quality divergence in the epenthetic vowel between *d* and *m*, uncited and unexplained even in the comparative source consulted** (the fetched declension data shows a related *sedme* oblique form but no clean statement of why the modern citation forms differ by vowel). **Croatian-shape adopted as the default**, the same honest "real, inherited, not independently derived" treatment already given to [[../Words/нама|нама]]'s own unexplained -a vowel — not a resolved derivation, an acknowledged gap.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, same status as every other numeral entry.
