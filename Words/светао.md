# светао (svetao)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Coined for [[../Texts/Conlang Syntax Test Cases|Conlang Syntax Test Cases]] item 8 ("The sun shines brightly"), and the vault's first adjective-to-adverb case. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Bright, shining, illustrious" — physical light, and by extension fame or distinction, as in real BCS *svijetao*. Both senses carry over to the adverb: **brightly**, **shiningly**, **illustriously**.

**Adverbial use: the adjective's own masculine singular short form, unchanged.** No suffix, no separate adverb. This is the Romanian strategy (*frumos* is both "beautiful" and "beautifully"), chosen because the neuter merger leaves no neuter form to serve as the Slavic-style adverb. See [[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]] § Adjective to Adverb.

| Feminine sg. | светла | светла |

The short-form paradigm has the same shape as the l-participle ([[../Words/сијати|сијао / сијала / сијали / сијале]]), because the cause is the same: the *l* vocalizes only where it ends up in coda position. The full long-form case paradigm is on [[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]], with [[../Words/нув|нув]] as the worked hard-stem example; светао declines the same way on the stem **светл-**.

## Etymology

**Common Slavic \*světlъ** ("bright, light"), inherited directly. Four steps, all already established:

- **Yat → e** (Ekavian): \*světlъ → *svetlъ*.
- **Final jer drops** ([[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]]): → *svetl*.
- **Mobile *a*** breaks the word-final -tl cluster ([[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]] § Mobile *a*, the same mechanism as [[../Words/један|један]]): → *светал*.
- **L-vocalization**, coda /l/ → /o/ ([[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § 10): → **светао**.

In светла, светли, светле the *l* is followed by a vowel and never vocalizes. This is real BCS *svijetao / svijetla*, with Ekavian *e* where Ijekavian has *ije*.
