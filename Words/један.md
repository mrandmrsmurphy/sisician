# један (jedan)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** First numeral entry — see [[../Grammar/Numerals|Grammar/Numerals]] for the full one-through-ten set. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"One," masculine. Per [[../Grammar/Nominal Morphology|Nominal Morphology]]'s neuter merger, covers old-neuter-singular referents too — the same landing site established for [[../Words/он|он]].

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*(j)edьnъ → један, the exact same mobile-*a* mechanism already modeled on [[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]] (*sъnъ→san/sna).** Two jers in the reconstructed form (ь, ъ): Havlík's Law counts from the right — final ъ is weak, drops; the remaining ь, now non-final, is strong, and vocalizes to the South Slavic outcome **a**. Result: **jed-a-n → један**, matching real Croatian *jedan* exactly (not derived independently — this is the same jer-count pattern already established, just a second word showing it). Real Slovene *eden* additionally drops the initial *j-*, a separate Slovene-specific development Kajkavian doesn't share; not adopted, matching this vault's standing 85%-BCS-baseline calibration.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary — no Slavic/Dalmatian/Greek stratification applies.
