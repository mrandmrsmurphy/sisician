# сада (sada)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "Now," coined for [[../Texts/Conlang Syntax Test Cases|Conlang Syntax Test Cases]] item 10 ("The sun is rising now"), from the Proto-Slavic etymon the user specified. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Now, at this moment." A bare adverb, no case or agreement.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*sьgъda** "now" ([Wiktionary's reconstruction page](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Reconstruction:Proto-Slavic/s%D1%8Cg%D1%8Ada)): \*sь "this" + \*-gъda, a temporal suffix the page doesn't explain, but which is the same one in \*kogъda ([[../Words/када|када]]), \*togъda, \*jegъda, \*vьśegъda and \*jьnogъda. \*sь itself goes back through Proto-Balto-Slavic \*śís to PIE \*ḱís "this," built on \*ís (demonstrative) + \*ḱe "here."

**Descendants listed there (South Slavic only):** OCS сьга, Bulgarian сега, Macedonian сега, Serbo-Croatian сада, Slovene zdaj. The split is the cluster: Bulgarian, Macedonian and OCS keep the g (-гд- → -г-), while Serbo-Croatian keeps the d (-гд- → -д-). Sisician follows Serbo-Croatian, as it did for када.

Sisician derivation, three steps, all already established:

1. **Fall of the Jers** ([[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]]): counting from the end, the last syllable has a full vowel, so the jer in \*-gъ- is weak and drops, while the jer in \*sь- is strong and becomes **a**: \*sьgъda → *сагда*.
2. **-гд- → -д-**, the same cluster simplification already applied to када: *сагда* → *сада*.
3. **Intervocalic lenition** ([[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § 12): the д surfaces as [ð], with no spelling change: /ˈsaða/.
