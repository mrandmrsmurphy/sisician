# како (kako)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "How" — see [[../Grammar/Interrogatives|Grammar/Interrogatives]]. Fills [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Etymology/Swadesh List]] item 15. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"How?"

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*kako → како directly** — trivial, no fork, both Croatian and Slovene already agree unmodified.

**A delightful coincidence, not a cognate:** real attested Dalmatian's own word for "how" is *kal* — strikingly close in shape to Sisician's како, but genuinely unrelated (Dalmatian *kal* descends from Latin *qualis* "of what kind," an entirely separate PIE-internal derivation from Slavic *kako*). Flagged as an aside worth noting, the same "coincidental convergence, not genealogy" category already used for a couple of other near-homophones in this vault (e.g. [[../Words/ова|ова]]/[[../Words/она|она]]), not a basis for merging the two words the way [[../Words/нув|нув]] did.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, no register coloring.
