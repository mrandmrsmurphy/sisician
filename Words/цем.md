# цем (cem)

**Status: exploratory/tentative — a deliberately kept archaism.** Instrumental of [[кто]] — see [[../Grammar/Interrogatives|Grammar/Interrogatives]] for the full paradigm. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"By whom?"/"with whom?" — instrumental of [[кто]]. **The one cell of this paradigm that doesn't follow the ко- stem every other oblique case uses.**

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*cěmь is a genuinely different root from *kogo/*komu — real, attested suppletion within the *kъto paradigm itself, confirmed via Wiktionary's own declension table, not an invented irregularity.** Via the already-established Ekavian yat reflex (ě→e), *cěmь → **цем** (final ь weak, drops). **Real modern Croatian regularized this away entirely**, leveling the instrumental to a uniform "kim" matching the rest of the paradigm — Sisician deliberately keeps the older, unleveled Common Slavic shape instead, the same "one cell resists regularization" texture English itself keeps in *who*/*whom*. This is the single clearest place in the whole interrogative system where a real historical case distinction survives as a fossil rather than being erased.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary — but carries a faint archaic/literary flavor by virtue of being the one form real colloquial speech elsewhere abandoned; not yet formally marked as a register distinction.

## Related

[[кто]] (nominative), [[кога]] (genitive/accusative), [[кому]] (dative) — цем's own paradigm siblings, all regular where цем alone isn't. [[чим]] — the parallel "what" instrumental, which stayed regular (no comparable suppletion on that side).
