# којих (kojikh)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Plural genitive of [[који]] — and, by the animate-accusative-uses-genitive rule, the animate plural accusative. See [[../Grammar/Relative Clauses|Grammar/Relative Clauses]]. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Of which (plural)?" — and, with an animate masculine plural antecedent, "whom" as a direct object: **даожници, којих видим** — "see" isn't built; the pattern stands in the paradigm: animate objects take којих, inanimate take које.

## Etymology

**кој- stem + -их**, the plural genitive of the established pronominal ending family. The х is the modern palatal /ç/ ([[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § 11), as in [[../Words/њих|њих]] /ˈɲiç/: **/ˈkojiç/**. No other change applies; real BCS *kojih* matches.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, both uses productive.

## Related

[[који]], [[које]] (feminine plural / inanimate accusative), [[којим]] (plural dat./instr.). [[../Words/њих|њих]] — the same -их ending and the same ç realization.
