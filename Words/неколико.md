# неколико (nekoliko)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "Some," filling [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Etymology/Swadesh List]] item 19 together with its Dalmatian counterpart [[../Words/кертиојн|кертиојн]] "certain." The user's design: Sisician splits English *some* the way English splits *some* from *certain*, and this is the **non-specific** half. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Some, several, a few": an unspecified quantity of countable things, with no particular ones in mind. **неколико домова** "some houses." It says how many in the loosest sense and nothing about *which*. For a specific referent the speaker has in mind but does not name ("a certain house"), see [[../Words/кертиојн|кертиојн]].

## Behavior

**Indeclinable, and the noun after it is in the genitive plural**, the same construction as real Serbo-Croatian, where [Wiktionary *nȅkoliko*](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/неколико) is labelled "with genitive" and glosses it "some, several, a few" (*неколико градова* "several cities," *неколико дана*).

| Phrase | Gloss |
|---|---|
| неколико домова | "some houses" |
| неколико каза | "some buildings" |

The quantifier does not change when the phrase is in another case; the noun's own case endings still apply, as with any noun ([[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]]).

## Etymology

**ne- + \*koliko.** The Serbo-Croatian entry gives it as a compound of *не-* and *колико* "how many/how much," and Sisician already has [[../Words/колико|колико]] from Proto-Slavic \*koliko. The form \*nekoliko is the one in the Swadesh table's Proto-Slavic column; I could not find a Wiktionary reconstruction page for it, so it is the compound read off the Serbo-Croatian entry, not a separately cited etymon.

**No sound change applies, so it comes through unchanged**, the same as колико: no jers, no yat or nasal, and the only stop is к, which lenition ([[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § 12) does not touch (only /b d g/). The result is identical to Serbo-Croatian *неколико*.

**Stress** is placed on the root of колико, **-ко-**, as on [[../Words/колико|колико]] itself. Serbo-Croatian stresses the first syllable (*nȅkoliko*); I have not checked that against Sisician's prefix rules, which are not worked out.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, no register coloring.

## Open Points

- **Mass nouns.** "Some water" has no construction yet, the same gap as [[../Words/њоги|њоги]]'s "much with mass nouns."
- **The negative and relative partners** (the *ne-* here is the indefinite prefix, not [[../Words/не|не]]) aren't built.
