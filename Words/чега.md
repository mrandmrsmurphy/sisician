# чега (čega)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Oblique cell borrowed for [[кај]] — see [[../Grammar/Interrogatives|Grammar/Interrogatives]] for the full paradigm. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Of what?" — genitive, filling the oblique slot [[кај]] never developed its own form for.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic reconstructs \*česo, but real modern Croatian shows *čega* — releveled by analogy with [[кога]]'s own -ga ending, since "who" and "what" share one historical paradigm** (see [[../Grammar/Interrogatives|Grammar/Interrogatives]]). A cross-paradigm analogical extension, not an independent sound change — the same kind of paradigm-uniformity pressure already normalized elsewhere in this vault.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, no register coloring.

## Related

[[кај]] (nominative/accusative), [[чему]] (dative), [[чим]] (instrumental). [[кога]] — the "who" cell this word's own ending was releveled to match.
