# к (k)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The whither member of the "at a person" triple, between [[../Words/код|код]] and [[../Words/од|од]]; dative's one spatial use. See [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] for the system and [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Toward, to" — goal of motion, typically a person: the spatial extension of dative's recipient sense. Whither partner of [[../Words/код|код]] + genitive ("to X's place"); "to beside" is код + accusative instead.

**Allomorph ка** before к and г and before hard clusters (real BCS *ka*): **ка кази** "toward the building."

## Government

| Case | Sense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Dative | toward, to | **к њему** "toward him"; **ка кази** "toward the building" |

## Etymology

Common Slavic **\*kъ(n)** (standard, not separately cited). The weak jer drops ([[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]]); its old final -n (\*kъn) is one source of the prothetic н- on њега/њему/њим — see [[../Words/он|он]] § Etymology, which explains why the pronoun after к is always the н-form (**к њему**).

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary — outside [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]]' three-layer model, the same footing as [[../Words/одовде|одовде]].
