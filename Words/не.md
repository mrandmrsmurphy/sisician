# не (ne)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** See [[../Grammar/Negation|Grammar/Negation]] for the full не/[[нон]] split and why it's a genuine Sisician-specific innovation. Fills [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Etymology/Swadesh List]] item 16. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

Verbal/adverbial negation — "not," negating a verb or clause. The ordinary, unmarked, grammatically-obligatory negator. Contrast [[нон]], the standalone/adjectival negation word.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*ne → не directly — trivial, no fork.** Real Croatian and Slovene both show *ne* unmodified; no jer, no denasalization, no adaptation needed at all. **Already present, unremarked, inside [[../Grammar/Biti (To Be)|Biti (To Be)]]'s own fused negative present нисам** (не + јесам → нисам, a real, well-documented Slavic negation-fusion pattern) — that page built the form without spelling out this half of it; this page is where it's finally named.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, no register coloring at all — the plainest, most unmarked word in the language.

## Related

[[нон]] — the Dalmatian-descended counterpart for the adjective/interjection slot. [[../Grammar/Biti (To Be)|Biti (To Be)]]'s нисам — the fused form already containing this word.
