# теби (tebi)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Twenty-fifth individual pronoun entry — the dative cell of [[ти]]'s paradigm, and the exact 2sg structural twin of [[мени]], down to drawing on the same already-fetched citation rather than a new one.

## Meaning

"To you" / "for you," 2nd person singular dative — the full/stressed counterpart to the short dative clitic already named on [[ти]]'s own page. **[[ти]]'s page already flagged a real, inherited homophony here, not a typo:** the dative short/clitic form is [[ти]] — spelled and pronounced identically to the nominative ти. This page doesn't need to re-flag that; it belongs to [[ти]]'s own paradigm entry, not to теби's.

## Etymology

**Already fetched, not re-derived:** [[тебе]]'s own page fetched the complete Proto-Slavic *\*ty* declension table for this paradigm's oblique row, and it already gives the dative forms directly: full **\*tebě̀**, short/clitic **\*ti**. This page draws on that same citation for its own case, exactly the way [[мени]] drew on [[мене]]'s citation rather than repeating the fetch.

**The same e~o ablaut already flagged on [[ти]]'s and [[тебе]]'s pages** — тебе/теби share the stem **теб-**, contrasting with instrumental [[тобом]]'s shift to **тоб-** — governs теби the same way it governs [[тебе]]; not a separate fact to establish here.

**The short form [[ти]] continues \*ti with no further complication**, the exact parallel to [[мени]]'s own finding for [[ми]]: unlike [[га]]/[[је]] (each with a real, explicitly-flagged unresolved gap) or [[ме]]/[[те]] (needing a denasalization story), *\*ti* is already the bare short form in the fetched Proto-Slavic table itself. The only genuine complication here isn't etymological at all — it's the homophony with nominative ти, already handled on that page.
