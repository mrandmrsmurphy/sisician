# хтети (hteti)

**Status: exploratory/tentative — the vault's second major suppletive-feeling auxiliary verb, structurally [[../Words/бити|бити]]'s closest parallel.** Everything here was already decided piecemeal across [[../Grammar/Hteti (To Want) and the Future|Hteti (To Want) and the Future]] and [[../Grammar/Verbal Categories|Verbal Categories]] — this page consolidates it into one lexical entry, the way [[../Words/бити|бити]] did for биti. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"To want" as an ordinary lexical verb (**хоћу да сијам** would be "I want to shine," if the sun could say so), and — in its short/clitic present — the **Future I** auxiliary: "will [verb]." The same verb does double duty in real BCS too; this isn't a Sisician-specific conflation.

## Paradigms

**Infinitive**: **хте́ти**. Continues Common Slavic **\*xъtěti** with its yer dropped (weak, per ordinary [[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]]) and its yat resolved to **e** via [[../Sources/Late Common Slavic|Late Common Slavic]]'s Ekavian reflex — real Serbian *hteti*'s own shape, not Croatian Ijekavian *htjeti*'s je/ije. Per [[../Grammar/Infinitive Loss and Da-Complementation|Infinitive Loss and Da-Complementation]], read as the historical citation form — receding gradually, gone by Modern Sisician in most verbal-complement contexts.

**Long/stressed present ("хоћу")** — the ordinary lexical "want," built on a *different* ablaut grade of the root than the infinitive (real Common Slavic \*xotjǫ, plain-o root + jotated thematic suffix, not the infinitive's yer-shaped хт-):

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | хо́чу | хо́чемо |
| 2nd | хо́чеш | хо́чете |
| 3rd | хо́че | хо́че |

Two real, inherited irregularities kept rather than smoothed over: **1st singular хочу**, not †хочем (real BCS's *hoću* is the same way); **3rd plural = 3rd singular** (real *hoće* covers both, an old syncretism, not a fresh leveling).

**Short/clitic present ("че")** — the Future I auxiliary, dropping the long form's **хо-** exactly the way [[../Words/бити|бити]]'s сам drops јесам's је-:

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | чу | чемо |
| 2nd | чеш | чете |
| 3rd | че | че |

**Negative present ("нечу")** — fused, not periphrastic, the same small closed class [[../Grammar/Negation|не]] already put биti in:

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | не́чу | не́чемо |
| 2nd | не́чеш | не́чете |
| 3rd | не́че | не́че |

## Future I

**сунце че сијати** (old/middle-period, infinitive-based) → **сунце че да сија** (modern-period, да-based) — "the sun will shine," per [[../Grammar/Infinitive Loss and Da-Complementation|Infinitive Loss and Da-Complementation]]'s own already-decided timeline, now with an actual verb to demonstrate it. Negated: **сунце нече да сија**, "the sun will not shine." See [[../Texts/Conlang Syntax Test Cases|Conlang Syntax Test Cases]] item 4.

**че occupies [[../Grammar/Clitic Ordering|Clitic Ordering]]'s existing AUX slot**, already stated generically enough to cover it without a fresh rule — real Browne's ordering doesn't reserve that slot for биti's clitics specifically.

## Etymology

Common Slavic **\*xъtěti**, inherited — real, cognate with Russian **хотеть** and Ukrainian **хотіти** (confirmed via search this session). The infinitive/present split is a genuine inherited root-ablaut alternation (yer-grade хт- vs. o-grade хоч-), not a Sisician-specific irregularity invented to spice things up — real *htjeti/hoću* keeps the identical split. хт- as a word-initial cluster is already independently legal per [[../Phonology/Phonotactics|Phonotactics]] (cf. кто).

## Register

**native-neutral** — like [[../Words/бити|бити]], substrate rather than belonging to one of [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Etymology/Semantic Domains]]'s three layers: auxiliary machinery underneath every register alike.
