# дати (dati)

**Status: exploratory/tentative — the vault's third native athematic verb, after [[../Words/бити|бити]] and [[../Words/хтети|хтети]], and its first genuinely ditransitive one.** Everything present-tense/participle/imperative-shaped here follows [[../Grammar/Verb Paradigm (Sketch)|Verb Paradigm (Sketch)]], [[../Grammar/Imperative|Imperative]], and [[../Grammar/Past Tense Fusion and Evidentiality|Past Tense Fusion and Evidentiality]] exactly as [[../Words/респонџати|респонџати]] and [[../Words/сијати|сијати]] already do — the new ground this page breaks is § Ditransitive Clitic Syntax below, extended in parallel on [[../Grammar/Clitic Ordering|Clitic Ordering]]. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"To give" — the basic transfer verb, taking a recipient (dative) and a theme, the thing transferred (accusative): **дати некому нешто**, "to give someone something." Perfective by inheritance (see § Etymology) — its unmarked aspectual sense is a single, bounded act of handing something over, not an ongoing or habitual one.

## Conjugation (irregular)

Real Common Slavic **\*dati** is one of only five verbs (alongside **\*byti**, **\*věděti**, **\*iměti**, **\*jasti**) that kept the old Indo-European athematic present-tense endings — no thematic vowel, endings attached straight to the root. Sisician inherits the real modern Serbo-Croatian/Slovene outcome of that paradigm directly (both branches agree here, no fork to resolve):

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | дам | да́мо |
| 2nd | даш | да́те |
| 3rd | да | да́ју |

**Quietly irregular, unlike [[../Words/бити|бити]]/[[../Words/хтети|хтети]]'s own loud suppletion.** дати's root is bare **да-**, and the productive a-type conjugation class ([[../Grammar/Verb Paradigm (Sketch)|Verb Paradigm (Sketch)]]'s гледати) also uses **-а-** as its thematic vowel — so the old athematic paradigm and the regular a-type pattern land on the *same surface endings* (-м/-ш/-Ø/-мо/-те/-ју) by historical coincidence, not design. Real modern speakers of Serbo-Croatian/Slovene alike reanalyze *dati* as if it were an ordinary a-type verb for exactly this reason. дати is filed as `irregular`, not `a-type`, because the *history* is athematic even though the *surface shape* isn't distinguishable from a-type — the same "real irregularity, not invented for flavor" standard [[../Words/бити|бити]] and [[../Words/хтети|хтети]] were held to.

**Root-anchored stress** stays on **да-** throughout, including the disyllabic да́мо/да́те/да́ју — the same principle already stated for every other paradigm in the vault ([[../Phonology/Modern Inventory|Modern Inventory]] § Prosody).

**Worth flagging, not built:** real \*dadętь (reduplicated 3pl, the genuinely athematic historical form, vs. modern да́ју's analogical leveling toward the -aju pattern) survives dialectally in real South Slavic. This vault has already kept one deliberate athematic archaism ([[../Words/кто|кто]]'s instrumental цем) at explicit user request — да̀деду/**дадеду** would be the parallel move here, plausibly surviving in a liturgical/archaic register the way the aorist does ([[../Grammar/Verbal Categories|Verbal Categories]]). Flagged as available, not adopted, per this vault's standing policy against inventing forms unprompted.

## L-Participle and the Three-Way Evidential Contrast

**L-participle**, via [[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § 10's L-vocalization (root **да-** + participle suffix, masculine singular vocalizing to *-o*):

| | Masculine (sg.) | Feminine (sg.) | Masculine (pl.) | Feminine (pl.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| l-participle | дао | дала | дали | дале |

The full [[../Grammar/Past Tense Fusion and Evidentiality|Past Tense Fusion and Evidentiality]] system, дати's own fourth full worked example (after радити, [[../Words/респонџати|респонџати]], [[../Words/сијати|сијати]]):

| Form | Masculine | Feminine |
|---|---|---|
| Conservative, singular | је дао | је дала |
| Conservative, plural | су дали | су дале |
| Fused (witnessed), singular | дја̀о | дја̀ла |
| Fused (witnessed), plural | сда́ли | сда́ле |
| Dropped (hearsay), singular | дао | дала |
| Dropped (hearsay), plural | дали | дале |

**Singular fuses with metathesis, plural doesn't — the same asymmetry as every prior verb.** је+дао puts /j/ (glide) directly in front of /d/ (obstruent), the identical Sonority Sequencing violation already resolved for [[../Words/бити|бити]]'s је+био→бјио and [[../Words/сијати|сијати]]'s је+сијао→сјѝјао — repairs by metathesis to /d/+/j/: **дја̀о**, not †јдао. Plural су+дали puts /s/ in front of /d/, an obstruent+obstruent "s+stop" cluster — already independently legal (per [[../Phonology/Phonotactics|Phonotactics]], and directly paralleled by [[../Words/бити|бити]]'s own су+били→сбили) — so it needs no repair at all: **сдали**, plain concatenation.

## Imperative

Per [[../Grammar/Imperative|Imperative]]'s formation rule: the present stem is vowel-final (да-, since 3rd singular **да** carries no ending), so it takes the **-ј/-јмо/-јте** allomorph, the same pattern as гледати and [[../Words/респонџати|респонџати]]:

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| 2nd | дај | да́јте |
| 1st | — | да́јмо |

Jussive 1sg/3sg/3pl (**нека** + ordinary present): **не́ка дам**, **не́ка да**, **не́ка да́ју**. Negative imperative, the suppletive немој-construction: **немо́ј да даш**, **немо́јте да да́те**, **немо́јмо да да́мо**.

## Verbal Noun — Deliberately Left Open

**Not built here, and not a gap to paper over.** [[../Grammar/Verbal Categories|Verbal Categories]]'s deverbal-noun machinery (the -ње/-че allomorphy that gave [[../Words/бити|бити]] its биче) would, applied mechanically, put дати in exactly бити's own special environment — root ending in a vowel right before infinitive -ти — predicting a form like **даче**. But real Slavic nominalization productively derives its *-nje/-tje* deverbal nouns from the **imperfective** member of an aspect pair, not the perfective; real Croatian's actual word for "the act of giving" is **davanje**, built on imperfective *davati*, not a form built on perfective *dati* directly. Since дати is perfective (§ Etymology) and its imperfective partner **давати** (real Common Slavic *\*davati*) hasn't been coined, forcing a да-based deverbal noun now would mean building on the wrong stem. Left open, flagged as the natural next step if давати ever gets built — not invented here per this vault's standing policy against unprompted coinage.

## Ditransitive Clitic Syntax

**The reason this word needed its own paradigm push.** дати is the vault's first verb whose ordinary argument structure fills *two* pronominal clitic slots at once — dative recipient and accusative/genitive theme — both already available (per [[../Grammar/Personal Pronouns|Personal Pronouns]] § Clitics) but never exercised together before. See [[../Grammar/Clitic Ordering|Clitic Ordering]] § "Ditransitive Stacking: дати" for the full worked paradigm; summarized here:

- **Present, no auxiliary needed:** **Дам ти га.** "I give it to you." (1sg дам, DAT ти, ACC га — the finite verb itself occupies first position since the subject јаз is pro-dropped, per [[../Grammar/Verbal Agreement|Verbal Agreement]].)
- **Past, conservative, full DAT+ACC+AUX stack:** **Отац му га је дао.** "Father gave it to him." (Отац is the first stressed constituent; the clitic cluster му-га-је fills second position in the fixed DAT–ACC–је order; дао follows.)
- **Past, fused (witnessed):** **Отац му га дја̀о.** — је has fused into дја̀о, so it no longer occupies the final clitic slot; the other clitics (му, га) stay clustered after Отац, exactly as [[../Grammar/Clitic Ordering|Clitic Ordering]] § "What Happens to Other Clitics When је Fuses or Drops" predicts.
- **Reflexive, hitting a fifth slot:** **Дала му се.** "She gave herself to him." (DAT му, then reflexive SE се — the object argument itself is the reflexive, not a separate accusative clitic, so only two of the five template slots are filled here, not stacked with ACC too.)

No new ordering rule was needed — [[../Grammar/Clitic Ordering|Clitic Ordering]]'s existing **li–AUX–DAT–ACC/GEN–SE–је** template already reserves adjacent DAT and ACC/GEN slots in exactly this order; дати is simply the first verb whose real semantics fill both at once, confirming rather than extending the template.

## Etymology

**Common Slavic \*dati**, inherited — real, bedrock Indo-European vocabulary, from Proto-Balto-Slavic **\*dṓˀtei**, ultimately Proto-Indo-European **\*dédeh₃ti** "to give" (a reduplicated present, the same formation type underlying Latin *dō/dare* and Greek δίδωμι — дати and its Vulgar Latin cousin behind [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Swadesh List]] item 128's *\*dare* are true cognates, not a coincidence, both continuing the same PIE root **\*deh₃-**). One of only five Common Slavic athematic verbs — [[../Words/бити|бити]] (\*byti) and [[../Words/хтети|хтети]] (\*xъtěti, thematic rather than athematic but similarly irregular) are this vault's other two; \*jasti "to eat" and \*věděti/\*iměti "to know/have" remain uncoined, a real, available next step rather than an oversight.

**Perfective by inheritance**, with a real, uncoined imperfective partner **\*davati** (→ Sisician давати, not yet built) — the same perfective/imperfective structure [[../Grammar/Verbal Categories|Verbal Categories]] already confirmed as inherited bedrock for every Sisician verb, дати's first concrete lexical instance of a *named* (if still unbuilt) aspectual partner.

No sound changes apply beyond the ordinary present/participle/imperative machinery already established — дати's root vowel /a/ is untouched by the Fall of the Jers, yat-resolution, or denasalization, the cleanest derivation of any verb built so far.

## Register

**native-neutral**, per [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]]'s three-layer model — "to give" is core Layer I ("basic verbs," explicitly listed) vocabulary, and stays native rather than crossing into Dalmatian the way [[../Words/фамаља|фамаља]] and [[../Words/респонџати|респонџати]] did for "family" and "answer." **A real Dalmatian alternative existed and was passed over**: [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Swadesh List]] item 128 records the real attested Dalmatian form **duor** (< Vulgar Latin *\*dare*) alongside дати's own Slavic comparanda — the same "road not taken" pattern [[../Words/дом|дом]]'s page documents against [[../Words/каза|каза]]. Unlike фамаља/респонџати, дати is the on-model case: basic-vocabulary Layer I holding, not eroding, the third such confirmation after [[../Words/дом|дом]] and [[../Words/сунце|сунце]].
