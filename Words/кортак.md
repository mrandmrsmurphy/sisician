# кортак (kortak)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "Short," filling [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Etymology/Swadesh List]] item 33, at the user's direction as a **Latinate-Slavic hybrid**: the Dalmatian stem *kort* with the native Slavic adjective suffix *-ъkъ*. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Short," of length and of time: **кортак пут** "a short road," **кортака реч** "a short word." One word for both senses, like [[../Words/даог|даог]] "long," which is its opposite. "Short" of a person's height is not claimed; nothing here rules it out.

**Adverbial use: the masculine singular, unchanged**, per [[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]] § Adjective to Adverb: **кортак** is also "briefly."

## Forms

Gender and number agreement only, like every adjective ([[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]] § The Modern System). **The masculine singular has a mobile а that the other three forms lack**, the same pattern as [[../Words/тежак|тежак]] / тежка and [[../Words/светао|светао]] / светла ([[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]] § Mobile *a*):

| | Masculine | Feminine |
|---|---|---|
| Singular | кортак | кортка |
| Plural | кортки | кортке |

No case and no definiteness. A noun that is old-neuter-plural takes the feminine plural, by the neuter merger. Stress is root-anchored on the first syllable, **ко-**, throughout (ˈkortak, ˈkortka, ˈkortki, ˈkortke), which is also where the Dalmatian stem stresses.

## Etymology

**The stem.** **Dalmatian *kort*** "short," the Swadesh table's entry for item 33 ([Appendix:Dalmatian Swadesh list](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Appendix:Dalmatian_Swadesh_list)), from **Vulgar Latin \*curtu** (Classical *curtus* "cut short"). The Latin ŭ is Vulgar Latin close-mid [o], which is why the Dalmatian form has *o*. I have not worked the Latin→Dalmatian step in [[../Sources/Dalmatian|Sources/Dalmatian]]; the page takes *kort* as given by the table.

**The suffix.** **Proto-Slavic \*-ъkъ**, the productive adjective suffix (it is in \*tęžьkъ → [[../Words/тежак|тежак]], with the front-jer variant \*-ьkъ). The bare adjective stem is **kort-**, so the formation is *kort* + *-ъkъ*.

**Result of the hybrid: \*kortъkъ.** This is exactly the **Proto-Slavic form** the Swadesh table lists for "short," \*kortъkъ (OCS кратъкъ, Serbo-Croatian кратак, Slovene kratek). The Latin and Slavic words are cognates, both from PIE \*(s)ker- "cut"; I am recalling that, not citing it. So the Dalmatian stem plus the native suffix rebuilds the inherited Slavic word, and the two routes can't be told apart. The hybrid stays as the stated source because it is the user's design and because the Dalmatian *kort* is what the Latinate side supplies; the stem is the one thing that does **not** go through liquid metathesis (below).

**Sisician derivation, one step:**

1. **Fall of the Jers** ([[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]]): two jers. Counting from the right, the final ъ is weak and drops; the medial ъ is strong and vocalizes to **a**. \*kortъkъ → **кортак**.

**Liquid metathesis does not apply** ([[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § 3). That change sends CoRC to CroC in Slavic words and is what gives Serbo-Croatian *кратак* (\*kortъkъ → \*kratъkъ). Here the *ort* comes in as a Dalmatian stem already fixed before the Slavic suffix is added, so the metathesis, which is a change in native Slavic words, doesn't reach it. This is the point of the hybrid: **кортак vs. кратак** sets Sisician apart from Serbo-Croatian, the same distancing aim as the [[../../AIOS/memory/project_distancing_from_slavic_neighbors|Distancing From Slavic Neighbors]] thread.

**The other forms.** In \*kortъka, \*kortъki, \*kortъke the only jer is the medial ъ, so it is rightmost and weak, and it drops: **кортка, кортки, кортке**. This is the ordinary mobile-a alternation.

No other change applies: the к is not a lenition target (§ 12 is б, д, г only), the о and р are already in the inventory, there is no л, and the к-т-к run is all voiceless, so regressive devoicing has nothing to change.

## Register

Native, unmarked, [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]] Layer I, since the result is the same as the native Slavic derivation. The Dalmatian pedigree of the stem is the only trace of Layer II.

## Open Points

- **Whether to keep the Slavic spelling away from кратак.** I read the user's choice as deliberate, so metathesis is blocked on the grounds above. If the user would rather the word simply be native, it would come out as кратак and Sisician would match Serbo-Croatian.
- **The coda cluster -рт- in кортка.** Syllabified кор.тка, the coda р is a single consonant and the onset тк is an obstruent+obstruent cluster, which is not among the onsets [[../Phonology/Phonotactics|Phonotactics]] lists. Alternatively корт.ка, with a -рт coda; тежка has the same question for ж-к. Not settled.
- **The Latin→Dalmatian step** isn't worked, and the cognacy with the Slavic root is from memory.
- **The comparative** isn't built.
- **The opposite** is [[../Words/даог|даог]] "long."
