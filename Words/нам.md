# нам (nam)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The short/clitic dative form of [[нама]], and, per that page's own finding, the clean half of a pair whose full form has a real unresolved wrinkle.

## Meaning

The unstressed second-position clitic covering dative for "to us," per [[../Grammar/Personal Pronouns|Personal Pronouns]]'s table. No instrumental clitic exists for this paradigm cell — [[нама]] itself covers instrumental in both registers, stressed and unstressed alike, matching [[../Grammar/Personal Pronouns|Personal Pronouns]]'s own standing confirmation that this paradigm has no instrumental clitic anywhere.

## Etymology — The Clean Half of the Pair

**[[нама]]'s own page already worked this out rather than leaving it for this page to re-derive:** нам continues Proto-Slavic dative **\*nàmъ** directly, via the ordinary [[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]] (loss of the weak final jer) — the same universal sound law used throughout this project for every other word, not a special case invented for this pronoun. This is a genuinely clean derivation, in direct contrast to [[нама]]'s own full/stressed form, whose *-a* ending isn't the regular reflex of either historical dative *\*namъ* or instrumental *\*nami* on its own (see that page's etymology for the honestly-flagged gap).

**Worth stating plainly, since it's easy to miss:** нам is *not* a reduction of нама. It's the direct, ordinary continuation of the older dative-specific form, while нама itself is the one that needed a separate (and only partially resolved) explanation for its shape. This is the reverse of [[их]]'s relationship to [[њих]] (where the clitic was the conservative form and the full form was innovated) but for a different reason — here the clitic simply preserves the regular sound-law outcome, while the full form was reshaped by a process (likely noun-paradigm analogy, per [[нама]]'s page) that only touched the stressed form.

**Real Slavic comparanda (dative clitic):**

| Branch | Language | Form |
|---|---|---|
| South Slavic (liturgical) | Old Church Slavonic | намъ (namŭ) — full form only; no distinct short form found in the sources checked |
| South Slavic | Serbo-Croatian | nam |
| South Slavic | Slovene | nam |
| East Slavic | Russian | no comparable short clitic in the standard language (full нам used, no register distinction) |
| West Slavic | Polish | nam |

**No branch tension found** — every branch checked keeps a *namъ*-derived dative shape; the real cross-branch tension in this paradigm belongs to [[нама]]'s own instrumental-merger question, not to this cell.

## Register — Clitic Placement

Per [[../Grammar/Clitic Ordering|Clitic Ordering]], нам occupies the dative-clitic slot in the second-position template, the same slot [[му]]/[[јој]] occupy for 3rd person singular. Full [[нама]] is required after a preposition and for contrastive emphasis; нам takes the ordinary slot otherwise.
