# овога (ovoga)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The proximal counterpart to [[тога]] — see [[../Grammar/Demonstrative Pronouns|Grammar/Demonstrative Pronouns]] for the full paradigm. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

Genitive of [[овај]] ("of this"), and — per the animate-accusative-uses-genitive rule already established on [[../Words/њега|њега]]/[[../Words/дом|дом]]/[[тога]] — the accusative form for an animate masculine referent. Inanimate accusative reuses [[овај]] itself.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*ovogo → овога directly**, the identical mechanism as [[тога]] on the ов- root: oblique endings are vowel-initial, so the root's jer was never word-final and needed no *jь-reinforcement. Same -ога ending family as [[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]]'s нувога and [[тога]] itself. Matches real Serbo-Croatian *òvōga* exactly.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, same status as every other pronoun entry.

## Related

[[овај]] (nominative), [[овому]] (dative), [[овим]] (instrumental). [[тога]] — the medial counterpart.
