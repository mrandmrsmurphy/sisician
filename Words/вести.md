# вести (vesti)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "To lead, conduct" — part of the [[../Texts/Pater Noster|Pater Noster]] set: **и не веди нас у напаст** "and lead us not into temptation." See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"To lead, conduct" — perfective: bring someone to a destination. The imperfective partner **водити** is unbuilt — worth noting because real BCS's own prayer text uses it (*ne vodi nas u napast*); Sisician follows the Slavonic's perfective въведи instead, so the prayer has **веди**, and водити stays available for ordinary "lead/guide" use. The negative imperative **не веди** is one of the prayer's liturgical fossils (the productive vernacular negative is немој + да — see [[../Texts/Pater Noster|the prayer page]] § The Prayer as a Fossil Register).

The initial в is bilabial [β]: /ˈβesti/.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*vesti** "to lead, conduct" — from Proto-Balto-Slavic \*westéi, PIE \*wedʰ- ([Wiktionary \*vesti](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Reconstruction:Proto-Slavic/vesti), fetched; the same page gives the imperative \*vedi and the full present paradigm this page's table follows). No sound change applies to the stem; the participle's вео is the one irregular cell, copied from real BCS *veo*.
