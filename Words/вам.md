# вам (vam)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Thirty-third individual pronoun entry — the short/clitic dative form of [[вама]], and the exact structural twin of [[нам]], the way [[вама]] was already the twin of [[нама]].

## Meaning

The unstressed second-position clitic covering dative for "to you" (plural, or formal singular per [[ви]]'s T–V function), per [[../Grammar/Personal Pronouns|Personal Pronouns]]'s table. No instrumental clitic exists for this paradigm cell — [[вама]] itself covers instrumental in both registers, matching [[нама]]'s own parallel.

## Etymology — The Same Clean Half, One Person Over

**[[нам]]'s own page already worked out the relevant reasoning, and it transfers directly rather than needing re-derivation:** вам continues Proto-Slavic dative **\*vàmъ** via the ordinary [[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]] — the same universal sound law, with none of [[вама]]'s own unresolved *-a*-vowel puzzle. Exactly as [[нам]] is not a reduction of [[нама]], вам is not a reduction of [[вама]]: it's the regular sound-law outcome, while [[вама]] is the one that departed from it (via the same likely noun-paradigm analogy already discussed on [[нама]]'s page).

**No fresh fetch was needed for this page** — the *vamъ* citation continues directly from [[ви]]'s and [[вама]]'s already-established sourcing, and the Fall-of-the-Jers mechanism is the same one applied to [[нам]], [[их]], and every other word in this project.

## Register — Clitic Placement

Per [[../Grammar/Clitic Ordering|Clitic Ordering]], вам occupies the dative-clitic slot in the second-position template, the same slot [[нам]] occupies for 1st person. Full [[вама]] is required after a preposition and for contrastive emphasis; вам takes the ordinary slot otherwise.
