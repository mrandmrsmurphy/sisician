# чијим (čijim)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Instrumental singular masculine of [[чији]] — and, by the same three-way syncretism as [[којим]], the plural dative/instrumental of both genders. See [[../Grammar/Relative Clauses|Grammar/Relative Clauses]]. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"By/with whose (masculine singular)?" — **даожник, чијим хлебом сијам** "the debtor by whose bread I shine" — and plural dative/instrumental, the [[../Words/тим|тим]]-parallel syncretism. The possessive relative in an instrumental role: "the means by whose..., the company with whose...."

## Etymology

**чиј- stem + -им**, the pronominal instrumental of the established family. No sound change applies; real BCS *čijim* matches.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, both uses productive.

## Related

[[чији]], [[чега]]/[[чијега]] (genitive), [[чему]]/[[чијему]] (dative). [[којим]] — the same cell on који.
