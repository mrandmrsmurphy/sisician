# куи (kui)

**Status: exploratory/tentative — proposed, not yet confirmed as adopted vocabulary.** Latinate register doublet of [[који]] — see [[../Grammar/Interrogatives|Grammar/Interrogatives]] § Which. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Which?" — the same meaning as [[који]], restricted to the written legal/notarial register rather than ordinary speech.

## Etymology

**Direct borrowing of Latin QUI** (the relative/interrogative pronoun "who/which"), entering not through spoken Dalmatian but through the **written legal/notarial Latin register** already established on [[../Culture/Sociolinguistic History|Culture/Sociolinguistic History]] — Latin surviving specifically in "diplomatic/foreign-trade/family-document contexts," the same channel already motivating [[абкле]]. Sisician's phonology has no labialized-consonant series ([[../Phonology/Modern Inventory|Modern Inventory]]'s consonant inventory), so Latin's /kw/ onset is adapted letter-by-letter as **ку-**, giving куи directly — a real citation-form borrowing (matching how real legal English still keeps largely-unadapted Latin phrases like *qui tam*) rather than an organically sound-changed loanword.

## Register

Dalmatian-formal/legal, per frontmatter — unlike [[../Words/ној|ној]] or [[../Words/дешет|дешет]], куи is not proposed to generalize or displace [[који]]; it stays confined to its own written register.

## Related

[[који]] — the native, unmarked counterpart. [[абкле]] — the other Latinate-legal-register hybrid from the same proposed channel.
