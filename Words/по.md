# по (po)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** A former locative preposition with a wide spread of senses, now genitive/accusative. See [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] for the system and [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

**+ genitive** takes over the old locative senses: "around, throughout" a space, "according to," "after." **+ accusative** keeps its own: "for" in the sense of going to fetch someone or something (real BCS *doći po nekoga* "to come for someone"), and the distributive. See [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] § Former Locative-Governing Prepositions.

## Government

| Case | Sense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Genitive | throughout; according to; after | **по тога** "according to that, by that" |
| Accusative | for (to fetch) | **по њега** "(to come) for him" |

## Etymology

Common Slavic **\*po**, from PIE **\*h₂pó** — cognate with Greek **ἀπό** (standard etymology, not separately cited).

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary — outside [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]]' three-layer model, the same footing as [[../Words/одовде|одовде]].
