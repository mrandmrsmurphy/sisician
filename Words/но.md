# но (no)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "But" — the [[../Texts/Pater Noster|Pater Noster]]'s adversative: **но избави нас од зла** "but deliver us from evil." See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"But" — adversative conjunction. **Flagged open:** whether но is the general vernacular "but" or a Slavonic-flavored/liturgical one, with a vernacular alternative (a него-type word, unbuilt) covering everyday contrast, is undecided. It is coined here on the Slavonic text's own authority (но избави ны) and is genuinely attested in real BCS as well (*no*), so it has a life outside the prayer regardless; the distribution question is recorded on [[../Texts/Pater Noster|the prayer page]].

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*no** "but" — recalled bedrock (not freshly fetched), attested in the OCS prayer text itself and across the family (BCS *no*, Russian *но*). No sound change applies.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary; the liturgical coloring, if it has any, is a use-register, not a different word.
