# твој (tvoj)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "Thy, your" (of one addressee) — the 2nd-person-singular possessive, part of the [[../Texts/Pater Noster|Pater Noster]] set: име твој, царство твој, волја твоја. The full paradigm and its design decision live on [[../Grammar/Possessive Pronouns|Grammar/Possessive Pronouns]]. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Thy, your" — possession/association belonging to the addressee (singular; the plural/formal counterpart is ваш, on [[../Grammar/Possessive Pronouns|the grammar page]]). Agrees with the possessed noun in gender and number: **твој** (m. sg.), **твоја** (f. sg.), **твоји** (m. pl.), **твоје** (f. pl.). **No case forms** — possessives follow the modern adjective system, per the decision flagged on [[../Grammar/Possessive Pronouns|the grammar page]].

In the prayer it shows the neuter merger twice: **име твој** and **царство твој** are masculine singular (old neuters take masculine agreement), where real BCS and the Slavonic both have the neuter-looking *твоје* — one of the small places the prayer sounds distinctly Sisician. **волја твоја** is genuinely feminine.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*tvojь** — modeled on \*mojь, from PIE \*twós ([Wiktionary \*tvojь](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Reconstruction:Proto-Slavic/tvoj%D1%8C), fetched, with the full declension and the note that the 2nd-person possessive was built on the 1st-person one's pattern). The final ь drops ([[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]]): **твој**, matching real BCS *tvȏj* exactly. The в is bilabial [β] in every position: /tβoj/.

## Register

Native, unmarked, closed-class-adjacent (a pronominal, not a content word).
