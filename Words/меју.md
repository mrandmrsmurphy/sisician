# меју (meju)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The three-case preposition of the location grid: instrumental, accusative, and genitive, each with its own sense. See [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] for the system and [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

**+ instrumental** = "among" (where), usually with a plural or collective. **+ accusative** = "into the midst of" (whither) — real BCS *došao je među nas* "he came among us." **+ genitive** = "between [two]," a pair specifically — the job real BCS gives the separate preposition *između*, which Sisician can't use for it because its из- compounds are whence-only; so меју takes it over. Whence is [[../Words/измеју|измеју]] + genitive.

The instrumental/genitive split is by number of referents: among many vs. between two. It is the one preposition in the grid whose three cases all mark *different* senses, as Greek παρά's do.

## Government

| Case | Sense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Instrumental | among | **меју нама** "among us" |
| Accusative | into the midst of | **меју нас** "into our midst" |
| Genitive | between (two) | **меју овога и тога** "between this and that" ([[../Words/овога|овога]], [[../Words/тога|тога]]) |

## Etymology

Common Slavic **\*medju**, from \*medja "boundary," ultimately PIE **\*médʰyos** "middle" — cognate with Latin **medius** and Greek **μέσος** (standard etymology, not separately cited). The **dj** cluster gives **ј**, not BCS **đ**, by Sisician's established ć/đ→č/j merger ([[../Sources/Late Common Slavic|Late Common Slavic]]) — меју, not †међу.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary — outside [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]]' three-layer model, the same footing as [[../Words/одовде|одовде]].
