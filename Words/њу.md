# њу (nju)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Thirty-seventh individual pronoun entry — the accusative cell of [[она]]'s paradigm, already promised as a genuinely separate word from [[ње]] on that page's own § Meaning.

## Meaning — The Word [[ње]]'s Page Already Promised

[[ње]]'s own page established the key fact this word depends on: Proto-Slavic feminine singular genitive **\*jeję̇** and accusative **\*jǫ** were two distinct forms from the start, unlike masculine (where [[њега]]'s page found genitive absorbing an accusative that never had its own shape). So њу isn't a variant of [[ње]] or a reduction of anything genitive-related — it's the direct continuation of its own separate historical accusative, exactly as promised.

## Etymology — The Back-Vowel Counterpart to ме/те's Denasalization

**The prothetic-n mechanism is [[он]]'s own** (see that page), applied here to the feminine accusative the same way [[она]]'s and [[ње]]'s pages already applied it without re-deriving it.

**\*jǫ carries the back nasal vowel *ǫ**, and its regular South Slavic outcome is **denasalization to *u*** — the exact structural counterpart to *\*mę̃*/*\*tę̑*'s front nasal *ę* denasalizing to *e*, already established (and explicitly flagged as recalled general knowledge rather than freshly cited) on [[ме]]'s page. This page extends that same already-flagged evidentiary category rather than re-investigating the mechanism from scratch: *\*ǫ → u* is the back-vowel counterpart of the same general Slavic nasal-vowel loss, not a separately unexplained development. **\*jǫ** + prothetic n- + regular denasalization → **њу**, cleanly.

**The short form [[ју]] is the bare, non-prothetic continuation of the same *\*jǫ*, by the same clitics-never-take-njь- pattern already confirmed across every 3rd-person cell in this set** ([[га]], [[је]], [[их]], [[му]], [[јој]], [[им]]).

**A real, useful cross-check: Polish, the known exception for front-nasal *ę* (retaining it, per [[ме]]'s page), is also the known exception here.** Polish keeps *ǫ* as nasal *ą* too (real Polish *ją*, "her," still nasal) — the same branch behaving the same way for both nasal vowels, not an independent coincidence. This is a nice internal confirmation that the *ę*/*ǫ* denasalization split by branch is a single unified phenomenon (loss of nasal vowels generally, except in Polish), not two unrelated sound changes that happen to pattern alike.

**Real Slavic comparanda:**

| Branch | Language | Form |
|---|---|---|
| South Slavic (liturgical) | Old Church Slavonic | ѭ (ję built on *jǫ, nasal retained in the script) |
| South Slavic | Serbo-Croatian | nju; clitic ju |
| South Slavic | Slovene | njo; clitic jo |
| East Slavic | Russian | её (jejo) — a different, genitive-based form entirely, not a reflex of *jǫ |
| West Slavic | Polish | ją — nasal retained, matching [[ме]]'s own already-noted Polish exception |

**A real branch tension worth flagging, distinct from the Polish nasal-retention point:** Slovene's accusative is *njo*, not *nju* — an *o*-outcome rather than the expected *u*. This is a second instance of the same kind of unresolved vowel wrinkle already flagged on [[њој]]'s page (that one *e→o*, this one an *o* showing up where *u* would be expected), not resolved here, just noted honestly as a real comparative fact rather than smoothed into false uniformity.

## Register

Same closed-class status as [[она]]. Per [[../Grammar/Clitic Ordering|Clitic Ordering]], full њу is required after a preposition and for contrastive emphasis; [[ју]] takes the ordinary second-position object-clitic slot otherwise, the same slot [[је]] (genitive sense) occupies — the real, already-known homophony risk with [[је]]'s biti-auxiliary sense doesn't apply to ју, since ју and је are simply different words with different shapes.
