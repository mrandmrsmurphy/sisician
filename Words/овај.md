# овај (ovaj)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Second demonstrative-pronoun entry, the proximal counterpart to [[тај]] — see [[../Grammar/Demonstrative Pronouns|Grammar/Demonstrative Pronouns]] for the full shared paradigm and mechanism. Fills [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Etymology/Swadesh List]] item 7 ("this"). See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"This" — the **proximal** demonstrative (near the speaker), contrasting with [[тај]] "that." Per [[../Grammar/Nominal Morphology|Nominal Morphology]]'s neuter merger, **овај also covers every old-neuter-singular referent**, the same way [[тај]] and [[../Words/он|он]] do — no separate "this (thing)" form exists.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*ovъ (masc.nom.sg.) + \*jь (anaphoric-pronoun suffix) → овај** — the identical mechanism as [[тај]], applied to a different root. Confirmed via Wiktionary: real Serbo-Croatian *ovaj* "developed... through a suffixed form (*ovъ-jь*), combining the original demonstrative with an additional morphological element."

**Same derivation as [[тај]], step for step**, via [[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]]: bare *\*ovъ*'s word-final jer is trivially weak and would vanish unsuffixed; adding *\*jь* pushes the root's own jer one slot leftward, making it non-final and hence **strong**, vocalizing to **a**; the suffix's *ь* itself, now rightmost, is weak and drops; the suffix's *j* consonant survives. Result: **ov + a + j = овај**, matching real Serbo-Croatian *òvāj* exactly.

**\*ovъ itself** ← Proto-Balto-Slavic *\*awas*, ultimately PIE *\*h₂ew-o-s* — a separate demonstrative root from *\*tъ* (Balto-Slavic *\*tas*, PIE *\*só/tód*) from the start, not a variant of it; the two demonstratives were always historically distinct, not derived from each other.
