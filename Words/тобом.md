## Etymology — The Same Regular Ending as мном, Not a Fresh Derivation

**[[тебе]]'s own page already fetched the relevant Proto-Slavic form**, and it's exactly the structural twin of [[мене]]'s own instrumental: **\*tobòjǫ** (alongside a stress-variant *\*tobojǫ̀*), built on the identical *-ojǫ* suffix as *\*mъnojǫ*.

**[[мном]]'s page already established the real mechanism, and this page inherits it directly rather than re-deriving anything:** Common Slavic's instrumental singular *-ojǫ* is the same ending underlying real Serbo-Croatian's ordinary feminine *a*-stem noun instrumental singular (*žena* → *ženom*, from *\*ženojǫ*) — a live, fully productive pattern, not a pronoun-specific irregularity. *\*tobojǫ* → тобом by the identical regular development that gives мном from *\*mъnojǫ*. No fresh citation was sought for the specific sound change here, since it's the same ending in the same slot already covered on [[мном]]'s page.

**This also completes the *e~o* ablaut already flagged on [[ти]]'s own page**: тебе/теби share the stem **теб-**, while тобом shifts to **тоб-** — the same real, inherited alternation already noted there, now given its own concrete word rather than just described.
