# две (dve)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Feminine counterpart to [[два]] — see [[../Grammar/Numerals|Grammar/Numerals]] for the full paradigm. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Two," feminine — and, per [[../Grammar/Nominal Morphology|Nominal Morphology]]'s neuter merger, every old-neuter-**plural** referent counted as two, matching [[../Words/оне|оне]]'s own precedent exactly.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*dъvě → две, a free result of Sisician's own already-decided Ekavian yat reflex — no fresh pick needed.** Real Croatian shows ijekavian *dvije*; real Slovene and standard Serbian show the Ekavian outcome *dve*. Since [[../Sources/Late Common Slavic|Late Common Slavic]] already committed to the Ekavian yat reflex system-wide (the same choice already behind [[../Words/јаз|јаз]]'s Slovene-aligned form), две simply falls out of that standing decision, not a numeral-specific choice.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, same status as every other numeral entry.
