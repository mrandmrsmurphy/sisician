# дешет (dešet)

**Status: exploratory/tentative — the most speculative word in the numeral set alongside [[ној]].** See [[../Grammar/Numerals|Grammar/Numerals]] § Nine and Ten for the full reasoning, and [[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § Prestige-Driven Lexically-Specific Developments for the standing category this belongs to. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Ten." No gender distinction. The sole modern Sisician word — there is no separate, still-current *десет* register variant; the reshaping described below is presented as complete, not an ongoing alternation.

## Etymology

**Two layers: an ordinary regular derivation, then one lexically-specific prestige-driven reshaping on top of it.**

**Layer 1 (regular):** Proto-Slavic **\*desętь** → ***десет*** via the already-established ę→e denasalization ([[../Words/ме|ме]]/[[../Words/пет|пет]]'s own mechanism) plus trivial final-jer loss. This intermediate stage matches real Croatian and Slovene *deset* exactly and was never independently attested as a Sisician word in its own right — it's the form дешет reshapes from, not a coexisting alternative.

**Layer 2 (lexically specific, not a sound law):** десет's medial **с** sits between two front vowels (**deSET**) — the same general "coronal softens near front vowels" environment that produces real Campidanese Sardinian *dexi* from Latin DECEM (medial /k/→[ʒ], confirmed via comparative sources) — a different specific mechanism (stop-lenition-to-fricative vs. fricative-postalveolarization), but the same family of effect, and the real comparandum that suggested this reshaping (per [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Etymology/Swadesh List]]'s own established practice of using Sardinian purely as a typological model, never a claimed ancestor). Dalmatian-philic prestige speech, already established elsewhere in this vault as reshaping native Slavic sounds toward a more "Romance" impression (the [x]→[ç] softening, the eye-spelling tradition), nudges десет — the single most commercially salient number in a toll/decimal economy ([[../Culture/Geography and Economy|Culture/Geography and Economy]]) — toward this shape, and it wins out entirely as the modern standard.

**Explicitly not a general rule.** No other Sisician с becomes ш; [[../Words/скинија|скинија]] is the standing counter-example, untouched.

## Register

Unlike most closed-class vocabulary, this word carries real register history even though it's now the sole form — it began as a Dalmatian-prestige-coded reshaping of ordinary vernacular vocabulary, per [[../AIOS/memory/project_prestige_register_principle|the prestige-register principle]], before generalizing to the whole speech community. Compare [[ној]], which underwent the same kind of motivated reshaping but via full replacement rather than in-place assibilation.

## Related

[[ној]] — the sibling numeral with the same motivation but a different mechanism. [[осам]] — the last unforked, ordinary numeral before this one.
