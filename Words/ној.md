# ној (noj)

**Status: exploratory/tentative — the most speculative word in the numeral set, flagged as such rather than smoothed over.** See [[../Grammar/Numerals|Grammar/Numerals]] § Nine and Ten for the full reasoning, and [[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § Prestige-Driven Lexically-Specific Developments for the standing category this belongs to. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Nine." No gender distinction. **A genuine lexical replacement, not a reshaping of the expected native word** — unlike [[дешет]], native *девет* (< Proto-Slavic *\*devętь*, the same ę→e mechanism as [[пет]]) never coexisted with ној as a register pair; ној displaces it entirely as the ordinary modern word.

## Etymology

**Dalmatian's own reflex of Latin NOVEM, independently eroding toward the same shape real Sardinian and Romanian both show — a genuine recurring Romance-internal weakness, not a coincidence limited to one language.** Worked through:

1. **NOVEM → *nove*** — final -m lost, universal and bedrock-confident pan-Romance behavior (already unstable even in Classical Latin poetic elision), not specific to Dalmatian.
2. **Stressed short *ŏ* stays a plain vowel** — [[../Sources/Dalmatian|Dalmatian]]'s diphthongization rule specifically targets *long* stressed *ē*/*ō* ([[../Sources/Dalmatian|Dalmatian]]'s own Bartoli-sourced rule); NOVEM's *ŏ* is short, so it simply merges to /o/ under the already-established ɔ→o vowel merger ([[../Sources/Dalmatian|Dalmatian]] § End-Stage Phonology), no diphthong.
3. **Intervocalic /v/ is lost** — confirmed via Wiktionary as a *real, independent* development in **two** separate Romance branches for this exact word: Sardinian *noi* and Romanian *nouă* both lose NOVEM's intervocalic /v/, a recurring word-specific weakness (plausibly dissimilatory pressure from the following nasal, or analogical leveling with neighboring numerals) rather than a general Romance sound law. Dalmatian is proposed to pattern the same way — **not borrowed from either language, but converging independently on the same *noi*-shape**, the same evidentiary category as [[../Words/нув|нув]]'s own cross-ancestry convergence, just with Dalmatian's own hypothesized reflex rather than a directly attested one.
4. **The resulting vowel hiatus (*no-e*) resolves to a glide**, the ordinary Romance hiatus-resolution pattern, giving Dalmatian ***noj*** by the time it enters Sisician's own borrowing chain — spelled **ној**, the regular Cyrillic rendering of a word-final *-oj* already used throughout this vault's own native vocabulary (real Slavic *kroj*, *broj*-type spellings).

**Why it displaces девет rather than staying a register doublet** (unlike [[дом]]/[[каза]] or [[скинија]]/[[икос]]): Siscia's own toll/commerce identity ([[../Culture/Geography and Economy|Culture/Geography and Economy]]) makes "nine" unusually salient — the real, cross-linguistically common commercial-pricing psychology of prices and counts ending in nine gives the merchant/toll register's word for nine outsized exposure, enough (per the [[../AIOS/memory/project_prestige_register_principle|prestige-register principle]]) to generalize out and fully replace the native vernacular term rather than settle into a stable two-register split.

## Register

Originates in the commercial/toll register (Dalmatian-formal, per frontmatter) but — unlike every other Dalmatian loan in this vault so far — doesn't stay there. By the modern stage it's simply the ordinary word, carrying no more register weight than [[../Words/дешет|дешет]] does.
