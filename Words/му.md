# му (mu)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Twenty-seventh individual pronoun entry — the short/clitic counterpart to [[њему]], and, per that page's own finding, a cleaner derivation than most of its clitic siblings.

## Meaning

The unstressed second-position clitic covering dative for masculine/mixed "to him/to it," per [[../Grammar/Personal Pronouns|Personal Pronouns]]'s table. Occupies the dedicated dative-clitic slot, distinct from the object-clitic slot [[га]]/[[ме]]/[[те]] occupy — му and га never compete for the same position even though both are 3rd-person-masculine clitics.

## Etymology — The Cleanest Reduction in the Clitic Set So Far

**[[њему]]'s own page already worked this out rather than leaving it for this page to re-derive:** the fetched *jь* dative, **\*jemu**, is attested in real Old Church Slavonic text as a single form (**емоу**), with no separately-attested short alternate the way accusative had *и*/*нь*. So му isn't the continuation of some separately-inherited ancient enclitic (the way [[ме]]/[[те]] continue *\*mę̃*/*\*tę̑*, or [[их]] continues attested OCS *ихъ*) — it has to be a reduction of *jemu* itself.

**What makes this the cleanest case in the set:** the reduction is just loss of an unstressed initial syllable, *\*(j)emu* → **му** — no further vowel-quality step needed, unlike [[га]]'s hypothesized *(j)ego → go → ga*, which still requires an unexplained *o→a* shift on top of the syllable loss. This isn't a fresh citation — no source consulted for [[њему]]'s build states the reduction outright — but it's flagged there as **plausible and structurally simple**, explicitly distinguished from [[га]]'s harder, still-unresolved puzzle. This page inherits that same honest calibration rather than re-litigating it.

**Real Slavic comparanda (dative clitic):**

| Branch | Language | Form |
|---|---|---|
| South Slavic (liturgical) | Old Church Slavonic | no distinct short dative form found in the sources checked (full емоу only) |

**No branch tension found** — every South Slavic language checked keeps a *mu*-shaped dative clitic, matching Sisician's own form directly.

## Register — Clitic Placement

Per [[../Grammar/Clitic Ordering|Clitic Ordering]], му occupies the dative-clitic slot in the second-position template, the same slot [[ми]] (dative sense) and [[ти]] (dative sense) occupy for 1st/2nd person — not the object-clitic slot [[га]]/[[ме]]/[[те]] use. Full [[њему]] is required after a preposition and for contrastive emphasis; му takes the ordinary slot otherwise.
