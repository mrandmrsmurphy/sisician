# кому (komu)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Oblique cell of [[кто]] — see [[../Grammar/Interrogatives|Grammar/Interrogatives]] for the full paradigm. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"To whom?" — dative of [[кто]].

**Second sense, same form: који's short masculine dative** — "to which / to whom (m. sg.)?", the colloquial half of the који doublet (long form [[којему]]), per [[../Grammar/Relative Clauses|Grammar/Relative Clauses]]. Same homograph relationship as [[кога]]'s own two senses: one кому form, кто's paradigm and који's, exactly real BCS *komu*.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*komu → кому directly** — no jer, no fork, matches the real reconstruction letter-for-letter. Trivial.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, no register coloring.

## Related

[[кто]] (nominative), [[кога]] (genitive/accusative), [[цем]] (instrumental). [[чему]] — the parallel "what" cell, same trivial derivation.
