# даожник (daozhnik)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "Debtor" — part of the [[../Texts/Pater Noster|Pater Noster]] set: **како и ми одпуштамо даожницима наши** "as we forgive our debtors." See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Debtor" — one who owes, a person, hence animate: accusative = genitive in both numbers. Built on [[../Words/даог|даог]] "debt" (the noun sense) + the agent suffix *-никъ. The prayer uses the dative plural **даожницима** ("to our debtors").

**Genitive plural даожника is syncretic with genitive singular** (real BCS *dužnik* → *dužnika* has the identical syncretism), kept as inherited texture. No -ов- extension: real BCS *dužnici* likewise pluralizes without it.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*dъlžьnikъ** — listed as a derivative of \*dъlgъ "debt" on the [Wiktionary \*dъlgъ page](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Reconstruction:Proto-Slavic/d%D1%8Alg%D1%8A) (fetched). **Derivation, two established steps:**

1. **Fall of the Jers** ([[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]]): counting from the right — final ъ weak (drops), ь weak (drops), root ъ strong (followed by a weak jer) and vocalizes to **a**. \*dъlžьnikъ → \*dalžnik.
2. **L-vocalization, pre-consonantal** ([[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § 10): the л closes the syllable before ж, so \*dalžnik → **даожник** — the exact same mechanism that turned \*dьlgъ into [[../Words/даог|даог]] "long," applied to the same лж cluster shape.

The initial д is word-initial, not intervocalic, so no lenition: /ˈda.oʒnik/, stress root-anchored on да́-.

## Register

Native formation, but the domain is the toll economy's own ([[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]] Layer II subject matter built from Layer I material) — and the prayer puts it to theological use ("our debtors" = those who owe us, mapped onto forgiveness).
