# којему (kojemu)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The **long-register** masculine singular dative of [[који]]. See [[../Grammar/Relative Clauses|Grammar/Relative Clauses]] for the paradigm. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"To which / to whom (masculine singular)?" — the careful, written-register half of the dative doublet; colloquial speech uses the short form, the same word as [[кто]]'s own dative. See [[../Words/кому|кому]] for the short form and the homograph note; this page records the long form only.

## Etymology

**кој- stem + -ему** — the soft-stem adjective long-form dative, paired with [[којега]]'s -ега as the first lexical examples of the soft -ега/-ему pair ([[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]]). The г lenites ([[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § 12): /ˈkojeɣu/. Real BCS *kojemu* matches.

## Register

The careful/written half of the doublet.

## Related

[[којега]] (long genitive), [[који]] (nominative), [[../Words/кому|кому]] (the short dative, one word serving both paradigms). [[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]] — the soft declension these endings come from.
