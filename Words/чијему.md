# чијему (čijemu)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The **long-register** masculine singular dative of [[чији]] — the counterpart of [[којему]]. See [[../Grammar/Relative Clauses|Grammar/Relative Clauses]]. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"To whose (masculine singular)?" — the careful, written-register half of the dative doublet; colloquial speech uses the short form **чему** (shared with [[кај]]'s paradigm — see the homograph note there). This page records the long form only.

## Etymology

**чиј- stem + -ему**, the soft-stem adjective long-form dative — paired with [[чијега]]'s -ега as the second -ега/-ему pair after [[којега]]/[[којему]] ([[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]]). The г lenites ([[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § 12): /ˈtʃijeɣu/. Real BCS *čijemu* matches.

## Register

The careful/written half of the doublet.

## Related

[[чијега]] (long genitive), [[чији]] (nominative), [[чему]] (the short dative, one word serving both paradigms). [[којему]] — the same cell on који.
