# вас (vas)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Nineteenth individual pronoun entry — the genitive/accusative cell of [[ви]]'s paradigm, and a *fourth* distinct genitive/accusative history, not a repeat of [[мене]]/[[тебе]]'s, [[њега]]'s, or [[нас]]'s — this one driven by functional necessity rather than just sound-change or loss.

## Meaning — Disambiguating a Homophony That Would Otherwise Actively Hurt

Fetched the Proto-Slavic *\*vy* declension table specifically for its oblique row, and it exposed something genuinely different from every pattern established so far: **[[ви]]'s own page already established that ви's nominative is itself the old accusative form, generalized via case-leveling** (*\*wōns*, PBS accusative of *\*jūˀs*, became the nominative). This fetch confirms the direct consequence: Proto-Slavic's **accusative was simply \*vy̑ again** — identical to the nominative, because they're historically the *same form*, not independently converged shapes the way [[он]]'s masculine *\*jь* accusative=nominative pair was. **Using that literally would make subject and object indistinguishable** in a sentence like "you see you" — a real functional problem [[он]]/[[њега]]'s situation never had, since [[он]]'s own nominative was later *replaced* by *\*onъ* anyway, removing any ambiguity risk before it could matter.

**Genitive \*vàsъ generalizing to cover accusative isn't just another instance of "genitive wins by default" — it's the specific fix for that ambiguity.** This connects directly to [[../Grammar/Nominal Morphology|Nominal Morphology]]'s and [[../Grammar/Personal Pronouns|Personal Pronouns]]'s own already-established **functional load** principle (accusative/genitive carry high referential/disambiguation traffic, motivating change where dative/instrumental don't) — now showing up a third time, in a genuinely new shape: here the motivating pressure isn't losing a gender distinction (as with [[../Grammar/Personal Pronouns|Personal Pronouns]]'s ЊЕХ innovation) but avoiding a *subject/object* collision that a literal inherited system would have produced.

## Etymology

**Genitive \*vàsъ** continues the separate suppletive oblique root already established on [[ви]]'s page (*\*vasъ*/*\*vamъ*/*\*vami*, distinct from the nominative's own case-leveled history).

**Accusative was \*vy̑ — genuinely, not just superficially, identical to nominative**, because [[ви]]'s nominative *is* the old accusative (per that page's case-leveling etymology). This is a structurally different situation from both [[нас]] (whose old accusative *\*ny* was a real, separate shape that simply died out) and [[њега]] (whose *\*jь* accusative was identical to a nominative *\*jь* that Slavic itself abandoned in favor of *\*onъ* before ambiguity could arise). вас is the one case among all four genitive/accusative pairs built so far where the "would-be accusative" and the living nominative genuinely coincide *in the modern language itself*, not just in an ancestor form nobody uses anymore — which is exactly why generalizing genitive to accusative here does real communicative work, not just tidying.

**A genuinely striking additional fact from this fetch, worth flagging on its own:** the *\*vy* reconstruction's dative row lists **\*vàmъ** as the full form, but its short/clitic alternate is given as ***\*vy*** — meaning Proto-Slavic's nominative, accusative, *and* short dative clitic could all surface as the identical shape *\*vy* in different contexts. Sisician's modern three-way split — ви (nominative only), вас (genitive/accusative), вама/[[вам]] (dative/instrumental) — resolves a real triple ambiguity that once existed at the Proto-Slavic stage, not an imagined one invented for tidiness.

**Real Slavic comparanda:**

| Branch | Language | Genitive/Accusative | Nominative |
|---|---|---|---|
| South Slavic (liturgical) | Old Church Slavonic | васъ (gen.); вꙑ (accusative, = nominative) | вꙑ |
| South Slavic | Serbo-Croatian | vas (single form, gen.=acc.) | vȋ |
| South Slavic | Slovene | vas | vȋ |
| East Slavic | Russian | вас (gen.=acc.) | вы |
| West Slavic | Polish | was | wy |

**No branch tension found** — every branch checked shows the same generalized genitive-for-accusative outcome, distinguishing nominative from accusative exactly where Proto-Slavic itself didn't. Pan-Slavic, not a Sisician-specific fix.

## Register

Same closed-class status as [[ви]], including the T–V formal-address function (see [[../Culture/Formal Address (ти vs ви)|Culture/Formal Address]]) — вас carries the same dual register as ви itself when addressing a single respected person, not just the genuine plural sense. As with [[нас]], the lack of a distinct clitic form is an open question for [[../Grammar/Clitic Ordering|Clitic Ordering]] to eventually address, not resolved here.

## Related

- [[ви]] — the nominative this word is suppletive with, and the source of the case-leveling history that makes вас's own story possible.
- [[нас]] — the closest structural parallel and clearest point of contrast: both lack a modern clitic form, but нас lost a genuinely separate accusative shape, while вас's "accusative" was never separate from nominative to begin with.
- [[њега]] — same accusative=nominative starting point at the Proto-Slavic level, but a different resolution: [[он]]'s nominative was itself replaced, defusing the ambiguity, rather than accusative needing its own fix.
- [[вама]], [[вам]] — the dative/instrumental counterpart, which resolves the *other* half of Proto-Slavic's *\*vy* triple-homophony.
