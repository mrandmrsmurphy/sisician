# њима (njima)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Thirty-fourth individual pronoun entry — the dative/instrumental cell shared between [[они]] and [[оне]], and a third confirmed instance of the same dative=instrumental merger pattern already established by [[нама]] and [[вама]].

## Meaning

"To them"/"with them," shared between masculine/mixed [[они]] and feminine [[оне]] — per [[../Grammar/Personal Pronouns|Personal Pronouns]]'s table, this is one of the cells where the ЊЕХ innovation did *not* split the paradigm by gender (unlike genitive/accusative, where [[њих]] and [[њех]] genuinely differ). Dative/instrumental plural remains fully shared, exactly as [[он]]'s own page originally noted for the inherited (pre-innovation) system.

## Paradigm

## Etymology — The Third Confirmation of нама's Pattern

**[[њих]]'s own page already fetched the relevant Proto-Slavic plural row for *jь***, and it gives dative **\*jimъ** and instrumental **\*jimi** as genuinely distinct forms — the exact structural twin of *\*namъ*/*\*nami* and *\*vamъ*/*\*vami*. So њима's dative=instrumental syncretism is a third real merger of originally-separate cells, not a coincidence: this is now a **recurring pattern across all three persons' plurals** (1st нама, 2nd вама, 3rd њима), not an isolated quirk of one pronoun.

**The same unresolved -a vowel question applies again, inherited rather than re-derived:** the [[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]] reduces *\*jimъ* to bare **им** (matching the clitic — see below, and confirming the prothetic-n-only-on-full-forms pattern already established on [[их]]/[[њих]]'s pages), not to a stressed form ending in *-a*. [[нама]]'s page already offered the most plausible account — pronoun-to-noun analogical leveling with the productive feminine *a*-stem plural dative/instrumental ending *-ама*, plus Common Slavic's own dual-number dative/instrumental syncretism as supporting precedent — and this page inherits that same account rather than re-investigating it a third time, since it's the identical vowel in the identical morphological slot.

**The short form [[им]] is, again, the clean half of the pair:** it continues **\*jimъ** directly via the ordinary Fall of the Jers, with none of њима's own vowel puzzle — the same relationship [[нам]] has to [[нама]] and [[вам]] has to [[вама]], now confirmed a third time.

**Real Slavic comparanda, checked directly rather than assumed to match:**

| Branch | Language | Dative | Instrumental |
|---|---|---|---|
| South Slavic (liturgical) | Old Church Slavonic | имъ (imŭ) | ими (imi) — kept distinct |
| South Slavic | Serbo-Croatian | njima | njima — merged |
| South Slavic | Slovene | njim (clitic jim) | njimi — kept distinct |
| East Slavic | Russian | им (im) | ими (imi) — kept distinct |
| West Slavic | Polish | im | imi — kept distinct |

**The same branch pattern as [[нама]]/[[вама]], now confirmed a third time via a fresh check rather than assumed:** fetched Slovene's own declension table specifically for this build, and it keeps *njim*/*njimi* genuinely distinct — Serbo-Croatian remains the only branch checked, across all three persons, where dative and instrumental actually merge. This is no longer a single coincidence; it's a real, repeatable fact about how Serbo-Croatian's whole pronoun system behaves, and Sisician follows that same branch-specific choice for internal consistency.

## Register

Same closed-class status as [[они]]/[[оне]]. Per [[../Grammar/Clitic Ordering|Clitic Ordering]], full њима is required after a preposition and for contrastive emphasis; [[им]] takes the dative-clitic slot otherwise. No instrumental clitic exists.
