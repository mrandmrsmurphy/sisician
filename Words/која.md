# која (koja)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Feminine singular nominative of [[који]] — interrogative "which?" and relative "who/that" (feminine singular antecedent). See [[../Grammar/Relative Clauses|Grammar/Relative Clauses]] for the paradigm and the relativization strategy. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Which (feminine singular)?" — **Која каза доје?** "Which house is coming?" — and, as a relative, "who/that" with a feminine singular antecedent: **ел гужва која кричи** "the crowd, which is shouting."

## Etymology

**кој- stem + feminine -а**, continuing the same \*kъjь-rooted further-suffixed form as [[који]] ([Wiktionary који](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D0%BA%D0%BE%D1%98%D0%B8), fetched: Proto-Slavic \*kъjь, PIE \*kʷos + relative particle \*yos; confirmed both interrogative and relative). Unlike the demonstratives' feminine ([[та]] drops the nominative-reinforcing *jь suffix the masculine [[тај]] needed), која keeps the ј throughout — here the glide is root-internal, not a suffix, exactly as real BCS *koja* keeps it. No sound change applies beyond the inherited shape.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary; the relative use is the productive default in every register (contrast [[../Words/куи|куи]], legal-register only, and [[../Words/иже|иже]], liturgical-fossil only).

## Related

[[који]] (masculine singular), [[које]] (feminine plural), [[коју]] (feminine accusative). The paradigm's full grid lives on [[../Grammar/Relative Clauses|Grammar/Relative Clauses]].
