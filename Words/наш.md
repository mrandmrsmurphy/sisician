# наш (nash)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "Our" — the 1st-person-plural possessive, part of the [[../Texts/Pater Noster|Pater Noster]] set: Отче наш, хлеб наш, даоге наши, даожницима наши. The first possessive pronoun built; the full paradigm and its design decision live on [[../Grammar/Possessive Pronouns|Grammar/Possessive Pronouns]]. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Our" — possession/association belonging to the speaker plus others. Agrees with the possessed noun in gender and number: **наш** (m. sg.), **наша** (f. sg.), **наши** (m. pl.), **наше** (f. pl.). **No case forms** — possessives follow the modern adjective system (gender/number only), per the decision recorded and flagged on [[../Grammar/Possessive Pronouns|the grammar page]]. In the prayer: наш with отац/хлеб (m. sg.), наши with даоге/даожницима (m. pl.).

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*našь** — \*nasъ "us" + \*-jь "belonging to" ([Wiktionary \*našь](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Reconstruction:Proto-Slavic/na%C5%A1%D1%8C), fetched, which states the composition directly and gives the full declension). The same \*-jь suffix family already behind the adjective long forms and the demonstratives. The final ь is weak and drops ([[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]]): **наш**, matching real BCS *nȁš* exactly.

## Register

Native, unmarked, closed-class-adjacent (a pronominal, not a content word).
