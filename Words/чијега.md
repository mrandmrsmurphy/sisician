# чијега (čijega)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The **long-register** masculine singular genitive (and animate accusative) of [[чији]] — the ч-root counterpart of [[којега]]. See [[../Grammar/Relative Clauses|Grammar/Relative Clauses]]. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Of whose (masculine singular)?" — the careful, written-register half of the doublet; colloquial speech uses the short form **чега** (shared with [[кај]]'s paradigm — see the homograph note there). Same cells as [[којега]]'s: genitive proper and animate accusative.

## Etymology

**чиј- stem + -ега**, the soft-stem adjective long-form genitive — the second example of the -ега/-ему pair after [[којега]]/[[којему]] ([[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]]'s soft declension). The г lenites intervocalically ([[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § 12): /ˈtʃijeɣa/. Real BCS *čijega* matches.

## Register

The careful/written half of the doublet; colloquial registers use [[чега]].

## Related

[[чега]] (short counterpart), [[чијему]] (long dative), [[чији]] (nominative). [[којега]] — the same cell on који.
