# чијих (čijikh)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Plural genitive of [[чији]] — and, by the animate-accusative-uses-genitive rule, the animate plural accusative. See [[../Grammar/Relative Clauses|Grammar/Relative Clauses]]. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Of whose (plural)? / whom (plural, possessed object)?" — **даожници, чијих каза сијају** "debtors whose houses shine" (genitive), and the animate object case by the usual rule.

## Etymology

**чиј- stem + -их**, the plural genitive of the established family. The х is the modern palatal /ç/ ([[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § 11): **/ˈtʃijiç/**, as in [[../Words/њих|њих]] /ˈɲiç/ and [[којих]] /ˈkojiç/. No other change applies; real BCS *čijih* matches.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, both uses productive.

## Related

[[чији]], [[чије]] (feminine plural), [[чијим]] (plural dat./instr.). [[којих]] — the same cell on који.
