# зло (zlo)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "Evil" — part of the [[../Texts/Pater Noster|Pater Noster]] set: **избави нас од зла** "deliver us from evil." See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Evil" — the abstract noun (from the adjective \*zъlъ "bad" + \*-o). The prayer uses the genitive **зла** after [[../Words/од|од]] ("from evil"). An old neuter noun: masculine singular agreement under the neuter merger.

## Declension (a-declension, hard variant)

| Case | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| Nominative | зло | зла |
| Genitive | зла | зала |
| Dative | злу | злима |
| Accusative | зло | зла |
| Instrumental | злом | злима |

The plural is rare for an abstract of this kind; genitive plural **зала** takes the filler vowel (real BCS *zala* shows the same), which also keeps it distinct from nominative plural зла. The зл- onset cluster is ordinary (obstruent + liquid, per [[../Phonology/Phonotactics|Phonotactics]]).

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*zъlo**, neuter — from \*zъlъ "bad" + \*-o ([Wiktionary \*zъlo](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Reconstruction:Proto-Slavic/z%D1%8Alo), fetched; OCS зъло, BCS *zlȍ*, Russian *зло* all listed). The ъ is weak (followed by a full vowel) and drops ([[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]]): **зло**. No other change applies.

## Register

Native, unmarked, [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]] Layer I (ordinary abstract vocabulary). The Slavonic's own word here is the more colorful лѫкаваго ("the crafty one"); Sisician's од зла follows the Latin *a malo* and the plainer Slavic register instead — a small, deliberate simplification noted on [[../Texts/Pater Noster|the prayer page]].
