# кричати (kričati)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Coined for [[../Texts/Conlang Syntax Test Cases|Conlang Syntax Test Cases]] item 11 ("All the people shouted"), from the Proto-Slavic etymon the user specified. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"To shout, to scream, to cry out": a loud vocal outburst, of anger, fear, joy or simple distance. Not "to weep." Imperfective, so a single completed outcry is not marked by the verb itself. A perfective partner is not coined.

## Conjugation (i-type, present tense)

Same present-tense paradigm as [[../Grammar/Verb Paradigm (Sketch)|радити]], even though the infinitive ends in **-ати**, not **-ити**. This is the real Slavic pattern for \*-ěti verbs that went to -ati after a palatal (compare real *držati*, *slušati*), and the Wiktionary page classes it \*-ě/i-. The class assignment is by present endings, as the Sketch page does. Stress root-anchored on the first syllable:

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | кри́чим | кри́чимо |
| 2nd | кри́чиш | кри́чите |
| 3rd | кри́чи | кри́че |

## Imperative

Consonant-final stem, so **-и / -имо / -ите**: **кри́чи**, **кри́чимо**, **кри́чите**, with the usual homophony of 2sg and 3sg present ([[../Grammar/Imperative|Imperative]]). Jussive **не́ка кри́чим**, negative **немо́ј да кри́чиш**.

## Verbal Noun: кричање

**кричање** "shouting, screaming," built on the infinitive stem крича-. This is the one place the page departs from the i-type rule: радити's thematic -i- jotates its д (раџење), but кричати's stem vowel is -a-, and its root-final ч is already palatal, so nothing jotates. It matches the reconstructed \*kričanьje on the Wiktionary page.

## Past Tense (l-participle + the three-way evidential contrast)

L-participle (Wiktionary's \*kričalъ): **кричао / кричала / кричали / кричале**.

| Form | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| Conservative | је кричао / је кричала | су кричали / су кричале |
| Fused (witnessed) | крјѝчао / крјѝчала | скрѝчали / скрѝчале |
| Dropped (hearsay) | кричао / кричала | кричали / кричале |

**New case for the fusion rules, flagged.** [[../Grammar/Past Tense Fusion and Evidentiality|Past Tense Fusion and Evidentiality]]'s metathesis has only been run on single-consonant onsets (р-, б-, с-). Here the onset is the cluster кр-. Moving је's /j/ behind just the first consonant would give †кјр-, which dips in sonority (obstruent, glide, liquid), so I took the repair to be: **the /j/ goes to the end of the onset cluster**, giving к-р-ј-, which rises all the way to the vowel. That reduces to the old rule for single consonants. The plural су gives **скрѝчали**, since /s/+/k/+/r/ is the ordinary s-plus-rising-cluster onset. Derived by me, not previously decided.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*kričati** "to scream," imperfective, from \*krȋkъ "shout, cry" + the verbal suffix \*-ěti, from Proto-Balto-Slavic \*kreik-, ultimately PIE \*(s)kreik- ([Wiktionary \*kričati](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Reconstruction:Proto-Slavic/kričati)). Cognates the page gives: Lithuanian *krỹkti* "to cry (of birds)" and *krỹkšti* "to shout," Ancient Greek κρίκε "(he) squeals." Descendants listed: Russian *кричать*, Ukrainian *кричати*, OCS *кричати*, Serbo-Croatian *kričati*, Slovene *kričati*, Czech *křičet*, Polish *krzyczeć*, Slovak *kričať*.

**Sisician outcome:** no sound change applies. The k→č before the suffix and the later ě→a after a palatal both happened in Proto-Slavic itself. There's no jer, no х, no intervocalic б/д/г, and no nasal vowel, so the word comes through unchanged: **кричати** /ˈkritʃati/, identical to Serbo-Croatian and OCS. The initial кр- is an ordinary stop+liquid onset.

## Register

Native, unmarked, [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]] Layer I.

## Open Points

- **The noun \*krikъ "shout"** (→ **крик**, with no sound change) is not built.
- **A perfective partner** ("let out one cry") is not coined.
- **"To weep"** is a different verb and not coined here.
