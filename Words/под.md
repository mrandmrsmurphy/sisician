# под (pod)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Static "under" with instrumental, motion "to under" with accusative; whence is [[../Words/испод|испод]]. See [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] for the system and [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

**+ instrumental** = "under" (where); **+ accusative** = "to under" (whither). The whence cell is the compound [[../Words/испод|испод]] + genitive, completing a where/whither/whence set (see [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] § Location Expressions). This alternation was never locative-based, so the locative's loss did not touch it.

## Government

| Case | Sense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Instrumental | under | **под домом** "under the house" |
| Accusative | to under | **под дом** "to under the house" |

## Etymology

Common Slavic **\*podъ**; the weak final jer drops ([[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]]).

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary — outside [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]]' three-layer model, the same footing as [[../Words/одовде|одовде]].
