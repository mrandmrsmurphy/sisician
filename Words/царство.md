# царство (carstvo)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "Kingdom, empire" — part of the [[../Texts/Pater Noster|Pater Noster]] set: **да доје царство твој** "let thy kingdom come." See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Kingdom, empire" — the Slavonic liturgical word for God's kingdom (цѣсарьствиѥ твоѥ in the source text). **Chosen for the prayer over the native \*краљевство** (unbuilt): царство is imperial and Byzantine-flavored, fitting the 1042 turn ([[../Culture/Religion|Culture/Religion]]), and it leaves краљевство free for secular Slavic kingdoms — a deliberate lexical split, recorded on [[../Texts/Pater Noster|the prayer page]]. An old neuter noun: masculine singular agreement under the neuter merger (царство твој, not †твоје).

## Etymology

**Old Church Slavonic цѣсарьство** — the prayer's own word, from цѣсарь "emperor" (Latin *Caesar*) + the abstract *-ьство suffix. Borrowed as a liturgical import and nativized with **а** where the OCS has ѣ: real BCS *цар/царство* "tsar/empire" shows exactly the same a-vowel in this word family, so Sisician's shape is adopted from the living South Slavic comparandum rather than derived from a stated yat rule — flagged honestly, since the vault's own Ekavian baseline would otherwise predict *цесарство. The deeper history of this word's irregular yat reflex is real but not re-derived here.

## Register

**church-slavonic-liturgical**, [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]] Layer III (heaven) — the first political-theological word in the lexicon, and the prayer's clearest case of the "Slavonic/Greek for heaven" channel supplying a word the native layer would have built differently.
