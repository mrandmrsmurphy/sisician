# у (u)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The "in" preposition of the location grid; its source partner is [[../Words/из|из]]. See [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] for the system and [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

**+ instrumental** = "in" (where); **+ accusative** = "into" (whither). The instrumental of location replaced the lost locative through a calque on Latin *in* + ablative, reinforced by the same pattern in [[../Words/за|за]]/[[../Words/под|под]]/[[../Words/над|над]]/[[../Words/пред|пред]] — marked (no other Slavic variety has it) but universal, used in every register (see [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] § у and на: The Latin Calque). Also marks clock time: **у** + accusative, "at [an hour]" (§ Time Expressions).

## Government

| Case | Sense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Instrumental | in (where) | **у домом** "in the house" |
| Accusative | into (whither); at [an hour] | **у дом** "into the house"; **у казу** "into the building" |

## Etymology

Common Slavic **\*vъ** "in" and **\*u** "at, by," which merged in South Slavic into a single **u** (standard account, not separately cited). \*vъ had a final -n before vowels (\*vъn), one of the prepositions whose resegmented -n became the prothetic н- of њега/њему/њим — see [[../Words/он|он]] § Etymology.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary — outside [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]]' three-layer model, the same footing as [[../Words/одовде|одовде]].
