# мени (meni)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Twenty-fourth individual pronoun entry — the dative cell of [[јаз]]'s paradigm, and the page where [[ми]]'s own already-flagged "other sense" (1sg dative clitic) finally gets its proper full-form treatment.

## Meaning — Where ми's Second Sense Actually Belongs

[[ми]]'s page already flagged, right from its own opening, that ми does double duty: 1pl nominative "we" (that page's real subject) and the short dative clitic of [[јаз]], "to me" — cross-referenced there but never actually built out. **This page is that build-out.** мени is the full/stressed dative form; [[ми]] (in this sense only, not its "we" sense) is its short/clitic counterpart — the same full/clitic structure as every other oblique pronoun in this set, just approached from the opposite direction this time (the clitic was named first, in passing, before its own full form existed as a page).

## Etymology

**Already fetched, not re-derived:** [[мене]]'s own page fetched the complete Proto-Slavic *\*azъ* declension table for this whole paradigm's oblique row, and it already gives the dative forms directly: full **\*mьně̀**, short/clitic **\*mi**. This page draws on that same citation for its own case specifically, rather than repeating the fetch.

**A genuinely different stem-vowel history from [[мене]]'s own row, worth stating rather than gliding past:** genitive/accusative *\*mȅne* and dative *\*mьně̀* share the *men-*/*mьn-* oblique root already discussed on [[мене]]'s page, but the dative's own ending, **-ě** (yat), is a separate old case-marking suffix, not a variant spelling of genitive's *-e*. Real Serbo-Croatian keeps this distinction visible in its own stem alternation (*mene* vs. *meni*), which Sisician's мене/мени pair continues directly.

**The short form [[ми]] continues \*mi with no further complication** — unlike [[ме]]/[[те]]/[[га]]/[[је]], all of which needed either a denasalization story or an explicitly-flagged unresolved gap, *\*mi* is already the bare short form in the fetched Proto-Slavic table itself, requiring no intermediate sound change to reach modern ми. This makes мени/ми the *simplest* full/clitic derivation in the whole 1st/2nd singular set, a genuine contrast worth noting after [[га]]'s and [[је]]'s honestly-flagged difficulties.

**Real Slavic comparanda (dative):**

| Branch | Language | Full form | Clitic |
|---|---|---|---|
| South Slavic (liturgical) | Old Church Slavonic | мьнѣ (mĭně) | ми (mi) |
| South Slavic | Serbo-Croatian | meni | mi |
| South Slavic | Slovene | meni | mi |
| East Slavic | Russian | мне (mne) | — (no distinct clitic in the standard language) |
| West Slavic | Polish | mnie | mi |

**No branch tension found** — every South Slavic branch checked keeps a *mьně*-type full form alongside a *mi*-type clitic, matching Sisician's мени/ми pair directly.

## Register

Same closed-class status as [[јаз]]. Per [[../Grammar/Clitic Ordering|Clitic Ordering]], full мени is required after a preposition and for contrastive emphasis; [[ми]] takes the ordinary second-position clitic slot (specifically the dative-clitic position, distinct from the object-clitic position [[ме]] occupies) otherwise — the two clitics [[ме]] and [[ми]] therefore never actually compete for the same slot in [[../Grammar/Clitic Ordering|Clitic Ordering]]'s template, despite both being 1sg.
