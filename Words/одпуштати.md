# одпуштати (odpushtati)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "To forgive (ongoing, habitual)" — the imperfective partner of [[../Words/одпустити|одпустити]], built together with it for the [[../Texts/Pater Noster|Pater Noster]]: **како и ми одпуштамо даожницима наши** "as we forgive our debtors." See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"To forgive" as an ongoing or habitual practice — the aspect the prayer needs for "as *we* forgive" (a standing disposition, not one act). This is the vault's first built imperfective/perfective pair used together in a single text: одпустити (pf.) for the petition, одпуштати (impf.) for the comparison.

## Etymology

**\*otъ- + \*puščati** — the [Wiktionary \*pustiti page](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Reconstruction:Proto-Slavic/pustiti) (fetched) gives \*puščati as \*pustiti's own imperfective. **Native \*šč → шт**, the regular South Slavic outcome (real BCS *otpuštati* has exactly this шт) — the same landing the OCS loan [[../Words/насуштни|насуштни]]'s щ reaches by a different route (loan nativization rather than native development), so the two words end up rhyming in -шт- without sharing a mechanism. The prefix is од- for the same reasons as [[../Words/одпустити|одпустити]] (the vault's own од, the Kajkavian/Slovene lean).

## Register

Native formation; liturgical in the prayer's use.
