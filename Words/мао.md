# мао (mao)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "Small, little, few," filling [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Etymology/Swadesh List]] items 20 ("few") and 32 ("small") with one word, at the user's direction. It replaces an earlier indeclinable quantifier **мало**, which the user retired in favor of an ordinary adjective whose masculine singular is also its adverb. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

One adjective with three overlapping senses, decided by context: **small** (of size: **мао дом** "a small house"), **little** (of amount or degree), and, in the plural, **few** (**мали домови** "few houses" or "small houses"). The plural's "few" versus "small" ambiguity is accepted; no separate construction marks it.

**Adverbial use: the masculine singular, unchanged**, per [[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]] § Adjective to Adverb: **мао** is both "small" and "a little, slightly" (*Ел сунце сија мао* "the sun shines a little"), as [[../Words/светао|светао]] is both "bright" and "brightly."

## Forms

Gender and number agreement only, like every adjective ([[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]] § The Modern System):

| | Masculine | Feminine |
|---|---|---|
| Singular | мао | мала |
| Plural | мали | мале |

No case and no definiteness. A noun that is old-neuter-plural takes the feminine plural, by the neuter merger. Stress is root-anchored on the first syllable throughout (ˈmao, ˈmala, ˈmali, ˈmale).

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*màlъ** "small; little, short, of quantity or duration," a hard adjective, from Proto-Balto-Slavic \*mṓˀlas ([Wiktionary \*malъ](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Reconstruction:Proto-Slavic/malъ)). Descendants listed there include OCS малъ and Serbo-Croatian мал.

**Sisician derivation, two steps, both already established:**

1. **Fall of the Jers** ([[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]]): the only jer is word-final and weak, so it drops: \*malъ → *мал*.
2. **L-vocalization**, coda /l/ → /o/ ([[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § 10): the *l* is now word-final, so *мал* → **мао**.

In мала, мали, мале the *l* is followed by a vowel, never in coda position, so it stays. This is exactly the [[../Words/светао|светао / светла]] pattern, and it is the **first non-participle, non-cluster case** of § 10: no mobile *a* is involved, and the word is a plain root-final -л. **Serbo-Croatian keeps мал**; мао is Sisician's own result of applying § 10 consistently, not a citation, and no other л-final word in the vault tests it.

## Register

Native, unmarked, [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]] Layer I.

## Open Points

- **"Few" with mass nouns** ("little water") has no construction, the same gap as [[../Words/њоги|њоги]] and [[../Words/неколико|неколико]].
- **The comparative** (Wiktionary lists \*mьňьjь) isn't built.
- **A separate word for "small"** (for example from \*drebьnъ, also in the Swadesh row) isn't coined; мао covers it for now.
- **The negative-polarity "barely" sense** of Serbo-Croatian *мало* isn't built.
