# бити (biti)

**Status: exploratory/tentative — the vault's first native verb `Words/` page, and its most grammatically load-bearing word by a wide margin.** Everything here was already decided piecemeal across [[../Grammar/Biti (To Be)|Biti (To Be)]], [[../Grammar/Clitic Ordering|Clitic Ordering]], [[../Grammar/Past Tense Fusion and Evidentiality|Past Tense Fusion and Evidentiality]], and [[../Grammar/Verbal Categories|Verbal Categories]] — this page consolidates it into one lexical entry, the way [[../Words/каза|каза]] did for the noun template. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"To be" — copula, existential, and (in its short/clitic present and l-participle) the auxiliary underlying the ordinary past tense, the pluperfect, and (via the бих-series) the conditional. Real Croatian doesn't have one *biti* paradigm; it has at least four, plus a participle and a historically unrelated conditional auxiliary that happens to share the same root. All are adopted here, unsimplified — there's no more reason to flatten this than there was to flatten the pronoun or declension paradigms.

## Paradigms

**Infinitive**: **би́ти**, root-anchored stress on би-. Per [[../Grammar/Infinitive Loss and Da-Complementation|Infinitive Loss and Da-Complementation]], read as the historical citation form — the infinitive recedes gradually and is gone by Modern Sisician, replaced in verbal-complement contexts by да-constructions.

**Short/clitic present** — the ordinary copula and the perfect-tense auxiliary, unstressed and proclitic (position 2 of [[../Grammar/Clitic Ordering|Clitic Ordering]]'s template):

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | сам | смо |
| 2nd | си | сте |
| 3rd | је | су |

**Long/stressed present ("јесам")** — emphatic, standalone, and the base the negative is built from. Used wherever the clitic can't stand (clause-initially, in isolation, for emphasis):

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | је́сам | је́смо |
| 2nd | је́си | је́сте |
| 3rd | је́ст(е) | је́су |

**Negative present ("нисам")** — fused, not periphrastic; real Croatian doesn't negate *biti* with a separate particle + jesam, it fuses into its own paradigm:

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | ни́сам | ни́смо |
| 2nd | ни́си | ни́сте |
| 3rd | ни́је | ни́су |

**Perfective/future present ("будем")** — used in subordinate temporal/conditional clauses and as the building block for the periphrastic Future II (Futur Exact):

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | бу́дем | бу́демо |
| 2nd | бу́деш | бу́дете |
| 3rd | бу́де | бу́ду |

**L-participle** — built the same way as any i-type verb's ([[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § 10's L-vocalization, stem би- + suffix):

| | Masculine (sg.) | Feminine (sg.) | Masculine (pl.) | Feminine (pl.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| l-participle | био | била | били | биле |

**Conditional auxiliary ("бих")** — historically a fossilized aorist, not a present tense of *биti* at all, and not derived from any of the four paradigms above:

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | бих | бисмо |
| 2nd | би | бисте |
| 3rd | би | би |

## What This Unlocks

биti's own l-participle was the one piece [[../Grammar/Verbal Categories|Verbal Categories]]'s **Pluperfect** category had been waiting on since 2026-09-19: биti's own past (aux + био/била/били/биле) + the main verb's own l-participle. **био сам радио** "I had worked," **био сам респонџао** "I had answered." The бих-series similarly completes the **Conditional**: **радио бих** "I would work," **респонџао бих** "I would answer."

**биti's own perfect can undergo [[../Grammar/Past Tense Fusion and Evidentiality|је-fusion/drop]] like any other verb's**, when биti itself is the main verb rather than the pluperfect's auxiliary — "[he] was," witnessed vs. hearsay, not just conservative.

**Corrected 2026-09-25: an onset-metathesis repair *is* needed here after all.** Fusing је onto био prefixes је's own glide /j/ in front of било's initial /b/, giving /j/+/b/ — the same shape of Sonority Sequencing violation as радити's /j/+/r/ (glides outrank obstruents even more steeply than they outrank liquids), not the already-compliant sequence an earlier version of this page claimed. It repairs the identical way: **бјио** (fem. **бјила**), not †јбио/†јбила. Plural **сбили**/**сбиле** (су+били/биле) are unaffected — /s/+/b/ is an obstruent+obstruent "s+stop" cluster, an independently attested legal onset type ([[../Phonology/Phonotactics|Phonotactics]]), not a violation.

## Verbal Noun: биче (built 2026-09-25)

Per [[../Grammar/Verbal Categories|Verbal Categories]] § "Concrete Formation and Worked Examples" — биti's own root ends in a vowel immediately before the infinitive-forming **-ти**, so there's no consonant for the usual **-en-** stem-extension to attach to; the deverbal-noun suffix \*-ьje lands directly on root+*t*, giving the special **-че** allomorph (т+ј→ч) rather than the general **-ње** most verbs take: **би-ти → биче**, "being, existence, entity."

**Real, well-attested, not a one-off guess**: this is the exact structural twin of real Croatian **biće** (same *bytьje* formation, Croatian's un-merged ć outcome where Sisician's own already-decided ć→č merger gives ч instead), independently confirmed by real Croatian **piće** "drink, beverage" (from *piti* "to drink" — the identical vowel-root-before-*-ti* shape), which shows this isn't specific to *biti* but a real, productive pattern for this stem type.

## Etymology

Common Slavic ***byti***, inherited, not borrowed — real, bedrock-confident Slavic vocabulary, the same evidentiary tier as [[../Words/дом|дом]]'s own Fall-of-the-Jers derivation. All four present-tense paradigms, the l-participle mechanism, and the бих-conditional are copied directly from real Croatian's own attested system; nothing here is a Sisician-specific reshaping. The one genuinely novel layer — [[../Grammar/Past Tense Fusion and Evidentiality|је-fusion/drop evidentiality]] — belongs to the *grammar*, not to биti specifically; биti supplies the auxiliary the innovation operates on, the same way it does for every other verb.

## Register

**native-neutral** — unlike every other word built so far, биti doesn't sit in one layer of [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Etymology/Semantic Domains]]'s three-layer model. It's substrate: the auxiliary machinery underneath Slavic, Dalmatian, and Greek-register sentences alike, native by inheritance and unmarked by register the way a language's most basic verb almost has to be.
