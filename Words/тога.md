# тога (toga)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** First oblique-case entry for [[тај]] — see [[../Grammar/Demonstrative Pronouns|Grammar/Demonstrative Pronouns]] § Etymology — The Oblique Paradigm for the shared mechanism this word and its siblings ([[тому]], [[тим]], [[тих]], [[та]]'s own [[тој]]/[[ту]]/[[том]]) all rest on. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

Genitive of [[тај]] ("of that"), and — per the general Slavic animate-accusative-uses-genitive rule already established on [[../Words/њега|њега]]/[[../Words/дом|дом]]/[[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]]'s нув — also the **accusative** form when [[тај]] modifies or stands for an animate masculine referent. Inanimate accusative instead reuses the nominative [[тај]] itself, no separate form.

## Etymology

**Continues the bare demonstrative root т- directly, not the nominative-specific тај compound.** Unlike the nominative singular masculine (which needed the *jь suffix to survive Havlík's Law at all — see [[тај]] § Etymology), oblique endings are already vowel-initial, so the root's own jer was never word-final and never needed reinforcement. Proto-Slavic **\*togo** (confirmed via Wiktionary's *tъ declension table) continues directly to Sisician **тога**, matching the identical -ога ending already established on [[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]]'s нувога — the same historical *jь-pronoun declension fused onto нув's adjective stem is simply reused here on its own original pronoun. Matches real Serbo-Croatian *tȍgā* exactly.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, same status as every other pronoun entry.
