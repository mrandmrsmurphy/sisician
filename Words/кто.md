# кто (kto)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** First interrogative entry — see [[../Grammar/Interrogatives|Grammar/Interrogatives]] for the full who/what suppletive paradigm this belongs to. Fills [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Etymology/Swadesh List]] item 11. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Who?" — the interrogative person-pronoun, nominative and accusative (inanimate-style syncretism, matching how the personal/demonstrative pronouns already treat this cell).

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*kъto → кто directly, kept deliberately unmetathesized.** The single jer (ъ) sits between к and т, is the only jer in the word, and is therefore trivially the rightmost — weak — and drops, per [[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]]. This gives **кто** directly, the literal Common Slavic order. **Deliberately not** real Croatian's own metathesized *tko* — a real, attested Croatian-internal consonant swap — because unmetathesized кто happens to coincide exactly with modern Russian's own *кто*, and the choice to keep it anyway (rather than following the vault's usual practice of avoiding accidental East-Slavic resemblance) was made explicitly at the user's request.
