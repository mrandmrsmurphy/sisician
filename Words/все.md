# все (vse)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "All"/"everything" — the vault's first quantifier word, fills [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Etymology/Swadesh List]] item 17. Kept the vernacular Kajkavian/Slovene shape rather than the standard Shtokavian one. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"All"/"everything" — a universal quantifier, substantivized (used pronominally, "everything," the same way real Slovene *vse* and real Serbo-Croatian *sve* both are: *to je sve* "that's all," *sve u svemu* "all in all"). Historically the **neuter singular** cell of a full masc./fem./neut. pronominal paradigm (Common Slavic \*vьśь/\*vьśa/\*vьśe, "all" in the way English "whole"/"every" behave adjectivally) — see § A Real Structural Tension below for how this sits against [[../Grammar/Nominal Morphology|Nominal Morphology]]'s neuter merger.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*vьśe → все directly — the simplest, least-mediated derivation in the vault so far.** \*Vьśe already ends in a full vowel (the pronominal/soft-adjective neuter ending *-e*), so unlike the masculine \*vьśь (jer-final), it never carries a jer at all — no Fall of the Jers step, no metathesis, nothing to resolve. **все = вс + е**, letter for letter.

**Why Slovene, and why this isn't really "leaning Slovene" the way [[../Words/јаз|јаз]]/[[../Words/штири|штири]] were:** real standard Serbo-Croatian shows a genuinely different outcome, **сав** — confirmed via Wiktionary as a real, attested **metathesis**, \*vьśь → an intermediate **вас** (*vas*, still listed as an alternate SC form) → **сав** (*sav*, v/s swapped a second time). Real Slovene *ves/vsa/vse* never underwent this and keeps the inherited вс- onset directly. **Crucially, so does Kajkavian itself** — Wiktionary's Serbo-Croatian entry for *vse* documents it explicitly as a live **Kajkavian** form (not just a Slovene one), with two real citations from the Kajkavian poet Dragutin Domjanić ("Mrtvo je vse, kaj smo imali..."). Since [[../Sources/Late Common Slavic|Late Common Slavic]] is already Kajkavian-leaning, все isn't reaching outside Sisician's own vernacular ancestor for an external comparandum the way јаз/штири's "Slovene lean" did — **Sisician's own established dialect base attests все directly**, and сав's metathesis is the outcome Sisician's vernacular substrate simply never underwent. The strongest-grounded "lean" pick in the vault to date.

**One thing deliberately *not* carried over:** real Slovene's own phonetic realization of this word is **[uˈse]**, not [vse] — Slovene /v/ has a documented allophone [u̯] before a consonant, confirmed directly from the Slovene entry's own IPA. Sisician's phonology already treats **в** as a plain fricative /v/ throughout (per [[../Phonology/Modern Inventory|Modern Inventory]] and the hardening sound change on [[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § 9), unrelated to this specific word — so Sisician adopts Slovene/Kajkavian's *lexical* choice (keep the вс- onset) without adopting Slovene's own distinct *phonetic* rule for word-initial в. Pronounced **[ˈvse]**, an ordinary fricative onset.

**Onset cluster, extending an already-catalogued category:** вс- is **obstruent + obstruent** (fricative + fricative), the same abstract cluster type [[../Phonology/Phonotactics|Phonotactics]] already established via [[../Words/кто|кто]]'s /kt-/ (stop + stop) — a new concrete instance of an existing category, not a new rule.

## A Real Structural Tension, Flagged Rather Than Silently Resolved

**Historically, все is the old-neuter-singular cell** of the \*vьśь paradigm — exactly the category [[../Grammar/Nominal Morphology|Nominal Morphology]]'s neuter merger already redirects onto the **masculine singular** everywhere else in the vault ([[../Words/он|он]] absorbing old-neuter "it," [[../Words/тај|тај]]/[[../Words/овај|овај]] absorbing old-neuter "that/this thing"). Taken literally, that rule would predict the substantivized "everything" sense should surface as a masculine form (\*весь or similar), not все.

**Tentative resolution, offered but not treated as decided:** все is presented here as a **frozen, indeclinable substantivized quantifier** that sits outside the live agreement system, rather than as a form still actively competing for a slot in it — the same kind of fossil-survival already established on [[../Grammar/Definiteness|Definiteness]] (the *-l* fossil surviving in fixed high-frequency expressions after the regular system moved on) and structurally similar to how [[../Words/тамо|тамо]] generalized into a fixed, no-longer-analyzed form rather than staying a live paradigm cell. Real Slovene/Serbo-Croatian both support this reading directly — *sve*/*vse* function as frozen, non-agreeing "everything" in exactly the idiomatic contexts cited above, alongside (not replacing) a fully live gendered adjective paradigm (*sav*/*sva*/*sve* agreeing with a noun) that Sisician hasn't built yet.

**Left open, not built:** the full gendered paradigm (весь-type masc., \*вса-type fem., agreeing "all/whole" as in "the whole world") isn't coined — only this one substantivized cell, matching what [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Swadesh List]] item 17 actually asked for.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, no Slavic/Dalmatian/Greek stratification — same footing as the pronoun/demonstrative sets.
