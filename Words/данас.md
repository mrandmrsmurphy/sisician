# данас (danas)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "Today" — part of the [[../Texts/Pater Noster|Pater Noster]] set: **хлеб наш насуштни дај нам данас** "give us this day our daily bread." See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Today" — the bare adverbial form, no preposition (the Slavonic's own дьньсъ is likewise bare). Built on [[../Words/дан|дан]] "day" + the old adverbial *-ьсъ suffix, a fossilized composition, not a live one.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*dьnьsь** — inherited, per the [Wiktionary данас entry](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D0%B4%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B0%D1%81) (fetched), which gives the reconstruction directly and cites Slovene *dánes* and Bulgarian *днес* as comparanda.

**Honestly flagged: the jer arithmetic here is messier than the vault's usual.** Three jers sit in \*dь-nь-sь, and the surface а-vowels of данас do not fall out of [[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Havlík's Law]] mechanically the way they do in [[../Words/дан|дан]] or [[../Words/отац|отац]] (real daughter forms disagree with each other too: Slovene *dánes*, Bulgarian *днес*, BCS *dànas*). Rather than force a derivation, Sisician **adopts the real BCS/Kajkavian shape данас directly** — the same adoption category as [[../Words/штири|штири]] and [[../Words/јаз|јаз]], where a real form is taken whole instead of being re-derived through the chain. The initial д is word-initial, not intervocalic, so no lenition: /ˈdanas/.

## Register

Native, unmarked, [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]] Layer I (time vocabulary).
