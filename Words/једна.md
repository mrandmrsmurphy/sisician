# једна (jedna)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Feminine counterpart to [[један]] — see [[../Grammar/Numerals|Grammar/Numerals]] for the full paradigm. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"One," feminine. Stays purely feminine — old-neuter-singular reference goes to [[један]] (masculine), matching [[../Words/та|та]]'s own precedent.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*(j)edьna → једна — the jer drops here rather than vocalizing**, because the feminine ending *-a* is already a full vowel following it, so ь is never the word's rightmost jer once counted correctly and stays weak throughout. This is the same "mobile *a* disappears when a vowel-initial ending already rescues the form" half of the alternation [[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]] describes — [[један]]'s vocalized *a* and једна's dropped jer are two faces of one paradigm, not two separate facts.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, same status as every other numeral entry.
