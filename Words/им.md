# им (im)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Thirty-fifth individual pronoun entry — the short/clitic dative form of [[њима]], and the third confirmed instance of the [[нам]]/[[вам]] pattern: the clean half of a pair whose full form carries a real unresolved vowel puzzle.

## Meaning

The unstressed second-position clitic covering dative for "to them," shared between [[они]] and [[оне]] — per [[../Grammar/Personal Pronouns|Personal Pronouns]]'s table, this cell is *not* split by the ЊЕХ innovation, unlike genitive/accusative's [[их]]/[[ех]] pair. No instrumental clitic exists; [[њима]] itself covers instrumental in both registers.

## Etymology — The Third Confirmed Instance, Not a Fresh Derivation

**[[њима]]'s own page already established the relevant facts, and this page inherits them directly rather than re-deriving anything:** им continues Proto-Slavic dative **\*jimъ** via the ordinary [[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]] — the same universal sound law already confirmed for [[их]] (genitive/accusative), [[нам]], and [[вам]]. This is now the **third** clitic in the 1st/2nd/3rd-person plural set (after [[нам]], [[вам]]) built on exactly this pattern: clean, regular derivation for the clitic, while the corresponding full form ([[њима]]) carries an unresolved *-a*-vowel puzzle that this page doesn't need to solve, since it doesn't apply here.

**Also worth restating, since им is a second confirmation of it within [[он]]'s own third-person set:** им's bare shape (no њ-) is exactly what the clitics-never-take-njь- generalization already established on [[их]]/[[њих]]'s and [[му]]/[[њему]]'s pages predicts — not a separate coincidence each time, but the same mechanism (prothetic n- arising only in a post-preposition environment clitics never occupy) applying consistently across every cell in this paradigm.

**Real Slavic comparanda (dative clitic):**

| Branch | Language | Form |
|---|---|---|
| South Slavic (liturgical) | Old Church Slavonic | имъ (imŭ) — full form only; no distinct short form found in the sources checked |
| South Slavic | Serbo-Croatian | im |
| South Slavic | Slovene | jim (per [[њима]]'s fetch, distinct from stressed *njim*) |
| East Slavic | Russian | им (im) — no register distinction in the standard language |
| West Slavic | Polish | im |

**No branch tension found for the clitic shape itself** — every branch checked keeps an *im*-type dative form; the real cross-branch tension in this paradigm belongs to [[њима]]'s own dative=instrumental merger question, not to this cell.

## Register — Clitic Placement

Per [[../Grammar/Clitic Ordering|Clitic Ordering]], им occupies the dative-clitic slot in the second-position template, the same slot [[му]] and [[нам]]/[[вам]] occupy across the rest of this paradigm. Full [[њима]] is required after a preposition and for contrastive emphasis; им takes the ordinary slot otherwise.
