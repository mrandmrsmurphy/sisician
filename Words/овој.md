# овој (ovoj)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The proximal counterpart to [[тој]] — see [[../Grammar/Demonstrative Pronouns|Grammar/Demonstrative Pronouns]] for the full paradigm. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"To this (one)" — dative singular feminine of [[ова]].

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*ovoji → овој**, the identical -ој ending family as [[тој]], [[../Words/он|он]]'s њој, and [[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]]'s нувој. Matches real Serbo-Croatian *òvōj* exactly.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, same status as every other pronoun entry.

## Related

[[ова]] (nominative), [[ове]] (genitive), [[ову]] (accusative), [[овом]] (instrumental). [[тој]] — the medial counterpart.
