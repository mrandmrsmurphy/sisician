# љубити (ljubiti)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** A native lexical verb, i-type, matching real Croatian/Serbian/Slovene identically with no fork. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"To love." Real Serbo-Croatian *ljubiti* carries a real, attested wider range too — to be devoted to, to dote upon, to fondle, to kiss — with a perfective partner **пољубити** ("to kiss," по- + љубити) covering the completed-action sense specifically. Sisician inherits the core "to love" sense here; the extended senses are real attested possibilities, not decided one way or the other on this page.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*ľubiti**, ultimately from PIE **\*lewbʰ-** "to desire, love" — the same root Germanic *leubaz*/*lubō* descends from, giving English **love** and German *lieben*/*Liebe* directly. A real, attested distant cognate between Sisician's own native word for "love" and the English word "love" itself, not a coincidence invented for this vault.

Palatalized **\*ľ** regularly gives Sisician's own **/ʎ/** (љ) — the same phoneme already established in the inventory for exactly this historical class ([[../Phonology/Design Principles|Design Principles]] § Palatalization). This is the first *native* word to attest љ from inherited Common Slavic \*ľ; the only other word carrying this letter, [[../Words/фамаља|фамаља]], reaches the identical phoneme by a completely different route (Dalmatian's -LI- cluster rule) — two independent historical pathways landing on one sound, not a coincidence to smooth over.
