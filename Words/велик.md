# велик (velik)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "Big, large, great," filling [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Etymology/Swadesh List]] item 27, from the Proto-Slavic adjective \*velikъ at the user's direction. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Big, large, great": **велик дом** "a big house," **велика каза** "a big building," **велики домови** "big houses." It is the ordinary size word and the natural opposite of [[../Words/мао|мао]] "small."

**Adverbial use: the masculine singular, unchanged**, per [[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]] § Adjective to Adverb: **велик** is also "greatly, largely."

## Forms

Gender and number agreement only, like every adjective ([[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]] § The Modern System):

| | Masculine | Feminine |
|---|---|---|
| Singular | велик | велика |
| Plural | велики | велике |

No case and no definiteness. A noun that is old-neuter-plural takes the feminine plural, by the neuter merger. Stress is root-anchored on the first syllable throughout (ˈvelik, ˈvelika, ˈveliki, ˈvelike).

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*velikъ** "great," a hard adjective, from \*velь (itself from PIE \*welh₁-) plus the adjectival suffix \*-kъ; \*malъ is listed as its antonym ([Wiktionary \*velikъ](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Reconstruction:Proto-Slavic/velikъ)). Descendants listed there include OCS великъ, Serbo-Croatian велик, Slovene velik, Macedonian велик and Bulgarian велик.

**Sisician derivation, one step:**

1. **Fall of the Jers** ([[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]]): the only jer is word-final and weak, so it drops: \*velikъ → **велик**, with no mobile *a* needed.

Nothing else applies. The к is word-final or before a vowel, never between vowels in a way that lenition affects (§ 12 covers only б, д, г); the в and л are plain; there is no cluster and no coda л for § 10. The result is identical to Serbo-Croatian and Slovene.

## Register

Native, unmarked, [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]] Layer I.

## Open Points

- **Dalmatian competitors.** The Swadesh row shows Dalmatian *maur* and *luarg* ("big"), the latter shared with "wide" (item 29). Neither is borrowed; велик stays native for now, and "wide" is still open.
- **The Proto-Slavic alternative \*golěmъ** is also in the Swadesh row and isn't used.
- **The comparative** is suppletive in Proto-Slavic (\*boľьjь, \*vęťьjь per Wiktionary) and isn't built.
- **The derived noun** \*veličina "size" and the related \*velь "great" aren't coined.
