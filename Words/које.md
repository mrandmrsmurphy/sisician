# које (koje)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Feminine plural nominative/accusative of [[који]] — with a real three-way syncretism: it is also the feminine singular genitive. See [[../Grammar/Relative Clauses|Grammar/Relative Clauses]]. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

- Interrogative "which (feminine plural)?" / relative "who, that" with a feminine plural antecedent: **небеса које сијају** "heavens which shine" — the shape an old-neuter-*plural* antecedent like [[../Words/небо|небо]]'s небеса takes, per the neuter merger.
- Feminine singular genitive: "of which (f. sg.)?" — the genitive relative role with a feminine antecedent (ан abstract use: волја, које се пита — the will in question — the role itself, no verb committed).

## Etymology

**кој- stem + -е**, the same pronominal *-y→*-ě merger behind [[../Words/оне|оне]] and the adjective feminine plural ([[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]]'s нуве — the same historical ending family surfacing across paradigms, as on [[../Words/ње|ње]]'s own page). No sound change applies beyond the inherited shape; real BCS *koje* matches.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, both interrogative and relative uses productive.

## Related

[[који]] (masculine), [[која]] (feminine singular), [[којих]] (plural genitive). The syncretism exactly parallels the demonstrative [[../Words/ове|ове]] (f.sg. gen. + f.pl. nom./acc.).
