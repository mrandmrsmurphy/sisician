# када (kada)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "When" — see [[../Grammar/Interrogatives|Grammar/Interrogatives]]. Fills [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Etymology/Swadesh List]] item 14. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"When?"

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*kogъda → regular jer loss (the single jer ъ is the word's only jer, hence weak, drops) gives an intermediate \*когда, with a real medial -гд- cluster.** Real modern Croatian further simplifies this cluster (-гд-→-д-), giving **када** directly — inherited texture, the same "real, recurring, not independently re-derived" category already used for [[../Words/нама|нама]]'s own vowel and [[../Words/седам|седам]]'s epenthetic vowel. No Croatian/Slovene fork adopted here (Slovene shows a more divergent *kdaj*, not chosen).

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, no register coloring.
