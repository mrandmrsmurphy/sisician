# земља (zemlja)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "Earth, land" — part of the [[../Texts/Pater Noster|Pater Noster]] set: **како на небом и на земљом** "as in heaven, so on earth." Fills [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Etymology/Swadesh List]] item 159. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Earth" — the ground, the world, the land. The prayer's pairing word: heaven above, earth below. Feminine e-declension.

**Genitive plural земаља takes an epenthetic a** (real BCS *zemalja* shows the identical inserted vowel in this exact word), copied directly rather than derived. Stress is root-anchored on зе́-.

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*zemľa** — already gathered on [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Swadesh List]] item 159 (OCS землꙗ, Serbo-Croatian земља, Slovene *zemlja*). The љ is the old epenthetic-l/iotation outcome already noted on [[../Sources/Old Church Slavonic|Sources/Old Church Slavonic]]'s own consonant page (\*zemja → zemlja) — no further sound change applies. The Dalmatian cell on the same row (*tiara*, < Latin *terra*) was not borrowed.

## Register

Native, unmarked, [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]] Layer I. Its instrumental **земљом** in the prayer is one of the two showcases of the у/на + instrumental Latin calque ([[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] § у and на: The Latin Calque) — "on earth" is на земљом, not the old locative.
