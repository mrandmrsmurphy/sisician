# чија (čija)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Feminine singular of [[чији]] — "whose" with a feminine possessed noun. See [[../Grammar/Relative Clauses|Grammar/Relative Clauses]]. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Whose (feminine singular)?" — **Чија је каза?** "Whose house is it?", and relative: **даожник, чија каза је велика** "the debtor whose house is big" (чија agreeing with каза, feminine).

## Etymology

**чиј- stem + -а**, continuing \*čьjь via the same structure as [[чији]] ([Wiktionary чији](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D1%87%D0%B8%D1%98%D0%B8), fetched). The ј is root-internal, as in [[../Words/која|која]] — no sound change applies beyond the inherited shape; real BCS *čija* matches.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, interrogative and relative uses both productive.

## Related

[[чији]] (masculine), [[чије]] (feminine plural), [[чију]] (feminine accusative). [[../Words/која|која]] — the same cell on the к-root twin.
