# њоги (njogi)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "Many," filling [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Etymology/Swadesh List]] item 18, kept native Slavic after the user decided against the /mn-/ cluster ([[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § 14). It replaces an earlier indeclinable quantifier **њого**, which the user retired. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Many," of countable things only. **It has plural forms only, and it is never used with mass nouns.** For the singular, see [[../Words/маног|маног]], the adverb "much, a lot", the only surviving singular form.

## Forms

Gender agreement only, like every adjective ([[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]] § The Modern System):

| | Masculine | Feminine |
|---|---|---|
| Plural | њоги | њоге |

No case and no definiteness. A noun that is old-neuter-plural takes the feminine form, by the neuter merger: **њоге сунца** "many suns."

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*mъnogъ** "many," a hard adjective, from Proto-Balto-Slavic \*munagas and late Northern PIE \*monogʰos, a derivation of \*menegʰ- "many, sufficient" ([Wiktionary \*mъnogъ](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Reconstruction:Proto-Slavic/mъnogъ)). Descendants listed there: OCS мъногъ, Serbo-Croatian мно̏гӣ, Slovene mnọ̑g, Czech/Slovak mnohý, Polish mnogi, Russian мно́го.

**Sisician derivation of the masculine plural \*mъnogi**, three steps:

1. **Fall of the Jers** ([[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]]): the ъ is the only jer, followed by full vowels, so it is weak and drops: \*mъnogi → \*mnogi (Croatian *mnogi*).
2. **Initial mn- → ɲ** (§ 14): \*mnogi → **њоги**.
3. **Intervocalic lenition** (§ 12): the г surfaces as [ɣ]: /ˈɲoɣi/.

The feminine plural \*mъnogy goes through the old plural -y → -e change ([[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]] § 4) to **њоге**. I'm assuming the g stays, as in Croatian and Polish *mnogi*, and doesn't show the second-palatalization alternation (\*mъnodzi) the original ending would have produced. That is a leveling I'm choosing, not a citation.

## Register

Native, unmarked, [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]] Layer I.

## Open Points

- **"Much" with mass nouns** (*much water*) has no construction yet.
- **The comparative** (Wiktionary lists \*vęťьjь) isn't built.
- **Derived words** like \*mъnožьstvo "multitude" aren't coined.
