# којој (kojoj)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Feminine singular dative of [[који]]. See [[../Grammar/Relative Clauses|Grammar/Relative Clauses]] for the paradigm. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"To which (feminine singular)?" / relative "to whom, that": the dative relative role with a feminine antecedent — **каза, којој сијам** "the dwelling to which I shine." The dative of the relative typically appears with a preposition (к/од/при/за + којој) as often as bare.

## Etymology

**кој- stem + -ој**, the feminine dative ending of the established pronominal family (the same -ој already behind [[../Words/тој|тој]] and [[../Words/њој|њој]] — and carrying [[../Words/њој|њој]]'s own honestly-flagged *e→o vowel puzzle along with it, since the whole family surfaces with -ој regardless of the reconstruction's expected *-eji). No sound change applies; real BCS *kojoj* matches.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, both uses productive.

## Related

[[који]], [[која]], [[којом]] (instrumental). [[../Words/тој|тој]] — the same -ој on the demonstratives.
