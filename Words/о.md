# о (o)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** A former locative preposition that keeps two senses, now genitive/accusative. See [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] for the system and [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

**+ genitive** = "about, concerning" (the old locative sense, moved to genitive). **+ accusative** = "against, in contact with" — physical contact, unaffected by the locative's loss. See [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] § Former Locative-Governing Prepositions.

## Government

| Case | Sense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Genitive | about, concerning | **о тога** "about that" ([[../Words/тога|тога]]) |
| Accusative | against | **о казу** "against the building" |

## Etymology

Common Slavic **\*o** / **\*ob(ъ)**, from PIE **\*h₁epi** — cognate with Greek **ἐπί** and Latin **ob** (standard etymology, not separately cited).

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary — outside [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]]' three-layer model, the same footing as [[../Words/одовде|одовде]].
