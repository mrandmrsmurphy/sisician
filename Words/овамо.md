# овамо (ovamo)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "Hither" — the proximal *goal* adverb, genuinely distinct from [[../Words/овде|овде]]'s static location, on the same **ов-** root. One cell of [[../Grammar/Demonstrative Adverbs|Grammar/Demonstrative Adverbs]]'s full grid. Not itself a standalone [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Etymology/Swadesh List]] item (like [[../Words/камо|камо]], it fills out the location/goal/source grid rather than a Swadesh slot). See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Hither" — motion toward the speaker ("come here!"), the goal counterpart to [[../Words/овде|овде]]'s pure location. Real Serbo-Croatian keeps *ovamo* distinct from *ovdje* as two living words (*Dođi ovamo!* "Come here!" vs. *Ja sam ovdje* "I am here") — Sisician preserves that same distinction rather than letting овде do double duty, mirroring [[../Words/камо|камо]]'s own distinctness from [[../Words/кде|кде]].

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*ovamo, confirmed via Wiktionary as \*ovъ + \*-mo** — the same ов- root already behind [[../Words/овај|овај]] and [[../Words/овде|овде]], fused with the same adverbial suffix family as [[../Words/камо|камо]] and [[../Words/тамо|тамо]]. Glossed "hither; there(?)"; the real Serbo-Croatian descendant *ovamo* is glossed "hereto; here" — Wiktionary's own systematic Proto-Slavic place-adverb table (the same table cited on [[../Words/тамо|тамо]]'s page) places \*ovamo directly in the "Place (to/in)" row, ов- column, the proximal-goal cell paired with \*tamo (medial-goal) and \*kamo (interrogative-goal).

**A real conditioned allomorph, flagged rather than forced through a false derivation:** \*tamo and \*kamo attach bare *-mo* directly, because their roots (*ta, *ka-) already end in a full vowel. \*Ovamo (and its table-mates \*onamo, \*jьnamo) instead show **-amo**, because their roots (*ovъ, *onъ, *jьnъ) end in a jer, not a vowel. This isn't a vocalized jer the way [[../Words/тај|тај]]'s a came from Havlík's Law (bare \*ovъ+mo, with the jer simply dropping weak the same way [[../Words/кде|кде]]'s did, would give an unattested *\*ovmo*) — it's the suffix's own **-a-**-augmented allomorph, conditioned by a consonant/jer-final root rather than a vowel-final one. Presented here as an observed conditioning pattern across the whole table, not an independently re-derived sound law.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, same as [[../Words/кде|кде]]/[[../Words/камо|камо]]/[[../Words/овде|овде]]/[[../Words/тамо|тамо]] — no Slavic/Dalmatian/Greek stratification applies.

## Related

- [[../Words/тамо|тамо]] — the medial-goal counterpart ("thither"), same -mo suffix family, bare rather than augmented since its root (*ta) is already vowel-final.
- [[../Words/камо|камо]] — the interrogative-goal counterpart ("whither"), same suffix family again, same bare/augmented split logic (ka- is vowel-final).
