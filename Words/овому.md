# овому (ovomu)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The proximal counterpart to [[тому]] — see [[../Grammar/Demonstrative Pronouns|Grammar/Demonstrative Pronouns]] for the full paradigm. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"To this (one)" — dative singular masculine of [[овај]].

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*ovomu → овому**, the identical -ому ending family as [[тому]] and [[../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]]'s нувому. Matches real Serbo-Croatian *òvōm(u)* exactly.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, same status as every other pronoun entry.

## Related

[[овај]] (nominative), [[овога]] (genitive), [[овим]] (instrumental). [[тому]] — the medial counterpart.
