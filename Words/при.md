# при (pri)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** A fossil preposition, antiquated: no longer productive, its work taken over by [[../Words/код|код]]. Survives only in fixed phrases, with genitive since the locative's loss. See [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] for the system and [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

Formerly "at, by, in the presence of, in connection with." Now found only in fixed phrases, and it sounds antiquated even there: [[../Words/код|код]] + genitive (within) and код + instrumental (beside) cover its old spatial senses. The same fate as real Serbian *pri*, which *kod* has largely displaced. Took locative only in the source; the locative's loss moves it to genitive only (see [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] § Former Locative-Governing Prepositions).

## Government

| Case | Sense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Genitive | at hand; in connection with | **бити некоме при руке** "to be at hand for someone"; **при тога** "moreover, in doing so" ([[../Words/тога|тога]]) |

## Etymology

Common Slavic **\*pri**, cognate with Lithuanian *prie* (standard etymology, not separately cited). No sound change applies.

## Register

Archaic: fixed phrases only. Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, outside [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]]' three-layer model.
