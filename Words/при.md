# при (pri)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The idiom-bound "at" preposition, genitive since the locative's loss. See [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] for the system and [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"At, by, in the presence of, in connection with" — now mostly in fixed expressions rather than as a free spatial preposition; [[../Words/код|код]] does the productive spatial work. Took locative only in the source; the locative's loss moves it to genitive only (see [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] § Former Locative-Governing Prepositions).

## Government

| Case | Sense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Genitive | at hand; in connection with | **бити некоме при руке** "to be at hand for someone"; **при тога** "moreover, in doing so" ([[../Words/тога|тога]]) |

## Etymology

Common Slavic **\*pri**, cognate with Lithuanian *prie* (standard etymology, not separately cited). No sound change applies.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary — outside [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]]' three-layer model, the same footing as [[../Words/одовде|одовде]].
