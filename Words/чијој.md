# чијој (čijoj)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Feminine singular dative of [[чији]]. See [[../Grammar/Relative Clauses|Grammar/Relative Clauses]]. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"To whose (feminine singular)?" — **каза, чијој сијам** "the dwelling to whose [window] I shine" — the dative of the possessed noun phrase, чијој marking it. Often appears with a preposition (к/за/пред + чијој).

## Etymology

**чиј- stem + -ој**, the feminine dative of the established family — the same -ој as [[../Words/тој|тој]]/[[../Words/којој|којој]], carrying [[../Words/њој|њој]]'s honestly-flagged *e→o vowel puzzle along with it. No sound change applies; real BCS *čijoj* matches.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, interrogative and relative uses both productive.

## Related

[[чији]], [[чија]], [[чијом]] (instrumental). [[којој]] — the same cell on који.
