# та (ta)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Feminine singular counterpart to [[тај]] — see [[../Grammar/Demonstrative Pronouns|Grammar/Demonstrative Pronouns]] for the full paradigm. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"That" (feminine referent) — the feminine singular of the same medial demonstrative as [[тај]]. Stays purely a feminine agreement form; per [[../Grammar/Nominal Morphology|Nominal Morphology]]'s neuter merger, old-neuter-singular reference goes to [[тај]] (masculine), not here — the same asymmetric pattern already established for [[../Words/она|она]] (stays purely personal/feminine, doesn't absorb old-neuter the way [[../Words/он|он]] does).

## Etymology

**Inherited directly from Proto-Slavic \*ta — no suffixation needed.** Unlike the masculine (which required the *jь reinforcement to survive as an independent word — see [[тај]] § Etymology), the feminine nominative singular of this demonstrative was already a full open-vowel form in Proto-Slavic itself (confirmed via Wiktionary's *tъ declension table: nominative *\*tъ* masc. / *\*ta* fem. / *\*to* neut., all three already distinct at that stage). No jer, no Havlík's Law step, no reduction — та continues *\*ta* unchanged, matching real Serbo-Croatian *tȃ* exactly.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, same status as every other pronoun entry.
