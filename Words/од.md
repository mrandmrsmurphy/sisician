# од (od)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The general "from"; whence member of the "at a person" triple with [[../Words/код|код]] and [[../Words/к|к]]. See [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] for the system and [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"From, away from" a person or place — general source, contrasting with [[../Words/из|из]] (out of an inside) and [[../Words/с|с]] (off a surface). Also the first element of [[../Words/одовде|одовде]] and [[../Words/одтамо|одтамо]].

## Government

| Case | Sense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Genitive | from, away from | **од њега** "from him"; **од дома** "from home" |

## Etymology

Common Slavic **\*otъ**, from PIE **\*éti** (Wiktionary). The final **-d** instead of expected -t is by analogy with the other d-final prepositions [[../Words/над|над]], [[../Words/под|под]], [[../Words/пред|пред]] (the standard explanation). Regressive devoicing gives [ot] before a voiceless consonant.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary — outside [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]]' three-layer model, the same footing as [[../Words/одовде|одовде]].
