# кога (koga)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Oblique cell of [[кто]] — see [[../Grammar/Interrogatives|Grammar/Interrogatives]] for the full paradigm. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Whom?"/"of whom?" — genitive and accusative of [[кто]] (animate-accusative-uses-genitive, the same general Slavic rule already established on [[../Words/њега|њега]]/[[../Words/дом|дом]]).

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic reconstructs genitive as \*kogo, but real modern Croatian shows *koga* — an analogical releveling, not a regular sound change.** The original -go ending gets remade to match the productive o-stem masculine noun genitive singular ending **-a** (the same ending already familiar from [[../Words/дом|дом]]→дома-type declension) — a real, attested analogical pressure, the same *kind* of paradigm-uniformity force already behind [[../Words/њех|њех]]'s own proportional analogy. Sisician follows the real modern outcome, кога, rather than the older bare reconstruction.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, no register coloring.

## Related

[[кто]] (nominative), [[кому]] (dative), [[цем]] (instrumental). [[чега]] — the parallel "what" cell, releveled the same way by analogy with this word.
