# закај (zakaj)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "Why" — see [[../Grammar/Interrogatives|Grammar/Interrogatives]]. Not itself a Swadesh item, but falls out directly from [[кај]]'s own decision. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Why?"

## Etymology

**За- ("for") + [[кај]] ("what") — a completely free consequence of кај already existing, not a fresh coinage of its own.** Matches real Slovene *zakaj* almost letter-for-letter (Slovene's own "why" is built the identical way, за+kaj, since Slovene's kaj already means "what" the way Sisician's does). Real Croatian instead builds its own "why" from *što* (**zašto**), so this word is a small, natural side effect of the vault's own already-decided Kajkavian "what," not an independent Slovene-lean pick.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary, no register coloring.
