# на (na)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The "on" preposition of the location grid; its source partner is [[../Words/с|с]]. See [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] for the system and [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

**+ instrumental** = "on" (where); **+ accusative** = "onto" (whither). Same pattern and history as [[../Words/у|у]]: a calque on Latin *in* + ablative, marked but universal (see [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] § у and на: The Latin Calque).

## Government

| Case | Sense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Instrumental | on (where) | **на домом** "on the house" |
| Accusative | onto (whither) | **на дом** "onto the house"; **на казу** "onto the building" |

## Etymology

Common Slavic **\*na**, cognate with Greek **ἀνά** "up, upon" and Gothic *ana* (standard etymology, not separately cited). No sound change applies.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary — outside [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]]' three-layer model, the same footing as [[../Words/одовде|одовде]].
