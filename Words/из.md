# из (iz)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The whence partner of [[../Words/у|у]]. See [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] for the system and [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Out of, from inside" — motion out of a container or enclosed space. Also the first element of the whence compounds [[../Words/иза|иза]], [[../Words/испод|испод]], [[../Words/изнад|изнад]], [[../Words/испред|испред]], [[../Words/измеју|измеју]].

## Government

| Case | Sense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Genitive | out of, from inside | **из дома** "out of the house"; **из казе** |

## Etymology

Common Slavic **\*jьz(ъ)**, from PIE **\*h₁eǵʰs** — cognate with Latin **ex** and Greek **ἐξ/ἐκ**, Greek's own whence preposition. The initial \*j- is lost and the jer vocalizes to **и** (standard South Slavic outcome, matching BCS *iz*). Regressive devoicing gives [is] before a voiceless consonant.

## Register

Closed-class grammatical vocabulary — outside [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]]' three-layer model, the same footing as [[../Words/одовде|одовде]].
