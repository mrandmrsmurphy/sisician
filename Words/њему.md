# њему (njemu)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** The dative cell of [[он]]'s paradigm, completing the trio [[њега]] (genitive/accusative), њему (dative), [[њим]] (instrumental, still unbuilt) that [[он]]'s own page originally promised.

## Meaning

"To him" — and, per [[он]]'s own page, also "to it" for any old-neuter-singular referent, the same neuter-merger consequence already fully worked out there. This page doesn't re-argue that; see [[он]] § Meaning for the full case.

## Etymology — A Cleaner Cousin of њега's Story, Not a Repeat of It

**The prothetic-n mechanism is [[он]]'s own** (see that page for the citation: a preposition ending in *-n* resegmented onto the pronoun, then generalized to every oblique context). This page's own contribution is the case-specific detail.

**Fetched the *jь* masculine singular declension specifically** (already used for [[њега]]'s build) and confirmed dative **\*jemu** is a *separate* formation from genitive **\*jego** — different suffix vowel (*-emu* vs. *-ego*), not two spellings of the same ending. Unlike [[њега]]'s situation, dative was never in competition with accusative for a shared slot — it's simply its own cell, inherited cleanly, with no genitive-absorbs-accusative complication to untangle.

**Double-checked against real attested Old Church Slavonic text, not just the reconstruction:** the same "Declension of the third personal pronoun" table already used for [[га]]'s build gives the masculine singular dative as a single form, **емоу** (jemu) — with no separate short/enclitic alternate shown there, unlike accusative's real attested *и*/*нь* pair. This matters for what it implies about [[му]]: there's no separately-attested ancient short dative form to point to (the way [[ме]]/[[те]] point to *\*mę̃*/*\*tę̑*, or [[их]] points to attested OCS *ихъ*) — whatever [[му]] is, it has to come from reducing *jemu* itself.

**This actually gives [[му]] a more plausible derivation than [[га]]'s, worth stating as a real point of contrast rather than lumping them into the same "unresolved" bucket:** *\*jemu* losing its unstressed initial syllable (*je-*) is a straightforward, ordinary kind of reduction — the ***-mu*** that's left over is already exactly [[му]]'s shape, with no further vowel-quality puzzle to solve the way [[га]]'s *o→a* step was. This is presented as a plausible, structurally simple hypothesis, not a citation — no consulted source states the reduction outright — but it doesn't carry the same specific unresolved gap [[га]]'s page had to flag.

**Real Slavic comparanda:**

| Branch | Language | Form |
|---|---|---|
| South Slavic (liturgical) | Old Church Slavonic | (н)емоу ((n)jemu) |
| South Slavic | Serbo-Croatian | njȅmu; clitic mu |
| South Slavic | Slovene | njemù; clitic mu |
| East Slavic | Russian | ему (jemu), position-conditioned нему (njemu) |
| West Slavic | Polish | jemu, niemu |

**No branch tension found** — prothetic-n distribution here matches [[њега]]'s and [[он]]'s own findings exactly, pan-Slavic rather than South-Slavic-specific.

## Register

Same closed-class status as [[он]]. Per [[../Grammar/Clitic Ordering|Clitic Ordering]], full њему is required after a preposition and for contrastive emphasis; [[му]] takes the dative-clitic slot otherwise, the same slot occupied by [[ми]] (dative sense)/[[ти]] (dative sense) for 1st/2nd person — [[га]]/[[ме]]/[[те]] occupy the separate object-clitic slot, so му never competes with them for position.

## Related

- [[му]] — the short/clitic form; unlike [[га]], plausibly a simple unstressed-syllable reduction of *jemu* rather than an unresolved puzzle.
- [[он]] — the nominative this word is suppletive with, and the source of the prothetic-n mechanism this page relies on.
- [[њега]] — the genitive/accusative counterpart; genuinely separate formation (*-ego* vs. *-emu*), not overlapping with this cell the way genitive/accusative overlapped with each other.
- [[њим]] — the remaining unbuilt cell of он's paradigm (instrumental).
