# вама (vama)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** Thirty-second individual pronoun entry — the dative/instrumental cell of [[ви]]'s paradigm, and the exact 2nd-person structural twin of [[нама]], down to the same specific unresolved vowel question.

## Meaning

"To you"/"with you" (plural, or formal singular per [[ви]]'s own T–V function) — dative and instrumental sharing one form, per [[../Grammar/Personal Pronouns|Personal Pronouns]]'s table, the same dative=instrumental pairing [[нама]] already established as a genuine departure from the genitive=accusative syncretism pattern seen everywhere else in this set.

## Etymology — The Same Story as нама, One Person Over

**[[ви]]'s own page already fetched the relevant Proto-Slavic forms**, and — checked specifically for this build rather than assumed to match [[нама]] — they show the identical structure: dative **\*vàmъ** and instrumental **\*vàmi**, genuinely distinct historical cells (different endings, *-ъ* vs. *-i*), exactly paralleling *\*namъ*/*\*nami*. So вама's dative=instrumental syncretism is a real merger of two originally-separate forms, not an inherited pattern — the same conclusion [[нама]]'s page already reached, now confirmed to extend rather than being a one-off.

**The same unresolved -a vowel question applies here, not re-investigated separately:** the [[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]] reduces *\*vamъ* to bare **вам** (matching the clitic — see below), not to a stressed form ending in *-a*, and instrumental *\*vami* doesn't produce that ending either. [[нама]]'s page already flagged this exact gap and offered its most plausible resolution — real Slavic pronoun-to-noun analogical leveling with the productive feminine *a*-stem plural dative/instrumental ending *-ама* (e.g. *женама*), plus Common Slavic's own dual number's independently-attested dative/instrumental syncretism as supporting precedent — both recalled general knowledge, not freshly cited. This page inherits that same account rather than re-deriving a separate one, since it's the identical vowel in the identical morphological slot one person over.

**The short form [[вам]] is, likewise, the clean half of the pair**: it continues **\*vamъ** directly via the ordinary [[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]], the same universal sound law, with none of вама's own vowel puzzle — the same relationship [[нам]] has to [[нама]].

**Real Slavic comparanda:**

| Branch | Language | Dative | Instrumental |
|---|---|---|---|
| South Slavic (liturgical) | Old Church Slavonic | вамъ (vamŭ) | вами (vami) — kept distinct |
| South Slavic | Serbo-Croatian | vama | vama — merged |
| South Slavic | Slovene | vam | vami — kept distinct |
| East Slavic | Russian | вам (vam) | вами (vami) — kept distinct |
| West Slavic | Polish | wam | wami — kept distinct |

**The same branch tension as [[нама]], not a new one:** Serbo-Croatian is again the only branch checked where dative and instrumental actually merge — Slovene, Russian, Polish, and OCS itself all keep вамъ/вами distinct, exactly mirroring намъ/нами's own pattern. Sisician's вама follows the same acknowledged Serbo-Croatian-specific outlier as [[нама]], for consistency within the paradigm rather than a freshly re-argued choice.

## Register

Same closed-class status as [[ви]], including the T–V formal-address function (see [[../Culture/Formal Address (ти vs ви)|Culture/Formal Address]]) — вама carries the same dual register as [[ви]] and [[вас]] when addressing a single respected person. Per [[../Grammar/Clitic Ordering|Clitic Ordering]], full вама is required after a preposition and for contrastive emphasis; [[вам]] takes the dative-clitic slot otherwise. No instrumental clitic exists.
