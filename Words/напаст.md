# напаст (napast)

**Status: exploratory/tentative.** "Temptation, trial, peril" — part of the [[../Texts/Pater Noster|Pater Noster]] set: **и не веди нас у напаст** "and lead us not into temptation." See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Meaning

"Temptation, trial, peril" — the Slavonic's own word (напасть) for the prayer's πειρασμός, covering both "temptation" and the broader "trial, ordeal" sense. Feminine i-declension (consonant-final, abstract — the same class as [[../Words/читвот|читвот]]).

## Declension (i-declension, feminine)

Following [[../Words/читвот|читвот]]'s concrete i-declension pattern ([[../Grammar/Declension Classes|Declension Classes]]):

| Case | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| Nominative | напаст | напасти |
| Genitive | напасти | напастију |
| Dative | напасти | напастима |
| Accusative | напаст | напасти |
| Instrumental | напасти | напастима |

The genitive plural **-ију** (напастију) follows читвот's functional-load-motivated extended form (real Croatian *kostiju*-type); the plural is rare for an abstract of this kind in any case. The prayer uses the accusative singular, which is identical to the nominative: **у напаст** (motion into, per [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]]).

## Etymology

**Proto-Slavic \*napastь**, feminine i-stem — \*na- + \*pastь, "downfall, peril, scourge" ([Wiktionary \*napastь](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Reconstruction:Proto-Slavic/napast%D1%8C), fetched; OCS напасть, Serbo-Croatian *nápast*, Russian *напа́сть* all listed). The final ь is weak and drops ([[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]]): **напаст**. No other change applies — the -паст- cluster is untouched, and there is no intervocalic stop to lenite.

## Register

Native in form, but the specific "temptation" sense belongs to the liturgical register — the everyday senses ("peril, scourge") are the word's Layer I core, the prayer's sense a Layer III application of it.
