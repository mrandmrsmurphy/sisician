# Sisician

Splash page — hub for the con-history and conlang. This is a fictional language and alternate-history project: a South Slavic language reshaped by a long-vanished Dalmatian Romance elite.

## The Pitch

> **Slavic grammar + Romance vocabulary + Orthodox Christian civilization.**

English is fundamentally Germanic, but centuries of French/Latin influence made its vocabulary so heavily Romance that it's sometimes mistaken, superficially, for a Romance language. Sisician reverses the intuition: a South Slavic language whose vocabulary was reshaped by **Dalmatian Romance** (the now-extinct Romance language of the eastern Adriatic), plus a Greek/Church Slavonic learned stratum from its Orthodox turn.

> **At first glance: Romance. After examination: unmistakably Slavic.**

The one-line historical engine: a Dalmatian-speaking noble household is stranded in the Slavic interior after backing the losing side in a succession war around 1000 AD → the household becomes a dynasty → the dynasty becomes a city → the city becomes a polity → the polity becomes a culture → the culture becomes a language. That is Sisician.

The governing design principle: **every weird feature should have a historical reason.** The modern language should feel *discovered*, not invented. See [[Conlang Workflow]] for the full method and what not to do.
