# Phonotactics

Decided 2026-09-23, prompted by a question about which onset clusters (два "two," etc.) are actually legal before starting numeral coinage. Not an exhaustive syllable inventory — a lightweight target sketch, the same "held loosely" spirit as [[Modern Inventory]] itself, giving enough structure to check a new word against rather than blocking every future coinage on a full formal grammar. See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## Syllable Template

**(C)(C)(C) V (C)(C)** — standard South Slavic shape, nothing exotic. Onsets can stack up to three consonants; codas are more restricted (see below). The nucleus is one of the five vowels, or — per [[Modern Inventory]] § Syllabic Sonorants, already decided — a syllabic /r/ (rarely /l/) standing in for a vowel entirely, register-conditioned (colloquial bare syllabic-r vs. standard written epenthetic-**a**; not re-explained here, just cross-referenced).

## Onset Clusters

Already-attested evidence from real built words, not invented from a blank slate:

- **Obstruent + liquid/glide** — the most common South Slavic cluster type (тр-, др-, кр-, гл-, пл-, тв-, св-, дв-…). "Two" (not yet coined) will be this pattern, /dv-/, whenever it's built.
- **Obstruent + nasal** — real and already attested: [[мном]] /mn-/ ("with me"), a genuine two-obstruent-adjacent... no, nasal-initial cluster, matching real Croatian *mnom* exactly.
- **s/š + obstruent** — already attested: [[скинија]] /sk-/. Real South Slavic also freely allows sm-, sp-, st-, sv- (cf. real BCS *smrt* "death," cited on [[../Culture/Sociolinguistic History|Culture/Sociolinguistic History]] as the eye-spelling tradition's own worked example — not yet a coined Sisician word, but real evidence the cluster type itself is native and unremarkable).
- **Three-consonant onsets** (s + obstruent + liquid, e.g. real Croatian *stvar*, *spric-*) — permitted, rarer, not yet exemplified by a built word.
- **Obstruent + obstruent (stop + stop)** — added 2026-09-23, confirmed by [[../Words/кто|кто]] "who" (/kt-/, real and unremarkable South Slavic, cf. Russian *кто* too, just not previously catalogued here since no built word had shown it yet).

## Coda

**Single-consonant codas are unrestricted and the norm** (дом, тим, ту, etc.). **Consonant-cluster codas are more restricted than onsets** — the general South Slavic pattern, and the real motivation behind the Fall of the Jers' own "mobile *a*" alternation ([[../Etymology/Fall of the Jers|Fall of the Jers]]): a root that would otherwise end in an illegal or awkward cluster gets a jer that vocalizes to **a** in exactly the forms where it would otherwise be stuck word-final, and drops where a vowel-initial ending already rescues it. Not a new rule — this page is just naming *why* that alternation exists phonotactically, rather than only stating that it happens.

**No general word-final obstruent devoicing.** This is a real, meaningful design choice, not a default: Russian/Polish (East/West Slavic) do devoice word-finally (*grad → [grat]*); real Serbo-Croatian/Kajkavian does **not** (*grad* stays [grad]). Sisician has consistently followed the BCS/Kajkavian side of every such fork already (rejected Russian-style hard/soft consonant pairing on [[Design Principles]], Kajkavian-specific choices throughout [[../Sources/Late Common Slavic|Late Common Slavic]]) — keeping final voicing here isn't a fresh decision so much as the same standing choice applied to one more feature. [[../Words/дом|дом]] is already live confirming evidence: it stays дом, not **†том**.

## Word Minimums

Monosyllables are fully legal words on their own (он, дом, ту, тим) — nothing here requires a minimum-two-syllable word shape.

## Vowel + Glide Codas

**Clarified 2026-09-24, see [[Modern Inventory]] § "No Separate Diphthong Inventory" for the full statement**: a coda /j/ after most vowels (aj, ej, oj, uj) is just an ordinary V+C coda, not a special diphthong category — already attested for aj/oj ([[../Words/тај|тај]], [[../Words/ној|ној]], [[../Words/денајр|денајр]], etc.), and ej/uj are equally legal even though no word has needed them yet. **Coda -ij is the one real exception, not yet attested and tentatively excluded**: [[../Words/који|који]]/[[../Words/скинија|скинија]]'s own -ији-/-ија- sequences were briefly (and wrongly) cited as -ij- coda evidence, then caught and corrected — real onset-maximizing syllabification puts that ј in the *onset* of the following syllable (*ko-ji*, *ski-ni-ja*), not in coda after *i* — and /i/+/j/ are close enough in articulation that a genuine coda sequence would be barely distinguishable from plain long [iː], the same kind of vowel-glide clash already behind the total ban on /w/ below. **No vowel+/w/ coda is ever legal**, full stop — Dalmatian loans that would introduce one (a w-final diphthong or onset-glide) have their /w/ hardened to a full consonant /v/ instead, per [[../Sources/Dalmatian|Dalmatian]]'s *mwart→мварт* and [[../Words/читвот|читвот]]'s *cituot→читвот* precedent. This resolves half of the second open item below (the glide case specifically); consonant-cluster simplification at loan boundaries more generally is still open.

**[[../Words/денајр|денајр]]'s own -јр coda cluster is the vault's first attested coda cluster of any kind**, worth naming directly rather than letting it slide past unremarked — this page has said "coda clusters are more restricted than onsets" since 2026-09-23 without a single example to anchor that claim. Glide+liquid (**j+r**) is **sonority-consistent**: codas generally prefer sonority to *fall* moving away from the vowel nucleus (glides rank just below vowels in sonority, liquids just below glides), so -jr falls cleanly in the expected direction — well-formed by the same general principle already implicit in real BCS's own liquid-final coda clusters (*vrt*, *smrt*, *prst*, already cited on [[../Culture/Sociolinguistic History|Culture/Sociolinguistic History]] and this page's own onset section), even though none of those happen to be glide-initial the way -jr is. Not independently attested in real BCS as a specific cluster type — this is Sisician's own first data point for the still-unstated coda-cluster inventory, not a citation.

## Open Items

- No formal statement yet of which specific three-consonant onsets beyond s+obstruent+liquid are legal, or of a full coda-cluster inventory — денајр's -јр (§ above) is the first concrete data point, not a complete answer.
- Whether coda -ij is a hard ban or just strongly dispreferred (§ above) isn't settled — no word has tested it either way yet.
- Whether any *consonant-cluster* simplification (as opposed to the now-resolved glide case, above) happens at Dalmatian-loan boundaries hasn't been checked against this page yet — worth doing whenever the next Dalmatian loan is coined.
