**When citing a real language as a model for how some Sisician feature should behave, check that language's actual genetic/typological classification before reaching for a geographically- or thematically-plausible-sounding analogy.** [[reference_construction_workflow]]'s Definiteness thread (2026-09-26) got this wrong: Dalmatian's article was modeled as postposed, reasoning that Dalmatian sits geographically inside the Balkan Sprachbund zone (Romanian/Bulgarian/Macedonian/Albanian, all of which have postposed articles). That reasoning skipped a stronger, more basic fact sitting one step earlier: Dalmatian is classified Italo-Dalmatian, part of Western Romance — the *opposite* group from the Balkan-Sprachbund postposing languages — and every one of its actual relatives (Italian, Venetian) has an ordinary preposed article. The user caught this, not a self-check; it cost a full rebuild of both the new material and an already-published 2026-09-19 decision.

**Why:** geographic proximity is a real, valid channel for language contact effects (this vault uses it correctly elsewhere — Kajkavian's dialect geography, Church Slavonic's liturgical spread), but it's a weaker signal than genetic classification for a feature's *default*, un-contact-driven shape. A language's family membership predicts its baseline grammar; contact can override that, but only when there's a real, specific contact mechanism argued for, not just adjacency on a map. The error here was treating adjacency as sufficient grounds on its own, without first checking whether the feature in question is even attested as a contact-diffused one for the specific neighbors involved.

**How to apply:** before extending Sisician via "language X near Sisician's setting does Y, so Dalmatian/Sisician plausibly does Y too" — first check what X's own genetic family actually is, and whether X's closest real relatives share Y or not. If the closest relatives don't share Y, geographic proximity alone isn't enough to justify the extension; either find a specific, named contact mechanism, or don't make the claim. This applies most directly to future [[project_distancing_from_slavic_neighbors]] work, where the temptation to reach for "this real Balkan-area feature" analogies will recur.
