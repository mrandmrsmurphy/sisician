**2026-09-26: the user flagged that Sisician reads as "too similar to Croatian and Slovenian"** — the lexical three-layer register model (Slavic/Dalmatian/Greek) was working as designed, but grammar and phonology were mostly adopted from real Croatian/Slovene wholesale, with no Sisician-specific innovation layered on top (recent coinages like [[../../Words/кто|кто]], [[../../Words/тамо|тамо]], [[../../Words/все|все]] all cite the nearest real Slavic form directly). The user's own comparandum: **Portuguese deliberately sought to distinguish itself from Spanish** — a *conscious*, elite-driven distancing project, not organic accident.

**Two fronts opened, in this priority order (user's own call):**

1. **A productive postposed definite article — [[../../Grammar/Definiteness|Grammar/Definiteness]] § Two Article Channels, decided and under construction as of 2026-09-26.** Revives [[../../Grammar/Definiteness|Definiteness]]'s existing -l fossil's *fuller* pre-erosion Dalmatian article forms (masc. el/li, fem. la/le) from written legal/notarial records, generalizing them onto ordinary nouns starting Early Modern Sisician — a real, deliberate elite revival project, not the same organic channel as the -l fossil. Coexists with [[../../Grammar/Adjective Agreement|Adjective Agreement]]'s existing long-form/short-form adjective definiteness system for centuries; **user specified the article only fully displaces the adjective system's definiteness function in the 1800s** — recent, not immediate. This is genuinely mid-construction: the article forms and gender/number mapping are decided, but the case-attachment mechanics (a real vowel-hiatus problem for singular -ел/-ла after vowel-final case endings) and the 1800s adjective-repurposing mechanism are both flagged open, not resolved.
2. **Categorical (not just register-conditioned) phonological erosion of "no-Latin-pathway" sounds — discussed 2026-09-26, not yet built.** The only two sounds flagged in the vault as having zero Vulgar Latin/Dalmatian pathway are /x/ (no velar/uvular fricative in VL/Dalmatian at all) and syllabic /r/,/l/ (no Latin syllable-nucleus-consonant pattern) — both currently just *register variation* ([[../../Phonology/Modern Inventory|Modern Inventory]]'s [ç]/[x]/[χ] three-way spread; [[../../Culture/Sociolinguistic History|Sociolinguistic History]]'s eye-spelling colloquial-vs-careful split). User wants these turned into a real *completed* historical sound change over ~200 years: standard Modern Sisician stabilizes at [ç] only (not a spectrum) and always uses the epenthetic vowel (not colloquial-only) — old forms surviving solely in a genuine non-standard/rural dialect plus the mockery-performance register, not as live standard-register options. **Not started — this is the next task once the article's own mechanics settle.**

**Why:** the user's explicit standard is that "basically every sentence" should read as visibly non-Croatian/non-Slovene, not just individual vocabulary items — a structural/grammatical distancing, not another lexical layer.

**How to apply:** treat this as a standing lens on future grammar/phonology work, not a one-off task — check whether a new grammar or phonology decision could lean into this distancing direction rather than defaulting to the nearest real Croatian/Slovene form, the same way [[../../Words/все|все]]'s own Kajkavian citation should now prompt asking "is there a *more* distinctive option" rather than stopping at the first real comparandum found. Don't retrofit this onto already-published lexicon retroactively without being asked — this governs new grammar-level decisions and the two open fronts above, not a mandate to revise all ~30 already-built Words/ pages.
