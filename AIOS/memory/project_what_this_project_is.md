This is an **Obsidian vault** for **Sisician**, a constructed language (*conlang*) with an accompanying alternate-history dossier. It is not a software project — no build commands, tests, or CI.

**The core idea:** Sisician is a fundamentally **South Slavic language** whose vocabulary was reshaped by centuries of contact with **Dalmatian Romance** (the now-extinct Romance language of the eastern Adriatic) and, later, Greek/Church Slavonic — the same relationship English has to Germanic-grammar-plus-French/Latin-vocabulary, but with Slavic in the Germanic role and Dalmatian in the French role. The one-line engine: a Dalmatian-speaking noble household, stranded in the Slavic interior after backing the losing side of the c.1000 AD Croatian succession crisis, becomes a dynasty, then a city, then a polity, then a culture, then a language.

**Governing design principle:** *every weird feature should have a historical reason.* Ask "what happened between 1050 and 1400 that would produce this?" rather than designing a feature first and retrofitting an etymology. The result should feel discovered, not invented. Full method, six-phase construction order, and an explicit "what not to do" list: [[../../Conlang Workflow|Conlang Workflow]] (also see [[reference_construction_workflow]]).

**Status as of 2026-09-18:** the con-history and all design principles have been harvested from the original brain-dump ("The Core Idea.md") into the vault proper — see [[../../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page and [[../site-map|site-map.md]] for the full page list. **No phonology, grammar, or lexicon has been constructed yet** — Phase 1 of the workflow has not started. The native endonym and every proper name (people, polity, dynasty, founder, title) are deliberately still undecided — see [[reference_naming_status]].

**Why:** The owner is building a historically-motivated conlang, not a token-mixing exercise. Every new word or grammatical rule must be checked against this premise and against the con-history, not just judged plausible-sounding on its own.

**How to apply:** Don't invent phonology, grammar, or vocabulary ahead of the workflow phase that's supposed to produce it (see [[reference_construction_workflow]]). Don't invent the endonym or proper names before the sound changes exist. When the user makes a foundational decision not yet recorded here or in the `Culture/`/`Grammar/`/`Phonology/`/`Etymology/` pages, update the relevant page immediately.
