Sisician must be built historically, in order, not top-down. Full detail, the ASCII structural diagram, and the complete "what not to do" list live in [[../../Conlang Workflow|Conlang Workflow]] — read that page before starting any construction work. Condensed phase list:

1. **Pre-founding linguistic base** — the South Slavic dialect c. 1000, the Dalmatian source variety, Church Slavonic/Greek, Hungarian contact, Latin writing tradition. No modern vocabulary yet.
2. **Old Sisician (c. 1000–1150)** — early bilingual speech, initial loans, phonological adaptation, early orthography.
3. **Historical sound changes** — a real sequence; every modern irregularity should descend from one.
4. **Middle Sisician (c. 1150–1350)** — the hybrid emerges: extensive Romance penetration, Greek/Slavonic religious vocabulary, Hungarian political vocabulary, morphological integration.
5. **Early Modern Sisician (c. 1350–1650)** — Dalmatian dies as a community language; Romance loans become opaque/native.
6. **Modern Standard** — only now: standardized grammar, orthography, literary vocabulary, dialects, registers.
