# Sisician

A constructed language (conlang) and alternate-history worldbuilding project, kept as an [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) vault: **a South Slavic language whose vocabulary was reshaped by a long-vanished Dalmatian Romance elite.**

> Slavic grammar + Romance vocabulary + Orthodox Christian civilization.
>
> At first glance: Romance. After examination: unmistakably Slavic.

English is fundamentally Germanic, but centuries of French/Latin contact made its vocabulary so heavily Romance that it's sometimes mistaken, superficially, for a Romance language. Sisician reverses that: a South Slavic language (grammar, core vocabulary, phonological bones) whose lexicon was reshaped by **Dalmatian Romance** — the real, now-extinct Romance language once spoken on the eastern Adriatic coast — plus a later Greek/Church Slavonic learned stratum. The one-line historical engine: a Dalmatian-speaking noble household is stranded in the Slavic interior after backing the losing side in a succession war around 1000 AD, and over the following centuries the household becomes a dynasty, the dynasty a city, the city a polity, the polity a culture, the culture a language.

The governing design principle is that **every unusual feature should have a historical reason** — the language should feel *discovered*, not invented. Every sound change, grammatical quirk, and word coinage is checked against real comparative Slavic and Romance linguistics (Proto-Slavic, Old Church Slavonic, Serbo-Croatian, Slovene, Vulgar Latin, real attested Dalmatian, and Sardinian as a typological stand-in where Dalmatian itself is too poorly attested) rather than invented from whole cloth.

**Start here:** [Sisician.md](Sisician.md) — the hub page, with the full pitch and links to every major section.

## Structure

- **`Culture/`** — the con-history and worldbuilding: the founding, the dynasty, religion, political identity, sociolinguistic history
- **`Sources/`** — the real ancestor/contact languages Sisician is derived *from* (Vulgar Latin, Dalmatian, Late Common Slavic, Old Church Slavonic)
- **`Phonology/`** — Sisician's own sound inventory, phonotactics, orthography
- **`Grammar/`** — morphology and syntax, one page per topic (pronouns, numerals, negation, verbal categories, etc.)
- **`Etymology/`** — sound-change chains, comparative Swadesh data, semantic-domain layering
- **`Words/`** — the lexicon, one file per word (filename = the word's own Cyrillic spelling)
- **`logs/log.md`** — an append-only session log of what's been built, when, and why

## Status

Actively under construction. Phonology, core grammar (pronouns, demonstratives, adjectives, verbal system), and a growing lexicon (pronouns, numerals one through ten, negation, and a handful of content words) are built out; a full sound-change derivation chain connects the modern target back to the real historical sources.
