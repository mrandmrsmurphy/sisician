# Interrogatives

See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page. Fills [[../Etymology/Swadesh List|Etymology/Swadesh List]] items 11–15.

## Method: Real Irregularity, Not a Correlative Chart

Real Slavic interrogative systems are *not* the clean, symmetric k-series correlative grids many conlangs default to — they're a pile of independently-evolved forms that happen to mostly start with a reflex of the same PIE stem, riddled with suppletion, fossilized old case endings, and words that took entirely separate historical paths. Sisician's own system leans into that texture deliberately, at the user's own request, rather than regularizing it.

## Who and What: Shared Suppletive Paradigm, Two Different Irregularities

Common Slavic *kъto* ("who") and *čьto* ("what") are historically one paradigm (masc./fem. vs. neuter reflexes of the same interrogative), and both keep real, citable irregularities rather than leveling to a uniform stem the way modern standard Croatian did.

| | Nominative/Accusative | Genitive | Dative | Instrumental |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **who** | [[../Words/кто\|кто]] | [[../Words/кога\|кога]] | [[../Words/кому\|кому]] | [[../Words/цем\|цем]] |
| **what** | [[../Words/кај\|кај]] | [[../Words/чега\|чега]] | [[../Words/чему\|чему]] | [[../Words/чим\|чим]] |

**"Who"'s irregularity: an unleveled archaic instrumental.** Common Slavic *cěmь* is a genuinely different root from the ко- stem carrying every other oblique case (*kogo, *komu) — real, attested suppletion, not an invented one. Modern Croatian regularized this away to a uniform "kim." **Sisician keeps the archaism**, по [[../Words/цем|цем]]'s own page — the same "one cell resists leveling" texture English keeps in *who*/*whom*, deliberately chosen over the regularized modern outcome.

**"What"'s irregularity: a borrowed oblique paradigm.** [[../Words/кај|кај]] itself is the already-decided defining Kajkavian marker (see [[../Sources/Late Common Slavic|Late Common Slavic]] § Dialect Zone) — a frozen, non-declining substantivized form that never developed its own case paradigm. Its oblique cells are borrowed wholesale from the wider Slavic *čьto* paradigm instead, matching real modern Croatian *šta*'s own full oblique forms (чега/чему/чим). Two suppletive words, two unrelated *reasons* for the suppletion.

## Which: One Root, Two Words, No Collision

**Real Croatian *koji* and Kajkavian *kaj* share the exact same Proto-Slavic root, *kъjь*** (confirmed via Wiktionary) — but they don't collide, because *koji* reflects further suffixation Kajkavian's frozen *kaj* never underwent. Bare *kъjь* alone regularly gives exactly кај (Havlík's Law: final jь's ь is weak and drops, non-final ъ is strong and vocalizes to a — the identical mechanism already behind [[../Words/тај|тај]]/[[../Words/овај|овај]]). Real modern *koji*'s own further material means it isn't simply that bare reflex substantivized — it's a fully declining relative/interrogative adjective, the same "declining word" shape already established for the demonstrative pronouns.

- **[[../Words/који|који]]** — native, adjective-like (matches real *koji* letter-for-letter; oblique paradigm not built yet, an open item).
- **[[../Words/куи|куи]]** — a Latinate register doublet, direct from Latin **QUI** (the relative/interrogative pronoun), entering not through spoken Dalmatian but through the **written legal/notarial Latin register** already established on [[../Culture/Sociolinguistic History|Culture/Sociolinguistic History]] ("Latin survives only in diplomatic/foreign-trade/family-document contexts"). Sisician's phonology has no labialized-consonant series, so Latin's /kw/ onset adapts letter-by-letter as **ку-**, giving куи directly.

## Where, Whither, Whence: The Old Case System, Fossilized Into the Words Themselves

**This is the answer to "should case affect these words" — it already does, just not as a live paradigm.** Building a synchronic case-declension onto "where" would be *more* regular than any real Slavic language actually is. Instead:

- **[[../Words/кде|кде]]** ("where") — *kъde* is literally *interrogative stem + an ancient PIE locative suffix (\*-dʰe)*, per Wiktionary — case is baked into the word's own etymology, fossilized and opaque, the same way English "where" itself fossilizes an old instrumental. **Kept k-initial deliberately** (per the user's explicit request), rather than the k→g voicing shift real Croatian/Russian *gdje*/*где* both independently show.
- **[[../Words/камо|камо]]** ("whither") — *kamo*'s own *-mo* is the same kind of fossilized ancient directional suffix (real Slavic *tamo* "thither" shows the identical suffix opposing *tu* "here," the same relationship камо has to кде).
- **[[../Words/абкле|абкле]]** ("whence") — the one place case is **live and visible, not fossilized**, and it survived a real lexical replacement rather than being a fresh invention. Native Common Slavic built "whence" compositionally: **од** ("from") regularly governs genitive in the ordinary living case system, so the original form was од + an oblique-shaped element related to [[../Words/кде|кде]]'s own material, matching real Croatian *odakle*'s own makeup — **but that native form is no longer independently attested** — Latin **AB** fully displaces it, the same generalizing trajectory [[../Words/ној|ној]] took against native девет. The replacement is a genuine hybrid calque, not a clean substitution — Latin **AB** took over the preposition slot while the native, genitive-shaped **-кле** stem survived unchanged underneath it, meaning the *case marking itself* outlived the word's own prefix. A new subtype of [[../Etymology/Sisician Sound Changes|Sisician Sound Changes]]'s § Prestige-Driven Lexically-Specific Developments, distinct from дешет's reshaping, ној's plain replacement, or нув's convergence. Regressive devoicing (б before the voiceless к) gives pronunciation [apˈkle] even though written абкле — the same Sava-corridor devoicing pattern already cited on [[../Sources/Late Common Slavic|Late Common Slavic]] (*v kući → f kući*).

**This also resolves [[Nominal Morphology]]'s own long-flagged open item** ("exact assignment [of preposition→case after the locative's loss] isn't decided yet"): **location and goal both land on preposition+accusative** (they already shared accusative even under the old seven-case system — "in the house" and "into the house" only differed by locative vs. accusative, and once locative dies they simply merge); **source alone stays preposition+genitive**, unchanged, since genitive-after-*od* was never locative's territory to begin with. Кде/камо being fossilized rather than live case forms doesn't contradict this — the *live* system this resolves is for ordinary nouns, and абкле is where it happens to surface transparently in the interrogative set too, even after its own preposition got replaced.

## Open Items

- Који's own oblique case paradigm (gen./dat./instr., matching the demonstrative-pronoun-style declension its adjectival shape implies) isn't built — only the citation form.
- Whether куи ever appears outside strictly written/legal register, or how a speaker would code-switch into it, isn't worked out.
- **Why абкле generalized into ordinary speech while куи stayed confined to the legal register** — both started in the same channel; only one fully displaced its native counterpart. Not yet given its own historical account, flagged honestly as an open question rather than an invented asymmetry.
- No dedicated word for "whatever/whoever"-type indefinite-extended interrogatives yet.
