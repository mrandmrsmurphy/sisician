# Relative Clauses

Resolves the relative-clause thread flagged in the October evaluation: the relativizer, its full paradigm, the clause's syntax, and the register split. See [[Interrogatives]] for the interrogative half of који's life (and for куи), [[Clitic Ordering]] for clitic placement inside relative clauses, [[Definiteness]] for the article, and [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page.

## The Relativizer: који

**који introduces a relative clause and agrees with its antecedent in gender and number — while its case comes from its role inside the relative clause**, the standard Slavic arrangement (real BCS *koji* is confirmed via Wiktionary as both interrogative and relative, with an indefinite sense besides). Three working consequences:

- **Agreement follows the antecedent, including the neuter merger**: **ел сунце који сија** "the sun, which shines" — the relative is masculine, like the article ел and the pronoun он, because сунце is an old neuter now taking masculine agreement. An old-neuter-*plural* antecedent instead takes the feminine plural: **небеса које** "heavens which."
- **Case follows the clause's role, not the antecedent's**: the paradigm below shows the full grid; the relative's case is whatever its own clause job requires (subject → nominative; object → accusative; object of a preposition → that preposition's case).
- **Animate/inanimate split applies**, the general Slavic rule from [[../Words/њега|њега]]/[[../Words/дом|дом]]: animate masculine accusative takes the genitive shape (**даожник кога** "the debtor whom…"), inanimate takes the nominative shape (**ел хлеб који је дао** "the bread which he gave" — accusative in function, nominative in shape).

## The Paradigm

| Case | M. sg. | F. sg. | M. pl. | F. pl. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nominative | који | која | који | које |
| Genitive | **кога** / **којега** | које | којих | којих |
| Dative | **кому** / **којему** | којој | којим | којим |
| Accusative | кога (anim.) / који (inan.) | коју | којих (anim.) / које (inan.) | које |
| Instrumental | којим | којом | којим | којим |

**The short/long doublet is a register split, copied from real BCS** (*koga~kojega*, *komu~kojemu*): the short forms **кога/кому** are the colloquial default; the long forms **којега/којему** are the careful/written register. The long forms are built on the кој- stem with the soft adjective endings -ега/-ему ([[Adjective Agreement]]'s soft declension), making them that ending pair's first lexical examples. The short forms use the bare **к- root** with the demonstrative ending family — the same formation as [[../Words/тога|тога]]/[[../Words/тому|тому]], which is why they land on кто's own кога/кому: one root, one form, two paradigms, the documented-homograph category already accepted for [[../Words/ти|ти]]/[[../Words/те|те]]. Plural syncretisms mirror the demonstratives exactly (које = f.sg. gen. + f.pl. nom./acc.; којим = m.sg. instr. + plural dat./instr. both genders, the [[../Words/тим|тим]]-parallel three-way syncretism).

## Clause Shape

- **Position**: the relative clause follows its antecedent, and the article stays on the antecedent: **ел дом који доје** "the house which comes," not a split or postposed arrangement.
- **Clitics anchor to који**: as the clause's first constituent, који takes the second-position cluster — **даожник који му га је дао** "the debtor who gave it to him." [[Clitic Ordering]]'s existing template handles the relative clause with no new rules.
- **Verb agreement** is with the antecedent: **ел гужва која кричи** "the crowd, which is shouting" — feminine singular, even though the crowd is notionally many people.
- **Preposition + који** carries oblique roles: **ел дом из којега доје** "the house from which he comes" (из + genitive, the long register form; colloquial *из кога*). у/на + instrumental works identically: **у којим** "in which (m.)."
- **Restrictive and non-restrictive** both use који; writing sets the non-restrictive off with a comma, as real BCS.
- **The clause is otherwise an ordinary clause** — the three-way evidential past applies inside it like anywhere else: **ел хлеб који је дао** (conservative) / **који дјао** (witnessed) / **који дао** (hearsay) "the bread which he gave."

## Whose: чији

**чији** "whose" — interrogative and relative alike — from Proto-Slavic **\*čьjь** ([Wiktionary чији](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D1%87%D0%B8%D1%98%D0%B8), fetched, with real relative examples), the ч-root twin of који's к-root. It agrees with the **possessed** noun in gender, number, and case; the possessor relation to the antecedent is genitive in force: **даожник чији дом је велик** "the debtor whose house is big" (чији masculine singular, agreeing with дом). The paradigm mirrors који's exactly, including the short/long doublet — and the short obliques are **чега/чему**, the very forms [[кај]]'s paradigm borrowed: same ч- root, same formation, one oblique family shared by "what" and "whose."

| Case | M. sg. | F. sg. | M. pl. | F. pl. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nominative | чији | чија | чији | чије |
| Genitive | **чега** / **чијега** | чије | чијих | чијих |
| Dative | **чему** / **чијему** | чијој | чијим | чијим |
| Accusative | чега (anim.) / чији (inan.) | чију | чијих (anim.) / чије (inan.) | чије |
| Instrumental | чијим | чијом | чијим | чијим |

Standalone interrogative: **Чији је дом?** "Whose house is it?"

## The Register Split

Three words, three niches — this resolves the open distribution questions on [[Interrogatives]] § Open Items:

| Word | Niche |
|---|---|
| [[../Words/који\|који]] | the productive relativizer/interrogative, every register |
| [[../Words/куи\|куи]] | the written legal/notarial Latin register only — a citation-form borrowing of Latin QUI, **indeclinable** (it never developed a Sisician paradigm, matching real legal Latin's frozen citation forms) |
| [[../Words/иже\|иже]] | the liturgical fossil, [[../Texts/Pater Noster\|Pater Noster]] only, preserved against the productive system |

A modern-register speaker recasting the prayer's opening says **Отче наш, који си на небесима** — који productive, иже fossil, and the two never cross.

## What This Doesn't License

- **No resumptive pronouns**: the relative pronoun itself carries the clause role; a doubled pronoun inside the clause ("the man who I gave it **to him**") is ungrammatical, as in real Slavic.
- **No preposition stranding**: English "the house which I live **in**" has no Sisician counterpart; only preposition + који (**из којега доје**), as in real Slavic.
- **The indefinite senses are not built yet**: real BCS који/чији also mean "some"/"anyone's" (Wiktionary's derived terms: *nȅkoji*, *bilo čiji*…), the same standing open status as the "whatever/whoever" item on [[Interrogatives]].
