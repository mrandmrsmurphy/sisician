# Pater Noster (Отче наш)

The Lord's Prayer in Sisician — the vault's first full liturgical text, and the first real workout for [[../Etymology/Semantic Domains|Semantic Domains]]' Layer III ("Slavonic/Greek for heaven"). See [[../Sisician|Sisician]] for the hub page, [[../Culture/Religion|Culture/Religion]] for the Byzantine-rite setting this belongs to.

**Base text: the Slavonic liturgical version** (chosen over the Latin Vulgate and the Greek), per the 1042 Byzantine turn and the Layer III channel. The standard Church Slavonic text, presented from knowledge (it is the fixed liturgical form, not freshly fetched this session — flagged):

> Отче нашъ, иже еси на небесѣхъ. да свѧтитъ сѧ имѧ твоѥ. да придетъ цѣсарьствиѥ твоѥ. да будетъ волѧ твоѧ, ѧко на небеси и на земли. хлѣбъ нашъ насѫщьнъıи даждь намъ дьньсъ. и отпусти намъ дългы нашѧ, ѧко и мы отпущаемъ дължьникомъ нашимъ. и не въведи насъ въ напасть, но избави ны отъ лѫкаваго.

## The Translation

> **Отче наш, иже си на небесима.**
> **да се свети име твој.**
> **да доје царство твој.**
> **да буде волја твоја, како на небом и на земљом.**
> **хлеб наш насуштни дај нам данас.**
> **и отпусти нам даоге наши, како и ми одпуштамо даожницима наши.**
> **и не веди нас у напаст, но избави нас од зла.**

Capitalization follows the Slavonic's own liturgical layout (only Отче capitalized). Line-by-line notes below; every word's full derivation is on its own `Words/` page.

## Line by Line

**1. Отче наш, иже си на небесима.** — "Father our, who art in the heavens."
- **Отче** — vocative fossil of [[../Words/отац|отац]], per [[../Grammar/Nominal Morphology|Nominal Morphology]]'s vocative-fossil decision; the exact parallel of real BCS *Oče naš*.
- **иже** — the Church Slavonic relative, kept as a **liturgical fossil** (see § The Prayer as a Fossil Register). It is *not* the productive relative; that role belongs to [[../Words/који|који]], whose paradigm and the full relativization strategy are now built on [[../Grammar/Relative Clauses|Grammar/Relative Clauses]] — иже is the fossil the modern system left behind.
- **си** — 2sg clitic of [[../Words/бити|бити]], second position after иже, per [[../Grammar/Clitic Ordering|Clitic Ordering]].
- **на небесима** — plural "heavens," matching the Slavonic's own plural (на небесѣхъ); на + **instrumental** for static location, the Latin calque of [[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]] § у and на.

**2. да се свети име твој.** — "let be-hallowed the name thy."
- **да** — frozen liturgical jussive (the Slavonic's own да); the productive vernacular jussive is нека ([[../Grammar/Imperative|Imperative]]).
- **се** — reflexive clitic, second position after да, slot 5 of the clitic template. [[../Words/светити|светити]] се = "to be hallowed."
- **име твој** — the neuter merger on display: име is an old neuter, so it takes **masculine** singular agreement (твој), where BCS and the Slavonic both have neuter *твоје*. One of the small places the prayer sounds distinctly Sisician.

**3. да доје царство твој.** — "let come the kingdom thy."
- **доје** — 3sg of [[../Words/дочи|дочи]] (e-type), perfective jussive present, matching the Slavonic's да придетъ. The form is the vault's own baseline merger (ć→č, đ→j) applied to the BCS доћи/дође compound; the *dojde*-type alternative is flagged on the word page.
- **царство** — the Church Slavonic loan (цѣсарьствиѥ), chosen over native \*краљевство for the prayer: imperial, Byzantine-flavored, and it leaves краљевство free for secular kingdoms. Recorded as a deliberate lexical split.
- **твој** — masculine again (царство is an old neuter).

**4. да буде волја твоја, како на небом и на земљом.** — "let be the will thy, as in heaven and on earth."
- **буде** — 3sg of the будем paradigm ([[../Grammar/Biti (To Be)|Biti (To Be)]]), already built long before this exercise: the prayer's most famous line was one noun and one possessive away from done.
- **волја твоја** — genuinely feminine (волја is not an old neuter), so the possessive shows its f. sg. form.
- **како** — "as," the interrogative "how" extended to the conjunction use (real Russian *как* covers both; the Slavonic's ѧко). Noted on [[../Words/како|како]]'s page.
- **на небом и на земљом** — singular here, matching the Slavonic's own alternation (plural in v. 1, singular in v. 4 — kept, not leveled). Both instrumentals after на: the calque's second and third showcase.

**5. хлеб наш насуштни дај нам данас.** — "bread our daily give us today."
- **Word order**: object fronted, the Slavonic's own order (хлѣбъ нашъ насѫщьнъıи даждь намъ дьньсъ) — the marked, erudite order per [[../Grammar/Word Order and Register|Word Order and Register]], kept for the liturgical register rather than normalized to SVO.
- **дај нам** — imperative of [[../Words/дати|дати]] (the -ј allomorph) + dative clitic in second position, the exact shape дати's own page established (дам ти га → дај нам).
- **насуштни** — the Church Slavonic loan (насѫщьнъıи), the ἐπιούσιος calque. The design conversation approved "насущни"; the modern inventory has no щ, so the loan nativizes щ→шт (the real Serbian treatment of Slavonic щ, свѧщеникъ → свештеник): **насуштни**. Flagged on [[../Words/насуштни|the word page]].
- **данас** — "today," bare adverb, as the Slavonic's дьньсъ is bare.

**6. и отпусти нам даоге наши, како и ми одпуштамо даожницима наши.** — "and forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors."
- **отпусти** — perfective imperative of [[../Words/одпустити|одпустити]] (one act of forgiveness requested); **одпуштамо** — 1pl imperfective of [[../Words/одпуштати|одпуштати]] (habitual "as we forgive"). The vault's first perfective/imperfective pair used together in one text.
- **даоге** — acc. pl. inanimate of [[../Words/даог|даог]] "debt" (second sense on that page), the short plural variant — real BCS's own prayer says *duge* where the regular paradigm has *dugove*, and Sisician mirrors the doublet. The noun is a perfect homograph of даог "long" (both from \*dъlgъ/\*dьlgъ through the identical jer-fall + L-vocalization chain), reproducing real BCS's *dug*/*dug* homophony by independent derivation.
- **даожницима наши** — dative plural + caseless possessive. The Slavonic has dative-marked *нашим*; Sisician's possessives are caseless per the decision on [[../Grammar/Possessive Pronouns|Grammar/Possessive Pronouns]] (flagged there as reversible — if reversed, this becomes **даожницима нашим**).
- "Debts," not "sins" — the Slavonic (дългы) and Latin (*debita*) both say debts; "sins" enters only via the later liturgical addition, not translated here.

**7. и не веди нас у напаст, но избави нас од зла.** — "and lead not us into temptation, but deliver us from evil."
- **не веди** — negative + synthetic imperative, a **liturgical fossil**: the productive vernacular negative imperative is немој + да + present ([[../Grammar/Imperative|Imperative]]). The Slavonic has не въведи, and real BCS's own prayer preserves *ne vodi nas* against the productive немој rule — the exact real-world parallel for keeping it.
- **веди** — imperative of [[../Words/вести|вести]] (the reconstruction's own \*vedi). The Slavonic's perfective (въведи) is followed, not the BCS prayer's imperfective *vodi* (водити, unbuilt — noted on the word page).
- **у напаст** — у + accusative for motion into ([[../Grammar/Case Government|Case Government]]); напаст is feminine i-stem, accusative = nominative.
- **но** — "but," the Slavonic's own word; whether it's the general vernacular "but" or liturgical-flavored is an open question (recorded on [[../Words/но|the word page]]).
- **од зла** — од + genitive "from evil." The Slavonic's more colorful лѫкаваго ("the crafty one") is simplified to plain [[../Words/зло|зло]], matching the Latin *a malo* — a small, deliberate simplification.

## The Prayer as a Fossil Register

Three of the prayer's constructions are **archaisms preserved against the productive grammar**, kept because that is what real liturgical texts do:

| Fossil in the prayer | Productive modern system |
|---|---|
| **иже** "who" (relative) | [[../Words/који\|који]] — the modern relative system ([[../Grammar/Relative Clauses\|Grammar/Relative Clauses]]) |
| **да** + present (jussive) | **нека** + present ([[../Grammar/Imperative\|Imperative]]) |
| **не** + synthetic imperative | **немој** + да + present ([[../Grammar/Imperative\|Imperative]]) |

The real-world warrant is exact: the living BCS prayer itself preserves *Oče naš* (a vocative fossil) and *ne vodi nas u napast* (не + imperative, against the productive *nemoj* rule) — liturgical language is a museum, and Sisician's prayer is built as one deliberately. None of these three fossils licenses using иже/да-jussive/не+imperative in ordinary modern text.

## What This Exercised

The prayer turned out to be a systems check for most of the vault's signature machinery:

- **The у/на + instrumental Latin calque** — на небесима / на небом / на земљом, the most famous sentence in the world showing off the vault's most distinctive prepositional decision.
- **The vocative fossil** (Отче), **the neuter merger** (име твој, царство твој), **the clitic template** (си, се, нам all second-position; дај нам = дати's own pattern).
- **L-vocalization** (даог "debt," даожник) and **intervocalic lenition** (небо /ˈnɛβo/, волја /ˈβoʎa/, његов /ˈɲeɣoβ/).
- **Caseless adjectives and possessives** (насуштни, наши) — the 1800s system doing its job.
- **An aspect pair in one text** (одпусти pf. / одпуштамо impf.).
- **No definite article anywhere** — the prayer's nouns are vocative, possessed, or abstract, so definiteness never arises. Worth stating explicitly, since the article is otherwise pervasive in modern text.

## Open Items

- **краљевство vs. царство** — the secular/divine kingdom split is decided in principle (царство = the prayer's word) but краљевство itself is unbuilt.
- **Vernacular "but"** — но vs. a него-type alternative, distribution open.
- **The possessive-case decision** — reversible; see [[../Grammar/Possessive Pronouns|Grammar/Possessive Pronouns]] § The Caseless Decision.
- **насуштни's m.sg. = m.pl. syncretism** — flagged on the word page.
- **Possessive + article co-occurrence** (ел наш дом?) — undecided.
- **"Amen"** — the Slavonic text proper ends without it; not coined.
